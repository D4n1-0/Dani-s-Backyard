# Hallucigenia Entity

## Summary
An ancient parasitic organism — the true source of all Titan powers — bonded to Ymir Fritz's spine ~1,800 years ago, granting regenerative immortality and the ability to manifest the Titan form.

## Details
Resembling the Cambrian _Hallucigenia sparsa_, the entity possesses a rudimentary imperative to survive and multiply. Its bond with Ymir's bloodline perpetuates a two-millennia cycle of violence, cannibalism, and the 13-year Curse — ensuring continuous replication through the Paths. It is the root cause of the Titan curse and the ultimate target for any force seeking to permanently end the Titan era.

## Context
Unknown to the modern world. Unknown even to most of the AOT world. The entity exists as the deepest secret of the Founding Titan — and the key to whether the Titan curse can ever be broken.
