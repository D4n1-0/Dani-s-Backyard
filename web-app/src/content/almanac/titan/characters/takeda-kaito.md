# Takeda Kaito

## Overview
Thirty-four years old. Career diplomat, Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs, seconded to CATF from the MOFA European Affairs desk. Served as Deputy Protocol Officer on the Odiha contact mission under [[Satomi Ishihara|Dr. Ishihara]]. Handled documentation, scheduling, and secondary diplomatic contacts while Ishihara conducted the primary negotiation with Admiral [[Konrad Verner]].

## Background
Joined MOFA at twenty-three. Postings in Brussels and Geneva before volunteering for the CATF secondment — a decision his superiors questioned and he could not explain except to say that the Gate was the most important thing that had ever happened to Japanese foreign policy and he wanted to be there when it opened. Spent the Odiha mission confined to the [[CATF Envoy|Envoy]] during the contact, monitoring the harbour via the ship's communications suite and processing the admiral's administrative signals. His notes from the mission formed the basis of the formal after-action report submitted to the Quadripartite Committee.

## Status
Returned to Grande Terre. Awaiting next assignment.
