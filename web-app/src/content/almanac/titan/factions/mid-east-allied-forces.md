# Mid-East Allied Forces

## Overview
A coalition of nations that declared war on the [[Marleyan Empire]] following Marley's catastrophic loss of the Colossal and Female Titans on Paradis in Year 850. Sensing critical vulnerability, the Mid-East Allies seek to break Marley's global dominance. Their forces possess the most advanced conventional military technology in the AOT world — including naval supremacy and 100mm anti-titan artillery capable of piercing Armored Titan plating.

## Structure
- Coalition command with rotating leadership among member states
- Advanced naval fleets — dominant in contested waters
- Fortified land positions (e.g., Fort Slava)
- Armoured trains mounting 100mm anti-titan guns

## Assets
- Superior conventional artillery and naval technology
- Fortified defensive positions
- No Titan Shifters — this is their critical strategic gap, compensated by anti-titan weaponry

## Relationships
- [[Marleyan Empire]] — active war (Year 850–854)
- [[Eldian Military|Paradis]] — unaware of each other directly; the Mid-East war buys Paradis time
- [[Hizuru]] — neutral; Hizuru deals with all sides opportunistically
