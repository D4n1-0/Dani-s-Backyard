# Sundered Cosmology

## Gaethon

The world of the Sundered Realms is **Gaethon** — an Earth-like planet whose landmasses splinter into three major continents: **[[Occidens]]** (the sunset expanse), **[[Oriens]]** (the dawn cradle), and **[[Meridies]]** (the scorched south), plus countless archipelagos. Together they constitute nine-tenths of the planet's dry surface. The remaining tenth is **[[Kaelen]]**, the Victor Island, alone in the central **[[Tethyan Ocean]]**.

## Kaelen

Kaelen is a continent-island of fourteen million square kilometres — comparable to Earth's Antarctica in scale — stretching from temperate fjords in its north to equatorial mangroves in its south. It is the sole source of industrial-era technology on Gaethon, preserved and advanced behind an impregnable sea-wall since the [[War of Convergence]] three centuries ago.

At Kaelen's core stand the **[[The Silent Spires|Silent Spires]]**: eight immense ballistic missile stations arranged in a precise geometric ring. A conventional warhead from any Spire can reach any point on Gaethon within an hour. The Spires, combined with orbital [[Eyes of Heaven|satellites]], the [[Silent Chorus|drone fleet]], and the enigmatic [[The Observers|Observers]], form a surveillance-and-strike triad that enforces the [[Kaelen Mandate]].

## Technological Asymmetry

The fundamental asymmetry of the Sundered World is not moral or political but technological. Kaelen possesses a complete industrial-scientific continuum — satellite networks, transistor computation, precision-guided munitions, automated manufacturing — while the Scattered Realms are frozen at approximately 1935-era technology, with critical gaps: no radar, no jet propulsion, no nuclear science, no transistors. The Mandate does not merely ban the *use* of advanced technology; it bans the *knowledge* of advanced technology. Pure physics is heresy.

## The Emptiness Question

Kaelen emits electromagnetic murmurs — encrypted data-chatter, fragments of alien music, navigational beacons — but no emissary has departed its shores since the Mandate was broadcast. The [[The Signal Seekers|Signal Seekers]]' recordings suggest patterns too complex to be random static yet too repetitive to be a living civilisation. This has spawned multiple theories:

- **The Dead Hand Hypothesis:** Kaelen's inhabitants perished long ago; the Mandate is maintained by an automated system executing its last programmed directive.
- **The Watcher Hypothesis:** Kaelen is inhabited but has chosen total isolation as a deliberate strategy — a post-human society that no longer communicates in ways recognisable to Gaethon's inhabitants.
- **The Test Hypothesis:** The Mandate is a developmental checkpoint. When the Scattered Realms reach a certain level of maturity — or answer the [[The Kaelic Melody|Kaelic Melody]] correctly — the cage will open.

None of these has been proven. None has been disproven.
