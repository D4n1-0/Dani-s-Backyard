# Freedom of Navigation Operation

## Summary
A military operation in which a navy — typically the United States Navy — asserts navigational rights under international law by transiting through waters claimed by another state without requesting permission. The [[United States Seventh Fleet]] announced a FONOP within hours of the Gate's discovery, sending an Arleigh Burke-class destroyer toward the Yellow Sea.

## Details
The announcement of a FONOP sends both a tactical message (a ship is coming) and a diplomatic message (the US will not be excluded from the Gate). The timing — 48 hours from announcement to arrival — creates a deadline for Seoul and Beijing to negotiate their own framework before American forces are on scene. In the [[Chapter 02 – The Long Morning|Seoul crisis meeting]], Minister [[Kim Dong-wook]] welcomes the announcement as allied support; Admiral [[Yoon Seok-jin|Yoon]] warns that a three-navy convergence raises the risk of miscalculation.
