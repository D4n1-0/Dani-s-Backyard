# National Security Office (ROK)

## Overview
The senior national security advisory body to the President of the Republic of Korea. Coordinates crisis response across defence, diplomacy, intelligence, and domestic security agencies. Staffed by a mix of career diplomats, military officers on secondment, and intelligence analysts. The NSO is the nerve centre for all major national security decisions.

## Structure
- Director of National Security (President's principal advisor)
- Deputy Directors for: Crisis Management, Foreign Policy, Defence Policy, Intelligence
- Crisis Management Centre (24/7 operations floor)

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- [[Lee Jun-ho]], Senior Secretary for Crisis Management, is the lead coordinator for the Gate crisis.

## Relationships
- [[Ministry of National Defense (ROK)]] — coordinates military response
- [[Central Foreign Affairs Commission (PRC)]] — counterpart; direct hotline connects the two offices
- [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (ROK)]] — coordinates diplomatic response
