# Marcus Rivera

## Overview
Captain, United States Navy Civil Engineer Corps. Forty-four years old. Built airstrips in Afghanistan and piers in the Philippines. Commanding officer of the [[Alfred Faure Forward Operating Base]] — the fastest expeditionary base build in US Navy history.

Views the AOT world the way a carpenter views uncut timber: as a project. Processes the ontological shock of standing on another Earth by filing it under "construction challenge" and continuing to work. His temperament is steady, his decisions are fast, and his loyalty is to the build — the base must stand, whatever the metaphysics of its location.
