# United States

## Overview
The United States of America in the modern timeline — the world's preeminent military and economic power, NATO's largest contributor, and the UK's closest ally. Briefed on the Anomaly via the Five Eyes intelligence-sharing arrangement, the US under [[Robert Cole|President Cole]] pushes for immediate joint military command and a direct presidential role in portal affairs.

## Position on the Anomaly
The US views the portal primarily as a *defence* problem. President Cole's position: the Anomaly represents an interdimensional threat of unknown capability within the NATO area of responsibility. A unified command structure under a Supreme Allied Commander — with American leadership — is the minimum acceptable response. The President has threatened to act unilaterally if the NATO framework stalls.

This position is driven by genuine fear of the [[Heretic Legion]] threat — Cole has read the intelligence assessments and concluded that no existing military doctrine covers what might come through the portal. But it is also driven by the American strategic calculus: whoever controls the portal controls the future.
