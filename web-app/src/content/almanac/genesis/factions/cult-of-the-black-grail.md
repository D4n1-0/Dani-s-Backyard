# Cult of the Black Grail

## Overview
A Heretic faction devoted to the Black Grail — a profane anti-sacrament served by Beelzebub during the Corpse Wars (1346–1429). The Cult deploys Grail-spores as biological weapons, transforming victims into vessels of corruption. They are the most feared and reviled of the Heretic Legions.

## Structure
Servants of Beelzebub, one of the Tyrants of Hell. The Cult operates through a mobile fleet of Grail-infected vessels, spreading spores across coastal and riverine settlements.

## Assets
- Grail-spore weaponry
- Infected host vessels
- Grail-fleet (naval assets)
- Biological corruption capabilities
