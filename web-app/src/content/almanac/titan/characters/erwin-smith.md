# Erwin Smith

## Description
Thirteenth Commander of the Survey Corps. Tall, blond, charismatic, ruthless. Died at the Battle of Shiganshina leading a suicide charge against the Beast Titan to give Levi an opening. His final act was the raising of his remaining arm — "My soldiers, rage!" — before being torn apart by Zeke's barrage. Posthumous influence on the Survey Corps is absolute.

## Background
Driven since childhood by a single question: did his father's theory about memory manipulation by the monarchy prove the outside world existed? Erwin's entire career — every expedition, every death, every sacrifice — was bent toward reaching Grisha's basement and proving his father right. He succeeded and died before seeing it.

## Relationships
- [[Levi Ackerman]] — Erwin's most trusted soldier; Levi chose to let Erwin die rather than bring him back to suffer more
- [[Hange Zoë]] — successor
- [[Armin Arlert]] — Erwin died so Armin could live; the weight of this is Armin's burden

## Current Status
Dead. Buried at Shiganshina. His ghost haunts every decision the Survey Corps makes going forward.
