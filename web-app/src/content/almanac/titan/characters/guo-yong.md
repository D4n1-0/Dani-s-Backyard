# Guo Yong

## Description
Fifty-one years old. Assistant Minister of Foreign Affairs for Asian Affairs. Career diplomat, posted in Washington D.C., Brussels, and Pyongyang before returning to Beijing. Speaks fluent English with a mid-Atlantic accent picked up during seven years at the Chinese Embassy on Connecticut Avenue. Thin, urbane, never raises his voice because he has never needed to. Understands the American mind better than anyone else in the room — and fears what the American military will do when it arrives.

## Background
Graduate of the China Foreign Affairs University. Junior diplomat during the 2010s trade war, senior diplomat during the Taiwan strait crisis of the 2020s. Appointed Assistant Minister for Asian Affairs two years ago. Has been in the room for more crises than anyone else in Zheng's meeting — and knows this one is different.

## Relationships
- [[Zheng Weimin]] — respects Zheng's political instincts; acts as his diplomatic counterweight to Zhang's military pressure
- Vice Admiral [[Zhang Haifeng]] — respectful but often at odds; Guo believes diplomacy wins wars that ships start

## Current Status
In Beijing, urging calibrated restraint and recommending a backchannel to Seoul before the Americans arrive. His fear: a miscalculation at the Gate site that triggers escalation neither side wants.
