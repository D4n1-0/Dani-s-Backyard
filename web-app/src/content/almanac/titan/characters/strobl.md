# Strobl

## Overview
Admiral of the Marleyan Home Fleet. Leader of the military faction within the General Staff. A hardliner who believes the empire should have seized the CATF ships the day they arrived at Odiha — and who has been trying to undo the MOU ever since. His defining trait: he is losing, and he does not know how to lose.

## Background
Commanded the Home Fleet for a decade. Built his career on the assumption that Marley was the world's only power and that any challenge to that assumption was a threat to be destroyed. Authorised the [[Langsdorff|Captain Langsdorff]] patrol order that sent the [[Steinadler]] into the construction channel — an operation that failed when the USS *Gerald R. Ford* CSG appeared on the horizon. Has not recovered from the humiliation.
