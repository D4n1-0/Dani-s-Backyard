# Pyricism

## Summary

Pyricism is the state religion of the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]], teaching that the [[War of Convergence]] was divine chastisement for human hubris and that [[Kaelen]] is the instrument of an angry God — making the [[Kaelen Mandate|Mandate]]'s articles holy scripture and its enforcement a sacred duty.

## Details

### Core Doctrine

Pyricism's theology rests on three tenets:

1. **The Sin of Knowledge:** Humanity once reached too far, seeking to understand and control the fundamental forces of the universe. This was the primordial sin — not pride, but *unsanctioned comprehension*. The War of Convergence was the divine response.

2. **Kaelen as Instrument:** Kaelen is not God, nor does it speak for God. It is a tool wielded by the divine — a "burning sword" placed at the centre of the world to enforce limits. Kaelen's inhabitants may not even know they serve this function.

3. **The Mandate as Scripture:** Each article of the Mandate is a revealed truth. To violate the Edict is to sin. To sin is to invite fire. The destruction of the [[The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr|Sunken Kingdom of Myrr]] and the vitrification of [[Keth]] are cited as evidence: God does not negotiate.

### Practices

- **Inquisition:** The Flame's inquisitors roam beyond the Theocracy's borders, hunting "heretics" — scientists dabbling in radar-like research, engineers sketching monoplane wings, anyone who whispers forbidden words. They arrive with pro forma lists and depart leaving ashes.
- **Ritual Burnings:** Heretics are executed by fire, symbolically returning their forbidden knowledge to the flame that birthed the universe. [[The Observers|Kaelen Observers]] have been witnessed at multiple Inquisition burnings, watching in stillness — interpreted by the faithful as divine endorsement.
- **The Census as Sacrament:** Population counting is a religious duty. Exceeding the Mandate's million-soul cap is not merely illegal but blasphemous.

### Theological Debates

The Synod of Intercessors is divided on several questions:

- **The Empty Throne:** Does Kaelen still house human inhabitants, or is it an abandoned divine mechanism? If the latter, does this make the Mandate *more* sacred (a direct relic of divine action) or *less* (an unmanned program running past its intended span)?
- **The End of the Edict:** Some theologians whisper that God may one day lift the Mandate — and that the Pyric Church must be prepared to govern a freed humanity. Others consider this the ultimate heresy.

## Context

To the Scattered Realms, Pyricism is simultaneously a comfort and a terror. Its doctrine gives meaning to suffering — the Mandate has a *purpose* — but its Inquisition makes the Theocracy the most aggressive internal enforcer of Kaelen's will. The [[Technate of Vorr]] considers Pyricism a collaborator faith. The [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] tolerate it diplomatically while working to limit Inquisition access to their territories.
