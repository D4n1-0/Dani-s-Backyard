# Garte

## Description

*Manager of the Whirling-in-Rags. Perpetually aggrieved, perpetually present. His face has settled into a mask of permanent grievance so complete it has become a personality. Full name Garte Theodorus Olivier, which he was once proud of. Wipes the same glass over and over — a Sisyphean act of hospitality in a town that has forgotten what hospitality means.*

## Background

*Has managed the Whirling-in-Rags through the strike, the Tribunal, and the occupation. Had to scrub blood off his floorboards after the Hardie Boys and Krenel's mercenaries shot each other in front of his establishment. The incident has not improved his disposition.*
