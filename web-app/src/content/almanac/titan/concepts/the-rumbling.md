# The Rumbling

## Summary
The apocalyptic activation of the millions of Colossal Titans embedded within Paradis's three concentric Walls — marching across the earth, flattening all life, ecosystems, and civilisation outside the island.

## Details
Each Wall (Maria, Rose, Sina) is composed of countless Colossal Titans, hardened into immobility by the Founding Titan's power. When the Founder commands them to awaken — the Rumbling — they break free and advance in all directions, generating enough thermal energy to incinerate everything in their path and trample the surface of the world flat. The Rumbling is the ultimate deterrent of Paradis Island — and the ultimate horror of the AOT world.

## Context
The modern world's assessment of the Rumbling will determine everything. Is it a credible threat? A bluff? Can Titan Shifters in human form be targeted before they trigger it? The moral calculus: to prevent omnicide, do you pre-emptively destroy Paradis? Or do you negotiate with a traumatised 15-year-old who holds the trigger?
