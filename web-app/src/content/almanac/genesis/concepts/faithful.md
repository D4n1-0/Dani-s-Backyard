# Faithful

## Summary
The collective term for the people, nations, and institutions of [[Trench Crusade Earth]] who serve the Unified Church and oppose the [[Heretic Legion]]. Every Faithful soul bears the mark of divine grace — tangible, detectable, and opposed to the infernal taint carried by the Heretics. They have fought an eight-hundred-year holy war against the literal forces of Hell, and they believe — with absolute certainty — that God intervenes visibly in military and civil affairs.

## Key Faithful Institutions
- [[Crown of England]] — theocratic monarchy, King Robert "the Longsword"
- [[Principality of New Antioch]] — fortress-capital, seat of the Patriarch
- [[Iron Sultanate]] — the third power, independent of the Catholic-Faithful structure
- [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]] — warrior-priests and paladins
- [[Holy Science Academy]] — techno-clergy blending faith and empiricism
- [[Office of the Propagation of Virtue]] — doctrinal analysis, propaganda, intelligence

## Theological Distinction
What defines the Faithful is not merely belief but *experience*. They have a God who answers — visibly, tangibly, daily. Saints appear in battle. Relics stop bullets. The [[Orichalcum]] in their armour is blessed metal that violates thermodynamics. This is not delusion sustained over eight centuries and a hundred million souls. It is the reality of their world.

This creates the central tension in first contact with modern Earth: the Faithful look at modern humanity and see souls that are "blank pages" — neither touched by Heaven nor Hell. To them, this is a sign. To modern governments committed to secularism, it is a dangerous invitation to theological entanglement that they cannot accept and cannot ignore.
