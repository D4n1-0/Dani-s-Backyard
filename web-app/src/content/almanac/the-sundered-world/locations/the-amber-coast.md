# The Amber Coast

## Overview

The Amber Coast is a string of prosperous port-cities along the northern shore of [[Meridies]], the economic artery of the Scattered Realms. Its airship yards, telegraph cables, and merchant banks connect the continents. Its neutrality is purchased by generous tribute to any power that threatens its caravans — and its true power lies in information.

## Features

- **[[Hanavel]]:** The greatest airship yard on [[Gaethon]], home to the *Cloud-King*-class passenger zeppelins.
- **The Exchange District:** A warren of counting houses, telegraph offices, and coffee houses where commodity prices, military intelligence, and [[Kaelen]]'s electromagnetic emissions are traded as equals.
- **The High Syndic's Palace:** Rotating seat of the League's government, occupied by whichever Syndic currently holds the annual chair.
- **The Signal Tower:** A massive directional aerial maintained by the [[The Signal Seekers|Signal Seekers]], angled permanently toward Kaelen. The Seekers' monastery adjoins it.

## Inhabitants

The Coin-Lords — merchant princes who control the League's banking houses — plus a cosmopolitan population of traders, spies, airship crews, and the quasi-monastic Signal Seekers whose recordings of Kaelen's emissions are the Coast's most valuable intangible asset.

## Current Events

The High Syndic is attempting to mediate between the [[Iron Concordat]] (massing on the hinterland) and the [[Technate of Vorr]] (testing stratospheric airships). The Coin-Lords are quietly moving assets out of exposed border territories.
