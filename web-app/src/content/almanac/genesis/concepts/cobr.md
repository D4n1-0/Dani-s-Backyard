# COBR

## Summary
The UK government's crisis management mechanism — literally the Cabinet Office Briefing Rooms beneath Whitehall — convened by PM [[Stephen Davenport]] for the first inter-reality emergency meeting in history.

## Details

COBR (or "COBRA," from Briefing Room A) is the standard activation for national-level emergencies in the UK: terrorist attacks, natural disasters, pandemic response, military crises. It brings together the Prime Minister, relevant Cabinet ministers, the Chief of the Defence Staff, and intelligence heads in a single secure room.

Chapter 04 marks the first time COBR has ever been asked to debate:
- The theological implications of first contact with a theocratic parallel civilisation
- The nature of the human soul as an empirically measurable quantity
- Whether the UK is a divinely preserved ark
- The operational requirements of a potential interdimensional war

The room itself — a soundproofed, air-filtered bunker with a long oak table, leather chairs, and encrypted screens — becomes a character in its own right, a symbol of the secular state's institutional machinery trying to process something it was never designed to handle.
