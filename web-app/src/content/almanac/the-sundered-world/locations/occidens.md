# Occidens

## Overview

Occidens is the sunset expanse — the westernmost of [[Gaethon]]'s three continents, characterised by temperate coastlines, deep-water harbours, and a maritime climate that bred the world's greatest naval power. It is the heartland of the [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] and the primary theatre of great-power naval competition.

## Features

- **Valdran Coast:** The eastern seaboard, where [[Valdris Prime]] and a dozen satellite precinct-cities cluster around sheltered harbours. The densest concentration of pre-dreadnought shipyards on Gaethon.
- **The Crown Interior:** Rolling agricultural land divided into noble estates, many of which blur the line between country manor and satellite town — a permanent census headache.
- **The Western Approaches:** Sparsely populated hinterland facing the open ocean. Used for naval exercises, airship proving grounds, and the Valdran Navy's gunnery ranges.

## Inhabitants

Dominated by the Valdran diarchy and its Chamber of Magnates. Secondary powers include minor coastal principalities that pay tribute to Valdris in exchange for naval protection. The population is the most stable on Gaethon — Valdran census management is the gold standard of Mandate compliance.

## Current Events

The Chamber of Magnates debates the first diplomatic mission to [[Kaelen]] in two centuries — a proposal that has divided the government between "Openers" (who believe the Mandate may be negotiable) and "Stabilisers" (who believe any approach risks annihilation).
