# Monsignor Franco Ricci

## Description

Small, compact, precise. Black cassock immaculate, Roman collar white as a flag of truce. A diplomat of the [[Holy See|Vatican]]'s Secretariat of State, dispatched to the portal zone on the personal instruction of the Cardinal Secretary of State. Speaks English, Italian, and Latin with the soft music of the Roman Curia. Kind — genuinely kind, not diplomatically kind — and utterly convinced that forced faith is no faith at all. Represents a Catholic Church that has evolved through centuries that the Faithful Church never experienced: the Reformation, the Enlightenment, Vatican II.

## Background

Assigned as escort to [[Bishop Aldric]] during his visit to modern London. Speaks the old Latin with a Roman accent — the same liturgy, the same apostolic succession — and yet his Church is unrecognisable to the bishop: ecumenical where the Faithful Church is militant, gentle where the Faithful Church is strong, convinced that the greatest threat to faith is coercion rather than heresy. His presence is a haunting mirror: same Church, entirely different religion.

## Relationships

- [[Bishop Aldric]] — his ward in modern London; treats him with genuine respect even as their theological worlds collide
- [[Holy See]] — serves the Vatican Secretariat of State

## Current Status

In modern London, escorting Bishop Aldric during his extended stay.
