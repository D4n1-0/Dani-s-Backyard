# Bishop Aldric

## Description

Fifty-three. Bishop of Southwark in the Faithful Crown's Church. Genuinely devout, not a political bishop — a working cleric who has spent decades ministering to the faithful of London's southern diocese. Dressed in the black-and-silver vestments of his office, bearing a reliquary containing a finger-bone of St. Thomas of Canterbury. A man who has never questioned the war against the Adversary — not because he lacks intelligence, but because the war is as fundamental to his world as gravity.

## Background

Selected as the Faithful Crown's envoy to the modern world — the counterpart to [[Malcolm Renfield|Sir Malcolm Renfield]]. Crossed the portal and was escorted to modern London by [[Monsignor Franco Ricci|a Vatican representative]] and Foreign Office liaison. Walked through a city that had never known war — a Parliament where no one prayed for guidance, streets where consumerism had replaced liturgy, churches turned into museums. Found modern London more terrifying than any Heretic trench because the enemy in the trench wore its own face; in modern London, the enemy was the absence of any awareness that an enemy existed. Accepted the offer to stay a few days. Alone in a hotel room, began to doubt the [[The Ark|ark]] theology — wondering if the ark is not a vessel but a test.

## Relationships

- [[Alistair Pembroke]] — travelled to the portal with him; the captain who arranged his crossing
- [[Monsignor Franco Ricci]] — his Vatican escort in modern London; same apostolic succession, utterly alien Church
- [[Inquisitor Paulus]] — the New Antioch inquisitor who is his counterpart in skepticism, though Aldric's doubt is theological rather than investigative

## Current Status

Returned from the modern world. Articulated [[The Theology of Waiting|a new theology]] — that the moderns are neither an ark ([[Father Beauchamp|Beauchamp]]) nor a test ([[Inquisitor Paulus|Paulus]]), but witnesses: a people who have lived through the hiddenness of God (*[[Deus Absconditus]]*) and whose meaning is not yet fully revealed. The Faithful must wait, watch, and learn rather than rushing to embrace or reject. Witnessed the collision between *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|USS Stout]]* and *[[HMS Resolute]]* from the open deck of the American destroyer — and watched the same destroyer obliterate [[The Heretic Submarine|a Heretic submarine]] with a single torpedo. Delivered his theology to the Faithful command aboard the crippled *[[HMS Resolute|Resolute]]*.
