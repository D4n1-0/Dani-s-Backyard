# Hange Zoë

## Description
Late twenties / early thirties. Goggles, unkempt brown hair, manic energy barely restrained by the weight of command. Now Commander of the Survey Corps following Erwin Smith's death. A Titan biologist by passion, a military leader by necessity. Her obsessive curiosity about Titan physiology and behaviour now extends to the nature of the world itself — and the revelation of the basement has given her more questions than answers.

## Background
Rose through the Survey Corps ranks as a researcher rather than a pure combatant. Responsible for key discoveries about Titan biology — including their solar dependency and the existence of Shifters within human ranks. Erwin's death forced her into command of an organisation gutted by Shiganshina. She is brilliant, erratic, deeply principled, and utterly unprepared for the geopolitical nightmare ahead.

## Relationships
- [[Levi Ackerman]] — closest surviving comrade; the two carry Erwin's legacy together
- [[Armin Arlert]] — protégé; Hange sees Erwin's strategic mind in him
- [[Eren Yeager]] — subject of intense interest and concern; Hange fears what Eren might become
- [[Erwin Smith]] — predecessor (deceased); Hange lives in his shadow

## Current Status
Commanding the Survey Corps from Paradis. Processing the basement revelations. Desperate to understand the new world before it kills them all. The Gate will simultaneously validate her deepest scientific instincts and present a crisis beyond anything the Corps has faced.
