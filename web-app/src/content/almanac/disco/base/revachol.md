# Revachol

*The city. Once the capital of the world during the Suzerainty. Crushed by the Coalition in '08 after the Commune fell. Now the seat of the Zone of Control — occupied, impoverished, perpetually on the edge of the next revolution or the final collapse. A city that remembers its glory and lives in its ruins. Home to Grand Couron, Jamrock, Martinaise, and a thousand other districts, each with its own history of hope and failure.*
