# UNCLOS

## Summary
The United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea, the international treaty that establishes the legal framework for maritime jurisdiction. [[Étienne Morel]]'s legal advisers invoke UNCLOS to challenge the UK's claim to unilateral authority over the Anomaly.

## Context
France's argument: the portal is approximately one hundred and fifty nautical miles from the nearest landmass — beyond the twelve-nautical-mile territorial limit of any coastal state. An EEZ (Exclusive Economic Zone) confers rights over natural resources, not sovereignty over the water column itself. Since the Anomaly is neither a resource nor a structure but a "discontinuity in the fabric of reality," no existing treaty category covers it, and the default position under international law is that no single nation holds jurisdiction. The portal, in French reading, belongs to everyone — and to no one.
