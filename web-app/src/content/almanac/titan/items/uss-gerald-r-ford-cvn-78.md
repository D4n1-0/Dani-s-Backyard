# USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)

## Description
Lead ship of the Gerald R. Ford-class — the largest, most advanced aircraft carrier ever built. Nuclear-powered (A1B reactor, 50-year core life), 337 metres in length, 100,000+ tonnes displacement. Embarked air wing of approximately 75 aircraft including F/A-18E/F Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, E-2D Hawkeyes, MH-60R/S Seahawks, and CMV-22B Ospreys. Electromagnetic Aircraft Launch System (EMALS) replacing traditional steam catapults. Dual-band radar. A single *Ford*-class carrier represents more combat power than the entire Marleyan navy combined.

## Function
Flagship of Carrier Strike Group Twelve, deployed to the AOT side of the Gate as CATF's ultimate power-projection asset. Her mission at the south bank was deterrence through presence — positioning the carrier group fifty kilometres off the Marleyan coast while the *Maersk København* delivered construction materials. No aircraft were launched. No weapons were fired. The message was the ship herself.

## History
Transited the Gate from Earth to the AOT world as part of the CATF forward deployment — the first supercarrier to operate in an alternate ocean. Staged from [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub|Grande Terre]] for the Odiha mission. Led the CSG escort for the *Maersk* south bank delivery, then withdrew south after the cargo was unloaded.
