# INSURCOM

## Overview

*The military arm of the Coalition occupation in Revachol. Coalition acronym for the Insulindic Surrender and Containment Military Command. Troops patrol the Zone of Control, enforce the terms of the Landing, and occasionally — as in Martinaise — serve as the mailed fist of corporate interests. Soldiers are professional, underpaid, and largely indifferent to the political theatre they're enforcing.*

## Structure

- [[Henrik Varga|Commander Henrik Varga]] — Local commander, Martinaise operation
- Infantry units — Occupation patrols, perimeter enforcement

## Assets

- Military control of the Zone of Control
- Patrol boats, transport vehicles, small arms
- Legal immunity within the Zone's occupation framework

## Relationships

- [[Coalition of Nations]] — *Parent body. Receives orders, files reports.*
- [[Wild Pines Group]] — *Client. Freed the terminal on their behalf.*
- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — *Adversary by assignment, not ideology.*
