# Tone & Themes

## Narrative Voice

*Wry, literary, melancholic. Internal monologue carries the weight of history and the lightness of dark humour. The narrative is always watching, always judging, always slightly drunk. Every description is a moral position. Every adjective is a confession.*

## Thematic Pillars

### The Cage You Build
*Systems of control — political, economic, psychological — and the people who build them. Lukas constructs a security apparatus for others while living inside Joyce's. The terminal is a prison; so is his career. The question is not whether cages exist, but who has the key.*

### Power and Flesh
*Power in Revachol is never abstract. It is transactional, physical, sexual. The relationship between Lukas and Joyce is the story's engine and its wound. Blackmail is intimacy. Leverage is love.*

### The Long Defeat
*Martinaise has been conquered before. The Commune rose and fell. The Coalition landed. The strike surged and broke. Nothing ends; everything cycles. The question is what a person does in the gap between defeats — whether they build, resist, or simply endure.*

### Memory and Forgetting
*Harry Du Bois lost his memory and found a truth. Lukas remembers everything and builds a lie. The Pale unmakes memory. The occupation erases history. What you choose to forget defines you as much as what you remember.*
