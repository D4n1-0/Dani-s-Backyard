# Iceburst Stone

## Summary
A volatile fossil fuel unique to Paradis Island — formed when pressurised underground gases were frozen and solidified by the Founding Titan's power over centuries. When warmed, it rapidly expands and vaporises, providing propellant for ODM gear and advanced aviation.

## Details
Iceburst Stone is Paradis's only strategic natural resource of interest to the outside world. Hizuru's Azumabito clan seeks an absolute monopoly on extraction rights. The substance is the key to ODM gear propulsion — without it, Paradis loses its signature combat mobility advantage. It is also a potential propellant for early aviation, making it strategically significant for a world at ~1920s technology level.

## Context
For the modern world, Iceburst Stone is an exotic chemical propellant — scientifically interesting but not revolutionary. Its true value is as Paradis's sole trade asset and as leverage for Hizuru. Modern nations may find it trivial, which itself becomes a negotiating dynamic. In AOT world terms, it is Paradis's oil.
