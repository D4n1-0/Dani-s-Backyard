# Ito

## Overview
Captain, Japan Ground Self-Defense Force (JGSDF). Forty-one years old. Commanding officer of the CATF advance detachment at the [[South Bank (Odiha)]] compound. A logistics specialist. Prior to the south bank posting, served for three months at [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub|Grande Terre]] managing the Paradis aid shipment supply chain. Volunteered for the south bank assignment because he wanted to see the AOT mainland.

## Background
Career logistics officer in the JGSDF. Commissioned from the National Defense Academy. Prior to the Gate's opening, his career was conventional — supply chain management, logistics planning, the unglamorous administrative backbone of the ground self-defence force. The Gate changed everything: he was seconded to CATF for his logistics expertise, served the Paradis aid pipeline, and was selected for the south bank advance detachment because he had proven he could manage a supply chain across two worlds.

## Relationships
- [[Theo Magath]] — met at the south bank helipad during the advance detachment's arrival; mutual professional respect between career officers
- [[Satomi Ishihara]] — reports to her as Chief of Mission for CATF Marley operations

## Status
Commanding the first forty CATF personnel at the south bank compound. The base is operational but not yet at full capacity. The next wave of personnel arrives within the month.
