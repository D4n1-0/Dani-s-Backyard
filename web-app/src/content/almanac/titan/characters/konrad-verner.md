# Konrad Verner

## Description
Sixty-one years old. Konteradmiral (Rear Admiral) of the Imperial Marleyan Navy. Commander of the Odiha Naval District for four years. Grey-haired, thick-browed, walks with a pronounced limp from a shattered knee sustained during a storm off Lakua decades ago. A career officer who rose to flag rank through competence rather than politics, then plateaued in a quiet southern port while the Mid-East War consumed the navy's attention and resources. His defining trait is a forty-year habit of listening before shooting.

## Background
Peaked at Konteradmiral — the senior-most field command available in the Marleyan Navy. Stationed at Odiha because he was politically reliable and tactically spent — too valuable to retire, too old to deploy to the Mid-East front. Has commanded every class of vessel in the Marleyan fleet across four decades. Knows the southern ocean better than any living Marleyan officer. The *Pelikan* expedition was one of the last vessels to depart under his signature before the expedition was declared overdue, then lost. Its return forced him to become the first Marleyan flag officer to meet a power his empire had no framework for understanding.

## Current Status
At Odiha. Communication channel with CATF established. Has sent his report to the capital. Awaiting the general staff's response — with the quiet, informed dread of a man who knows exactly what his government does to things it cannot control.
