# Omni-Directional Mobility Gear

## Description
A gas-propelled body harness enabling three-dimensional aerial manoeuvring. Uses pressurised [[Iceburst Stone]] gas for propulsion and dual steel-wire grappling hooks for anchoring. Operators wield paired ultra-hard steel blades specifically designed to sever Titan napes. The rig sits at the waist and thighs, with grapple launchers on each hip and blade sheaths mounted for quick-draw.

## Function
Designed to fight Titans at their own elevation — the only technology that allows unaugmented humans to engage 3m–15m class Titans effectively. Requires exceptional balance, spatial awareness, and physical conditioning. Useless on open, flat terrain where no anchor points exist.

## History
Developed by Paradis engineers over decades of Titan warfare. Continuously improved — the latest models have enhanced gas efficiency and grapple range. The Marleyan military has no equivalent; their doctrine relies on artillery and Titan Shifters, not mobile infantry.
