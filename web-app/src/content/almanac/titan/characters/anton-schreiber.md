# Anton Schreiber

## Overview
Colonel of the Marleyan Titan Security Division, deputy to General Richter. Forty-five years old, twenty years in the Security Division. A career administrator rather than a field commander whose most significant command was a three-month rotation supervising Eldian labour battalions during the Mid-East mobilisation. Loyal to Richter. Intelligent in the bureaucratic sense. Utterly unqualified to assess a multinational military operation.

## Background
Joined the Titan Security Division at twenty-five as a junior administrator. Rose through the ranks by being reliable, discreet, and loyal to his superiors rather than through any distinction in field command. His expertise is Eldian population management and Titan deployment doctrine — for which he was chosen as the military faction's observer at the [[South Bank (Odiha)]] compound. The appointment was political: Richter needed eyes in the room, and Schreiber was the man who asked the fewest questions.

## Relationships
- [[Richter|General Richter]] — direct superior; owes his appointment and loyalty to Richter's patronage
- [[Theo Magath]] — nominal superior at Odiha; Schreiber reports through Magath's office but his dispatches are written for the capital
- [[Satomi Ishihara]] — no direct contact; Schreiber's escorted access to the compound prevents any unsupervised CATF-Marleyan interaction

## Status
Assigned to the south bank as military faction observer. Has not yet arrived at Odiha. His office is being prepared in the Port Authority. His telegrapher is standing by.
