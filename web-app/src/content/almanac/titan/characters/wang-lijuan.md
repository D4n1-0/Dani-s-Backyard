# Wang Lijuan

## Description
Forty-six years old. Director of the Oceanographic Survey Bureau, State Oceanic Administration of China. Marine biologist by training — PhD from the Institute of Oceanology, Chinese Academy of Sciences in Qingdao. Practical, unsentimental, the kind of scientist-bureaucrat who has spent her career translating field data into policy positions. Understands that the Gate is not just a geopolitical crisis — it is a scientific event that will define her discipline for a century.

## Background
Graduate of Ocean University of China. Post-doctoral work at the Second Institute of Oceanography in Hangzhou. Joined the SOA during the 2010s expansion of Chinese oceanographic research. Published on deep-sea ecosystems and East China Sea biodiversity. Never imagined her expertise would be relevant to an interdimensional contact event.

## Relationships
- [[Zheng Weimin]] — provides scientific counsel to the incident group
- Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]] — South Korean counterpart; will soon serve alongside him on the joint scientific committee
- Vice Admiral [[Zhang Haifeng]] — respectful distance; she is a scientist, he is a soldier, and the Gate needs both

## Current Status
Deployed aboard [[Xiang Yang Hong 06]] at the exclusion zone perimeter. Co-chair of the [[Joint Scientific Committee]] alongside Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]]. Commanding Chinese UAV, ASV, and ROV operations through the Gate. Confirmed the isotopic anomaly in the AOT-side water chemistry and co-authored the finding that confirmed the [[Inverted Earth]] theory. Maintains professional warmth with Ahn while remaining conscious of the political eyes on their collaboration.
