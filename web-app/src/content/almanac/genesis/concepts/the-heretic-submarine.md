# The Heretic Submarine

## Summary
A submarine of the [[Heretic Legion]]'s naval branch — the Heretic Fleet under High Captain Ranga — that conducted systematic intelligence surveillance on the portal cordon, intercepting and relaying every transmission from both the modern coalition and the Faithful fleet. Discovered during [[Bishop Aldric]]'s return transit aboard *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|USS Stout]]*; destroyed by a Mk 54 torpedo after a high-speed chase that resulted in the collision between *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|Stout]]* and *[[HMS Resolute]]*.

## Detection
The submarine was detected through fragmentary, incomplete intelligence:
1. **SIGINT:** Anomalous radio transmissions — encrypted signals that did not match any known modern or Faithful pattern — were detected as relay signals originating from within the portal cordon.
2. **Passive Sonar:** The *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|USS Stout]]*'s SQS-53C hull-mounted sonar confirmed a subsurface contact at the approximate bearing, operating silently below the thermocline.
3. **Pattern Confirmation:** Coalition SIGINT analysis confirmed the submarine had been systematically intercepting and relaying fleet communications for hours — possibly days — transmitting to an unknown Heretic command post.

## The Chase and Destruction
The submarine used *[[HMS Resolute]]* as cover when the *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|Stout]]* moved to engage — ducking beneath the Faithful dreadnought's hull to avoid the modern destroyer's pursuit. In the close-quarters chase, *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|Stout]]* collided with the *[[HMS Resolute|Resolute]]*'s port side, tearing open her hull below the waterline. *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|Stout]]* recovered, reacquired the contact, and destroyed the submarine with a single Mk 54 lightweight torpedo — thirty-one seconds from launch to impact.

## Significance
- **First confirmed Heretic presence** at the portal. The [[Heretic Legion]] has been aware of the portal, the cordon, and the alliance discussions for an unknown period.
- **Everything has been compromised.** Every transmission — military, diplomatic, theological — across the portal cordon has been intercepted and relayed to Heretic command.
- **The Heretics now know:** the existence of the modern world, the composition of the coalition fleet, the theological debates within the Faithful command, the contents of [[King Robert's Letter]] and [[Davenport's Reply]], and the fractures within both fleets.
- **Implication:** The [[Heretic Legion]]'s silence up to this point has not been absence — it has been preparation. What they do with the intelligence they have gathered is unknown.
