# Maya Patel

## Overview
Neuropsychologist, age 33. Former Neuralink researcher — left the company when she concluded that the regulatory ceiling in the private sector would never let her test the technologies she believed were viable. A true believer in neural augmentation as the next stage of human cognitive evolution. Not malevolent — the malice is absent — but the techno-optimism is so total that it renders ethical braking effectively inert. Sees the Ackerman bloodline not as a threat to be neutralised but as a proof-of-concept for enhanced-human interface: "If we can control an enhanced human, we can augment any human."

## Key Role
Provided the psychological assessment of [[Levi Ackerman|Levi]] as a BCI subject. Her argument: Levi's trauma architecture (Underground survivor, Erwin's death, utilitarian self-sacrifice, loyalty to [[Hange Zoë|Hange]]) makes Medium-level incentive stimulation uniquely effective — the subject is already predisposed to accept orders from trusted authority. The BCI would not need to *override* his will; it would need only to *steer* his will toward the right targets.
