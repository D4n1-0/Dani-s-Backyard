# Southern Indian Ocean Gate Site

## Overview
The AOT-world side of [[The Gate]]. Opens into a remote stretch of the southern Indian Ocean, approximately 20 kilometres from the inverted equivalent of the Alfred Faure research station on Île de la Possession (Crozet Archipelago). In the Attack on Titan world, these identical geographic features are mapped in the northern hemisphere due to the [[Inverted Earth|north-south inversion]].

The site is thousands of kilometres from any human habitation or trade route in the AOT world's ~1914-era. No Paridisian, Marleyan, or Mid-Eastern vessel regularly transits these waters. At the moment of the Gate's opening — the night before the Battle of Shiganshina — no one on the AOT side is aware the Gate exists.

## Features
- Deep subantarctic ocean water, ~3–4°C
- High biological productivity — krill, fish, seabirds (including wandering albatross)
- Both sides are night at the Gate's opening; the same solar time on each side means simultaneous dawn
- The Gate is not visible from the AOT side through any visual signature — radar or thermal cross-section would be detectable, but no AOT vessel passes nearby

## Inhabitants
- None (at time of opening)
- Wildlife: wandering albatross, petrels, southern right whales, krill

## Current Events
Unaware of the Gate. The survey radius has expanded to 150 km. Drone and ROV operations have mapped the seafloor, confirming it matches terrestrial Indian Ocean bathymetry. The [[Inverted Alfred Faure Islands]] — 20 km west of the Gate — have been imaged in detail: barren, volcanic, uninhabited. No vessel, structure, or human presence has been detected. The Gate bleeds through — warm Yellow Sea water, plastic particulates, and chemical pollutants continue diffusing into the AOT ocean, contaminating the baseline data with every passing day.
