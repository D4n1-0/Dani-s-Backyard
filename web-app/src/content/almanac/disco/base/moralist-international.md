# Moralist International

*The dominant political bloc within the Coalition of Nations. Centrist, technocratic, imperial under a veneer of reason. The Moralintern governs the Reál Belt through institutions: the Coalition military (INSURCOM), economic oversight (EPIS), police supervision (ICP). Its ideology is order. Its project is the Zone of Control. Its mandate in Revachol expires with Le Retour in May '51 — a deadline that no one in Martinaise can afford to ignore.*
