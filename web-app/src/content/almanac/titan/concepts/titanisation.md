# Titanisation

## Summary
The process by which a Subject of Ymir is transformed into a Pure Titan through injection of Titan spinal fluid.

## Details
Subjects of Ymir injected with Titan spinal fluid become mindless Pure Titans — giant, regenerating, sun-powered humanoids that instinctively hunt humans. They can be restored to human form only by consuming the spinal fluid of a Titan Shifter.
