# Office of the Propagation of Virtue

## Summary
A canonical Trench Crusade organisation within the [[Principality of New Antioch]] that combines doctrinal analysis, propaganda, intelligence-gathering, and morale operations. Its officers wear a scholar's robe over a naval tunic.

## Details
The Office serves as the ideological arm of the Faithful war effort — ensuring doctrinal purity, producing propaganda, analysing captured Heretic texts, and providing theological assessments of unusual phenomena. In the Genesis scenario, the Office is mobilised by [[Caius Blackwood|Inquisitor-Admiral Blackwood]] to conduct a doctrinal analysis of [[The Anomaly]] and the implications of contact with a world untouched by the war.

## Context
The Office's involvement signals that [[The Anomaly]] is being treated not just as a military event, but as a potential theological crisis for the Faithful Church.
