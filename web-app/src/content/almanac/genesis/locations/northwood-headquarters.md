# Northwood Headquarters

## Overview
The Permanent Joint Headquarters of the United Kingdom, located in Northwood, Hertfordshire. The operational nerve centre from which the modern British response to [[The Anomaly]] is coordinated.

## Features
- Main threat board and imagery analysis stations
- Direct communication links to the White House and NATO Brussels
- Strategic command over all Royal Navy assets in the North Atlantic

## Inhabitants
- [[Sarah Vance]] — Commander, Royal Navy Operations Directorate

## Current Events
Primary command post during the first contact standoff at [[The Anomaly]].
