# Odiha

## Overview
A large Marleyan coastal city on the continental mainland's southern border. Overlooks the sea to the southeast, surrounded by high mountains to its north. A major river traverses the continent to the south, making Odiha a key transit point connecting the Marleyan interior to the southern ocean. The port is economically and strategically significant — deep-water berths capable of servicing naval and commercial vessels, a harbour master's office with a full signal tower, and an Azumabito clan hangar owned by the nation of [[Hizuru]]. Stone buildings climb the lower slopes of the mountains; streets follow the river inland. Gas lamps light the waterfront. The city has a permanent Marleyan naval presence: the district command under Admiral [[Konrad Verner]].

## Features
- **Inner Harbour:** Deep-water berths, Pier 3 (commercial), Pier 4 (diplomatic/visitor), outer cargo wharf
- **Watchtower:** Signal tower with visual and flag communication, harbour entry control
- **Port Authority Building:** Two-storey stone structure, wood-panelled offices, the admiral's corner office overlooking the harbour
- **Azumabito Hangar:** Hizuru-owned aviation facility at the port — currently unused but maintained
- **Mountains:** Sheer grey peaks north of the city, snow-capped, natural defensive barrier
- **River:** Broad river mouth south of the harbour, navigable deep into the continental interior

## Inhabitants
- Admiral [[Konrad Verner]] — Commander, Odiha Naval District
- Marleyan frigate *Steinadler* crew — Captain Langsdorff commanding (at anchor in harbour)
- Harbour master, dockhands, port authority staff
- Civilian population: fishermen, traders, merchants, river-boat operators
- Azumabito clan representatives (minimal presence; hangar maintenance staff)

## Current Events
Site of the [[CATF–Marley First Contact]]. The *Pelikan* returned after 78 days missing, escorted by CATF diplomatic and support vessels. Admiral Verner received the delegation personally. A communication channel was established. CATF departed after two days; Verner's report to the capital is pending response. The *Pelikan* remains at Pier 3, restored to Marleyan service.
