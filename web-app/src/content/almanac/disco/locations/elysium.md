# Elysium

## Overview
The world — a fractured sphere whose surface is not contiguous. Seven known isolas (continents of matter) are separated by the [[Pale]], which covers 72% of the known surface. The Pale-to-matter ratio is approximately 2:1 and slipping.

## Features
- Seven known isolas: [[Mundi]], [[Insulinde]], [[Seol]], [[Samara]], [[Graad]], [[Katla]], [[Iilmaraa]]
- The [[Pale]] between them
- Aerostatic airships for interisolary transit
- No orbital spaceflight possible — the Pale interferes
- Weather balloon imagery shows a "dark grey corona" enveloping the planet

## Current Events
The [[Moralist International]] dominates global politics. No Innocence has been elected in this century. The Pale continues to expand.
