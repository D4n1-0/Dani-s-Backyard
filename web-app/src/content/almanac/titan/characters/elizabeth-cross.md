# Elizabeth Cross

## Overview
Deputy Secretary of State of the United States. A career Foreign Service officer who served as Ambassador to Seoul before her elevation to Washington. Speaks fluent Korean. Selected personally by the President for the Singapore delegation — not for her language skills alone but for a temperament that is steady under pressure.

Represents the United States on the CATF [[Quadripartite Executive Committee]]. Pragmatic, sophisticated, aware that American military weight must be wielded carefully in a coalition that includes both allies and strategic competitors.
