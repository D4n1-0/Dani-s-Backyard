# Grande Terre Logistics Hub

## Overview
The full-spectrum CATF command, logistics, and fleet anchorage facility on the AOT side, located forty kilometres southwest of [[The Gate]] on the inverted Kerguelen Archipelago. Grande Terre is the rear base and operational hub of CATF's AOT-side presence — the port through which all construction materials, fuel, and personnel transit. The island is barren, uninhabited, and was never known to any AOT-native power.

The harbour is a deep-water fjord sheltered on three sides by volcanic ridges, capable of anchoring the entire CATF fleet. The installation combines command-and-control, intelligence fusion, fleet anchorage, and heavy logistics in a single site. Built across Weeks 2–5 under the [[Construction Progress]] timeline.

## Construction Timeline
- **Day 16 (Week 2):** Advance team lands. Harbour survey begins. Deep-water fjord identified.
- **Day 18–20:** Pontoon causeway assembled from modular floating sections. First truck drives onto AOT soil.
- **Day 22–24:** Fuel farm erection — 500,000+ litre bladder tanks.
- **Day 24:** Command complex graded — shipping container grid, operations centre, intelligence fusion cell.
- **Day 29:** Pontoon causeway superseded by semi-permanent modular pier (steel/reinforced concrete, berths two destroyers).
- **Day 30–32:** Command complex complete — secure comms suite, science module.
- **Day 33–35:** Fleet anchorage occupied by Task Group CATF-Alpha; [[Undersea Sensor Array]] processing station online.

## Features
- **Modular pier:** steel and concrete, berths two destroyers simultaneously
- **Pontoon causeway (replaced):** initial supply route through the Gate, superseded by permanent pier
- **Fuel farms:** 500,000+ litres diesel and aviation fuel in bladder tanks
- **Command complex:** shipping containers and prefab shelters, operations centre, intelligence fusion cell, communications suite
- **Quarters:** 200+ personnel capacity, expandable
- **Hydrophone processing station:** seafloor terminal for [[Undersea Sensor Array]] data
- **Fleet anchorage:** Task Group CATF-Alpha — one destroyer per CATF nation, plus patrol boats and support vessels
- **Science module:** joint KIOST/SOA/CATF research facility, including [[Elias Vogler|Dr. Elias Vogler]]'s office

## Inhabitants
- CATF multinational command, engineering, intelligence, and support personnel
- [[Elias Vogler|Dr. Elias Vogler]] — Marleyan scientific consultant (employed Day 36)
- [[Klaus Dreher|Oberleutnant Klaus Dreher]] — Marleyan naval observer (held Day 36)
- [[Frieda]] and seven other Eldian engineers — CATF civilian employees (sanctuary granted Day 36)
- No native population (island was uninhabited pre-CATF)

## Current Events
Full-spectrum operational as of Day 35. Hosting thirty-one Marleyan nationals (crew of the *Pelikan*) under [[First Contact Protocol]]. Remains undetected by the broader AOT world.
