# Vorran 105mm Howitzer

## Description

The Vorran 105mm howitzer is the signature artillery piece of the [[Technate of Vorr|Vorran Artillerie Corps]] — and the most politically charged weapon on [[Gaethon]]. It achieves a maximum effective range of *exactly* 9.9 kilometres, one hundred metres short of the [[Kaelen Mandate]]'s 10-kilometre artillery cap. The precision of this specification is not an engineering limitation — it is a political statement. Every howitzer produced by the Vorran foundries is deliberately limited to 9.9 km, a margin so narrow it verges on provocation, yet legally compliant by one hundred metres.

## Function

The howitzer's 105mm shell delivers a 15-kilogram high-explosive payload with a circular error probable of approximately 50 metres at maximum range — accuracy enabled by the Technate's famous clockwork fire-control computers, mechanical analogue devices that compute firing solutions from range, wind, temperature, and barrel-wear inputs. No other Scattered Realm possesses fire-control systems of comparable sophistication.

In Vorran doctrine, the howitzers provide the artillery preparation that precedes infantry and tank advances. A standard Artillerie Corps division fields 72 guns in three battalions of 24, capable of saturating a 500-metre front with over 1,000 shells in a fifteen-minute barrage.

## History

The 105mm howitzer was developed in Year 275 PC, replacing an earlier 75mm field gun with inferior range and payload. The 9.9 km limit was a deliberate decision by the Board of Applied Industries — a signal to [[Kaelen]] that the Technate understood exactly where the line was drawn and intended to live precisely on it. The [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame|Last Flame]]'s Inquisition has repeatedly demanded the howitzers be inspected for "range-creep modifications." No violations have been found. The Vorran foundries are meticulous.
