# Operation REEVE

## Summary
Covert CATF mission to capture a live Pure Titan on Paradis Island for biological analysis. Launched Day 58, extraction Day 65. Utilised the [[USS Tripoli (LHA-7)]] (LHA) and [[JS Suzutsuki (DD-117)]] (escort) for the 8,000 km transit. Capture executed by ROK Marine Corps element under Captain [[Park Sung-ho]], with CH-53K heavy-lift extraction. One Marine wounded (Corporal [[Lee Dae-won]], non-fatal). One live Titan captured.

## Mission Sequence
1. KUS-15 drone identifies and tracks 6 m-class Pure Titan in northern Paradis (low-priority Survey Corps zone)
2. Tripoli positions 32 km offshore under night / cloud cover, emissions control
3. CH-53K deploys capture team (12 Marines + 2 biologists) at 200 ft AGL, NVGs
4. M2 .50 cal disables Titan's limbs; four-point industrial chain anchoring
5. CH-53K sling-loads Titan to Tripoli flight deck
6. Light-blocking shroud deployed within 13 seconds; Titan goes dormant
7. Transit back to Grande Terre; specimen transferred to Titan Containment pen

## Outcome
Successful capture of first live Titan in human history. Provided the biological samples that revealed the Ex Nihilo Matter Creation discovery and confirmed Titan transformation mechanism. Mission status: classified under CATF special access programme.
