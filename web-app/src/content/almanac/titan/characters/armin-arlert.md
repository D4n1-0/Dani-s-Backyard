# Armin Arlert

## Description
Fifteen years old. Blond, slight, blue eyes — the strategic mind of his generation. Recently inherited the Colossal Titan from Bertolt Hoover after being given the Titan serum over Erwin Smith. Carries the weight of that choice — the belief that he must earn the life he was given, that Erwin should have lived instead.

## Background
Childhood friend of Eren and Mikasa. The intellectual of the trio — his ability to read situations, deduce enemy strategies, and propose unorthodox solutions has saved Paradis multiple times. His dream of seeing the ocean came true after Shiganshina — and was immediately poisoned by Eren's question: "If we kill all our enemies, will we finally be free?" Armin believes in diplomacy, understanding, and the possibility that the cycle can be broken without genocide.

## Relationships
- [[Eren Yeager]] — best friend; increasingly worried about Eren's trajectory
- [[Mikasa Ackerman]] — close friend
- [[Annie Leonhart]] — complicated; Armin visited her crystal repeatedly during the time skip
- [[Hange Zoë]] — intellectual partner; successor-in-waiting

## Current Status
On Paradis Island. Holds the Colossal Titan. Wants to find a path to peace. Has not yet been to Marley or seen the internment zones.
