# USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)

## Overview
The lead ship of the Ford-class nuclear-powered aircraft carriers, the newest and most advanced carrier in the United States Navy. Ordered to the portal cordon as a reinforcement to *[[USS Harry S. Truman (CVN-75)|USS Harry S. Truman]]* — a signal from Washington that the United States is prepared to commit its best assets to the crisis. Her hangar bay, partially converted for diplomatic use, serves as the site of the international military commanders' meeting convened by [[Terrence Kohler|Rear Admiral Kohler]] in response to [[Malcolm Renfield|Renfield]]'s report.

## Features
- Electromagnetic Aircraft Launch System (EMALS)
- Advanced Arresting Gear (AAG)
- Dual Band Radar (DBR)
- Nuclear propulsion — unlimited endurance
- Air wing of F/A-18E/F Super Hornets and F-35C Lightning IIs
- Hangar bay large enough to accommodate international diplomatic gatherings

## Inhabitants
- [[Terrence Kohler]] — Commander, Carrier Strike Group Two, embarked aboard the *[[USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)|Ford]]*
- Ship's company of approximately 4,500
- Embarked air wing

## Current Events
Stationed at the portal cordon alongside *[[USS Harry S. Truman (CVN-75)|USS Harry S. Truman]]* (CVN-75), serving as the hub for international military coordination. Her hangar bay has been converted for the international commanders' meeting and, separately, for [[Inquisitor Paulus]]'s examination of [[Rebecca Osei|Lieutenant Commander Osei]].
