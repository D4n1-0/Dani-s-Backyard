# Vow Renouncing War

## Summary
An ideological failsafe embedded in the Founding Titan by Karl Fritz, the 145th King — any royal-blooded inheritor is mentally possessed, rendered incapable of commanding the Wall Titans, and accepts the world's hatred as just retribution for Eldian sins.

## Details
The Vow ensures the royal family's perpetual passivity. It explains why the Reiss family never used the Founding Titan's power against the Titans within the walls, and why they suppressed knowledge of the outside world. The Vow can only be bypassed if a non-royal individual holds the Founding Titan and makes physical contact with a Titan of royal blood.

## Context
The Vow is both Paradis's greatest weakness (royals cannot defend the island) and its greatest weapon (a non-royal Founder + royal-blooded Titan = full Rumbling capability). Understanding this mechanical nuance is essential for modern negotiators.
