# Hizuru

## Overview
An oriental nation and former ally of the ancient Eldian Empire. Suffered severe economic and diplomatic isolation following the Eldian Empire's collapse in the Great Titan War. Now represented by the Azumabito clan, Hizuru seeks economic resurgence through a monopoly on Paradis Island's [[Iceburst Stone]] — the volatile fossil fuel unique to the island.

## Structure
- **Azumabito Clan:** ruling merchant family; Ambassador [[Kiyomi Azumabito]] is the primary foreign agent
- Diplomatic corps focused on technology-for-resources exchanges
- Advanced engineering and aviation capabilities (flying boats, railway blueprints)

## Assets
- Flying boat technology — the most advanced aviation in the AOT world
- Engineering and railway expertise
- Diplomatic cover — Hizuru can operate where Paradis cannot
- [[Mikasa Ackerman|Mikasa]]'s shogun bloodline — a potential dynastic link

## Relationships
- [[Eldian Military|Paradis]] — exclusive trade partner (not yet formalised at Gate-opening)
- [[Marleyan Empire]] — tolerated; Marley permits Hizuru's neutrality for now
- [[Mid-East Allied Forces]] — neutral; opportunistic trade

## Current Status
At the time of the Gate's opening, Hizuru has NOT yet made contact with Paradis. Kiyomi Azumabito will arrive in Year 852. The Gate may force an earlier meeting — or an entirely different first contact.
