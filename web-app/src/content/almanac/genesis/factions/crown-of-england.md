# Crown of England

## Overview
The theocratic English monarchy of the Trench Crusade timeline. A faithful nation that has fought the Heretic Legions for centuries, it maintains the Royal Fleet, the Crown Army, and the Air Knights.

## Structure
- High Command
- Crown Army
- Royal Fleet (includes *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]* and her escorts)
- Air Knights

## Assets
- The Moving Fortress of Britannia (completed 1907)
- The [[Fortress of the White Cliffs]] at Dover — naval headquarters under [[Caius Blackwood|Inquisitor-Admiral Blackwood]]
- Dreadnoughts (*[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*), fast cruisers (*[[HMS Herald|HMS Herald]]*), torpedo boat destroyers, and supporting vessels
- The [[Holy Science Academy]] — the Faithful techno-clergy
- The [[Office of the Propagation of Virtue]] — doctrinal and intelligence arm

## Relationships
- [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]] — the Order serves the Crown
- [[Heretic Legion]] — sworn enemy
- [[Royal Navy (Modern)]] — first contact through The Anomaly
