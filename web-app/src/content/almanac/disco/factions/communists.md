# Communists

## Summary
The political ideology of [[Kras Mazov]] and the Communard forces of the [[Antecentennial Revolution]]. Once rulers of the Commune of [[Revachol]] ('02–'08). Now a scattered, illegal remnant — a faith of the defeated, a ghost haunting the city. Massacred by the [[Coalition of Nations|Coalition]] in '08; their mass graves are in [[Ozonne]]. Despite never formally surrendering, they have no institutional power in '51.
