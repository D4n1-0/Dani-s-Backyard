# UK Carrier Strike Group

## Summary
The Royal Navy's primary power projection formation, built around an aircraft carrier with a screen of destroyers, frigates, and support vessels. Activated for the portal crisis, *[[HMS Queen Elizabeth (R08)|HMS Queen Elizabeth]]* sails as the core of the UK CSG.

## Details
In the context of the portal crisis, the UK CSG represents Britain's assertion of naval presence alongside — not beneath — the US deployment. The group includes *[[HMS Queen Elizabeth (R08)|HMS Queen Elizabeth]]* with her F-35B air wing, Type 45 air defence destroyers, and Royal Fleet Auxiliary tankers and supply ships. The activation signals that the United Kingdom retains independent power projection capability and will not cede operational control of the portal to any single ally.

## Context
The decision to assemble a full CSG rather than dispatch individual escorts reflects the strategic weight the UK places on the portal crisis. It is the largest Royal Navy deployment since the 1982 Falklands campaign.
