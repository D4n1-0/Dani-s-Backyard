# Ministry of Foreign Affairs (ROK)

## Overview
The diplomatic arm of the Republic of Korea, responsible for managing international relations, consular services, and treaty negotiations. Represents South Korea in the UN, multilateral forums, and bilateral relationships. In the Gate crisis, the MFA is responsible for managing the diplomatic framing of Seoul's response — including coordination with Washington, Beijing, Tokyo, and the UN Secretariat.

## Structure
- Minister of Foreign Affairs
- Vice Minister
- Bureaus for North American, Asian, European, and Multilateral Affairs
- Overseas embassies and consulates (160+ missions)

## Relationships
- [[National Security Office (ROK)]] — the NSO coordinates crisis response; the MFA implements diplomatic components
- [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (PRC)]] — counterpart in Beijing
- [[United States Department of State]] — primary diplomatic partner; alliance management

## Current Status
Supporting the crisis response through diplomatic channels. The Deputy Minister for Asian Affairs participated virtually in the Blue House crisis meeting.
