# Corpse Wars

## Summary
A devastating series of conflicts from 1346 to 1429, triggered by Beelzebub unleashing the Black Grail upon Europe.

## Details
The Corpse Wars began when the Black Grail was released, infecting tens of millions. The combined forces of France, the Holy Roman Empire, and the Crown of England fought against the Cult of the Black Grail. The war ended at the Battle of Valence in 1429, where Saint Jeanne d'Arc drove the Grail forces from mainland Europe.

## Context
Captain Roth has survived a decade of the Corpse Wars, marking him as a veteran of the Faithful's most brutal conflict.
