# Lukas Strieder

## Overview
Corporal, Garrison Regiment cavalry. Age 23. Veteran of the post-Shiganshina Titan purges in Wall Maria territory, where he survived extended operations against the remaining Pure Titan population. Partnered with Private [[Heinrich Vogt]] on the eastern patrol route along Paradis's new railway. First to identify the [[First Contact Pod]] as non-Marleyan technology — his combat experience with captured Marleyan equipment allowed him to distinguish the pod's materials and construction from anything the empire produced. After [[Armin Arlert]]'s arrival, [[Lukas Strieder]] rode for [[Hange Zoë]] to escalate the contact.

## Background
Recruited into the Garrison after the Wall Maria reclamation, when surviving Titans in the recovered territory required constant patrols. Developed a sharp eye for anomalous objects and a pragmatic fatalism — he survived the purges because he learned to treat every unknown as a potential threat until proven otherwise. The pod was the first unknown he classified as non-hostile.
