# Mikasa Ackerman

## Description
Fifteen years old. Oriental features inherited from her Hizuru mother. Black hair, grey eyes, lean muscular build. The deadliest soldier in Paradis — possibly on any battlefield in the known world. Her Ackerman bloodline grants her superhuman combat instincts and immunity to the Founding Titan's memory manipulation. Her sole psychological anchor is Eren Yeager.

## Background
Orphaned at age 9 when human traffickers murdered her parents. Rescued by Eren, who killed two of the attackers, awakening her Ackerman instincts. Taken in by the Yeager family. Joined the 104th Training Corps and graduated top of her class. Has proven lethal against Pure Titans and Shifter-level threats alike.

## Relationships
- [[Eren Yeager]] — adoptive brother; her entire emotional world orbits him
- [[Armin Arlert]] — close friend; the three are inseparable
- [[Levi Ackerman]] — distant cousin and captain; mutual respect
- [[Kiyomi Azumabito]] — Hizuru ambassador; descendant of a shogun bloodline Mikasa is heir to

## Current Status
On Paradis Island. Grappling with the revelation that she is part of a clan with ancient purpose. Her loyalty to Eren is absolute — and that may become the most dangerous variable in the coming crisis.
