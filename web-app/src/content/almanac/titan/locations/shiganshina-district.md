# Shiganshina District

## Overview
Southern gateway of Wall Maria. The birthplace of [[Eren Yeager]], [[Armin Arlert]], and [[Mikasa Ackerman]]. Site of two world-altering events: the first breach of Wall Maria by the Colossal and Armored Titans in Year 845 (resulting in 250,000 deaths), and the decisive Battle of Shiganshina in Year 850 where the Survey Corps defeated the Beast, Armored, and Colossal Titans at horrific cost. The basement of Grisha Yeager's house, located here, contained three books revealing the truth of the outside world.

## Features
- Grisha Yeager's basement — site of the revelation
- The breached gate — now sealed; memorial to the fallen
- The outer wall — scarred from Titan combat
- Mass graves from both the Year 845 breach and the 850 battle

## Inhabitants
- Resettlement population — former refugees returning
- Survey Corps outpost
- The ghosts of 250,000 dead

## Current Events
Cleared of Titans. Resettlement beginning. The basement's contents have been retrieved and are being analysed by the military government. Shiganshina is simultaneously a symbol of Paradis's greatest defeat and greatest victory.
