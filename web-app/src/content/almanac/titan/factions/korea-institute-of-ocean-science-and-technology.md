# Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology

## Overview
The Republic of Korea's premier oceanographic research institution. Conducts research in ocean circulation, marine ecosystems, seabed geology, and climate science. Operates research vessels and deep-sea submersibles. Has never been a first-contact organisation — until now.

## Structure
- Ocean Circulation and Climate Research Division
- Marine Ecosystem Research Division
- Marine Geology and Geophysics Division
- Research fleet: RV Isabu, RV Onnuri, and smaller vessels

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- A scientific team is being assembled for immediate dispatch.

## Relationships
- [[State Oceanic Administration (PRC)]] — research counterpart; will soon co-chair a joint scientific committee
- [[National Security Office (ROK)]] — providing scientific counsel to the crisis response

## Current Status
Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]] has presented preliminary sonar and water-sample data to the crisis meeting in Seoul. KIOST is preparing to be the scientific lead on the Gate — but must contend with a Chinese counterpart that wants co-leadership.
