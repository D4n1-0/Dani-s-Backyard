# Fortress of the White Cliffs

## Overview
The Faithful naval headquarters at Dover, carved into the chalk cliffs of southern England. A commandery-fortress combining military architecture with sacred geometry — gun batteries backed by cathedrals, telegraph lines running through consecrated stone. The seaward batteries face south toward Heretic-held Gibraltar. Construction began in 1670 after the fall of Gibraltar (1666) gave Hell Atlantic access. It is the operational nerve centre of the Faithful Fleet under [[Caius Blackwood|Inquisitor-Admiral Blackwood]].

## Features
- Inquisitor-Admiral's command chamber — cut directly into the chalk
- Seaward orichalcum-alloy gun batteries
- Signals and telegraph relay station
- [[Office of the Propagation of Virtue]] doctrinal analysis wing
- Garrison barracks and [[Sacred Order of the Dragon|Order]] chapel

## Inhabitants
- [[Caius Blackwood]] — Inquisitor-Admiral, commander
- [[Morgan Pell]] — Chaplain-Lieutenant, Order liaison
- Naval garrison, signals corps, engineer detachment

## Current Events
Mobilising the Faithful response to [[The Anomaly]]. Council convened as of Day 1 post-contact.
