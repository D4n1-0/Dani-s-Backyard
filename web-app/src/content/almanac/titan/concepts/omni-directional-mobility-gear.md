# Omni-Directional Mobility Gear

## Summary
A gas-propelled harness system enabling soldiers to grapple, swing, and manoeuvre in three dimensions — the signature combat technology of the Paradis military, designed to fight Titans at their own height.

## Details
Uses pressurised Iceburst Stone gas for propulsion and steel-wire grappling hooks for anchoring. Worn as a belt-and-harness rig with dual blades for nape strikes. Requires extraordinary balance, spatial awareness, and physical conditioning. Ineffective in open terrain without anchor points — designed for urban and forest environments within the Walls.

Marley has no comparable mobile infantry — their anti-Titan doctrine relies on artillery, naval firepower, and Titan Shifters. ODM gear is a uniquely Paradisian innovation born of the Walls' specific three-dimensional combat environment.

## Context
For modern militaries, ODM gear is tactically fascinating but strategically limited — a 19th-century-mechanical solution to a problem that doesn't exist on Earth. However, in close-quarters or urban combat, an ODM-equipped Ackerman is arguably more lethal than any special forces operator alive.
