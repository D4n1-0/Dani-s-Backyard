# United States

## Overview
The United States is a founding member of the [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)]] and contributes significant naval, intelligence, and logistical assets to the Gate theatre. U.S. Seventh Fleet provides the CATF with the largest single-nation naval presence on the AOT side.

## Assets in Theatre
- [[USS Ralph Johnson (DDG-114)]], [[USS Milius (DDG-69)]], [[USS Tripoli (LHA-7)]]
- [[Alfred Faure Forward Operating Base]] — commanded by [[Marcus Rivera|Captain Marcus Rivera]] (USN CEC)
- Intelligence support from [[Elizabeth Cross]] and CIA assets
