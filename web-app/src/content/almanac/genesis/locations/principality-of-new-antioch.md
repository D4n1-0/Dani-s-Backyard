# Principality of New Antioch

## Overview
The fortress-capital of the Faithful forces in the Trench Crusade timeline. A city built atop the ruins of ancient Antioch, fortified with walls that incorporate Orichalcum-alloy reinforcement, blessed by the Patriarch, and garrisoned by the most veteran troops of the Crusade. It is the seat of the Patriarch, the headquarters of the Holy Science Academy's theological division, and the home of the Office of the Propagation of Virtue.

## Features
- **The Cathedral-Patriarchal:** the spiritual heart of the Faithful, where the Patriarch presides over the largest congregation in Christendom.
- **The Scholarium:** a complex of libraries, laboratories, and scriptoria maintained by the Holy Science Academy.
- **Office of the Propagation of Virtue:** headquartered in a fortified cloister, where doctrinal analysis and intelligence assessments are produced.
- **The Arsenal Quarter:** workshops where Orichalcum is forged, relics are prepared, and weapons are blessed before deployment.
- **The Martyrs' Gate:** the main entrance, carved with the names of every Faithful soldier who has fallen in the defense of the city.

## Current Events
The Office of the Propagation of Virtue has been mobilised to assess the theological implications of [[The Anomaly]] and the arrival of modern Earth. Reports from London (TC) and the [[Fortress of the White Cliffs]] are being analysed in the Scholarium.
