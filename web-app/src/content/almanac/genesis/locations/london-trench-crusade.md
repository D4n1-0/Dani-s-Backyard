# London (Trench Crusade)

## Overview

The capital of the [[Crown of England|Faithful Crown]] in the Trench Crusade timeline. A fortress-city built on the same geography as modern London but shaped by eight centuries of continuous holy war against the [[Heretic Legion]]. The skyline is dominated by cathedral-fortresses — spires that double as watchtowers, bells replaced by signal lanterns, steeples reinforced with stone buttresses designed to withstand artillery fire. The streets are cobbled, lit by prayer-candles and oil lamps, and marked at every intersection by Orichalcum-tipped crosses bearing silver-thread prayers of warding. The Tower of London is a working fortress bristling with cannon and prayer-pennants. St. Paul's is a dark stone mass with defensive walls and arrow-slit windows and a tower that mounts twelve-pounder guns blessed by the Synod. The [[Office of the Propagation of Virtue]] maintains a fortress in the city — a dark stone building with the crowned-flame seal above its doors, its interrogation chambers visible through the lower windows.

## Features

- **Palace of Whitehall** — seat of [[King Robert the Longsword]], a working palace and military headquarters
- **The Strand** — main thoroughfare, now a barracks for the [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]]
- **Public squares** — every crossroads features a cross and a set of stocks for [[Public Penance|public penance]]
- **Cathedral-fortresses** — every church doubles as a defensive position; the air smells of incense and gunpowder
- **River Thames** — the same river, but the banks are fortified, the bridges are drawbridges, the shipping is wood and sail
- **Coastal artillery** — constant distant rumble from the eastern batteries, a sound as constant as the wind

## Inhabitants

The Faithful — a population that has never known a generation of peace. Every citizen is a soldier or a priest or both. The fear of the Adversary is not a doctrine; it is a fact of daily life. People walk with their heads down and their weapons ready.

## Current Events

[[Malcolm Renfield|Sir Malcolm Renfield]] visited the city — the first modern diplomat to set foot in TC London. [[Rebecca Osei|Osei]] has been living here for seven days under the Crown's hospitality. [[Inquisitor Paulus|Inquisitor Paulus]] has recently passed through on his way to the portal.
