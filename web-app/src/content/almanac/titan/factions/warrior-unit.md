# Warrior Unit

## Overview
The elite Titan Shifter corps of the Marleyan military — composed entirely of Eldian child soldiers brainwashed to serve their oppressors in exchange for "Honorary Marleyan" status for their families. The Warriors are the empire's strategic backbone, but their loyalty is maintained through coercion, propaganda, and the promise of a better life for their loved ones — a currency that is rapidly depreciating.

## Structure
- **Warchief:** [[Zeke Yeager]] (Beast Titan)
- **Vice-Captain:** [[Reiner Braun]] (Armored Titan)
- **Active Shifters:** Pieck Finger (Cart), Porco Galliard (Jaw)
- **Lost:** [[Bertolt Hoover]] (Colossal — killed), [[Annie Leonhart]] (Female — crystalised), Marcel Galliard (Jaw — killed)
- **Warrior Candidates:** [[Falco Grice]], [[Gabi Braun]], Udo, Zofia — children in training for inheritance

## Assets
- Four active Titan Shifters + War Hammer (Tybur, separate chain of command)
- Access to Marleyan naval and infantry support
- Field experience in the Mid-East War

## Relationships
- [[Marleyan Empire]] — master; Warriors are tools to be expended
- [[Eldian Military|Paradis]] — mortal enemy; three Warriors dead or lost to Paradis
- [[Anti-Marleyan Volunteers]] — traitors; Yelena and Onyankopon are former Marleyan subjects who defected
