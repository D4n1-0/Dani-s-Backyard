# The Silent Spires

## Overview

The Silent Spires are eight immense ballistic missile stations arranged in a precise geometric ring across [[Kaelen]]'s interior — each a mountain of steel and ceramic from which a conventional warhead can reach any point on [[Gaethon]] within an hour. They are the sword arm of the [[Kaelen Mandate]].

## Features

### Physical Description

The Spires are visible from orbit (according to astronomers) and faintly discernible through the best terrestrial telescopes as conical structures approximately 200–300 metres in height, surrounded by support infrastructure — access roads, fuel storage, probable launch control bunkers. Each Spire is estimated to house between twelve and twenty missile tubes, for a total Kaelen arsenal of 96–160 ready-to-launch weapons.

### Observed Behaviour

Missile launches have been recorded on five confirmed occasions since the [[War of Convergence]]:

1. **Year 17 PC** — Single launch. Target: an early industrial restoration attempt in eastern [[Oriens]]. Factory complex destroyed.
2. **Year 89 PC** — Twin launch. Target: an experimental airship with monoplane wing configuration. Vessel and construction yard destroyed.
3. **Year 201 PC** — Single launch. Target: disputed. May have been a false alarm or test.
4. **Year 262 PC** — Thirty-seven launches. Target: [[The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr|Sunken Kingdom of Myrr]]. Entire peninsula reduced to vitrified bay.
5. **Year 287 PC** — Single launch. Target: [[Keth]]. 15-kilometre radius of fused glass.

### Launch Signatures

Pre-launch activity includes: increased electromagnetic chatter on Kaelen's usual frequencies (reported by [[The Signal Seekers|Signal Seekers]]), repositioning of [[Silent Chorus]] drones over the target area, and — in the Myrr incident — a twenty-minute pause during which all Kaelen emissions fell silent. This "silence before the storm" is now the most feared signal in the Scattered Realms.

## Inhabitants

None — the Spires are assumed to be fully automated, maintained by systems that have functioned without observable human intervention for three centuries.

## Current Events

No launch since Year 287 — the longest quiet period in recorded history. Some analysts find this reassuring. Others find it terrifying.
