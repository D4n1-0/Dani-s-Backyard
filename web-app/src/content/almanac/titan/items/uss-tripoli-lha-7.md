# USS Tripoli (LHA-7)

## Overview
America-class amphibious assault ship. Displacement approximately 45,000 tonnes. Gas turbine propulsion with sustained speed 35+ knots. Capable of launching LCAC hovercraft from the well deck and CH-53K heavy-lift helicopters from the flight deck. Arrived at Grande Terre on Day 53 of the Gate era and served as the primary platform for [[Operation REEVE]].

## Role in CATF Operations
- Deployed from Grand Terre on Day 58 with [[JS Suzutsuki (DD-117)]] as escort
- Transited 8,000 km to Paradis Island under emissions control
- Launched CH-53K with ROKMC capture team on Day 65
- Received live Titan specimen on flight deck; maintained light-shrouded containment during return transit
- Currently stationed at Grande Terre as the CATF fleet's largest amphibious asset
