# Marley

## Overview
The capital and heartland of the [[Marleyan Empire]] — a continental superpower that overthrew the ancient Eldian Empire in the Great Titan War. Marley is technologically the most advanced nation in the AOT world, possessing steamship navies, railroads, firearms, radio, and early airship technology. However, its over-reliance on Titan Shifters has led to stagnation in conventional military development — a vulnerability being exploited by the [[Mid-East Allied Forces]].

Society is built on a rigid racial hierarchy: Marleyans at the top, conquered peoples in the middle, and Eldians at the bottom — confined to internment zones, used as conscript soldiers and biological weapons, subject to legalised discrimination and periodic purges. The empire's ideology holds that Eldians are devils whose ancestors brutalised the world for 1,700 years, and that their subjugation is moral justice.

## Features
- Capital city — seat of military government
- [[Liberio Internment Zone]] — confined Eldian population
- Naval dockyards — steam-age fleet
- Military academies — Warrior candidate training
- Tybur family estate — shadow rulers

## Inhabitants
- Marleyan majority
- Conquered peoples from across the empire
- Eldians in internment zones
- [[Warrior Unit]] — child soldiers wielding the empire's Titans

## Current Events
Marley is losing the Mid-East War. Its Titans are becoming obsolete against anti-titan artillery. The loss of two Titans on Paradis has created a strategic crisis. The empire is desperate — and desperation will drive its next moves toward Paradis.
