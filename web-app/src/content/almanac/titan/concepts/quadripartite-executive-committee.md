# Quadripartite Executive Committee

## Summary
The governing body of the [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)]], established under the [[Four-Power Gate Protocol]]. Four members, one per CATF nation, with consensus required for strategic decisions. Rotational operational command authority.

## Members
- [[Lee Jun-ho]] — Republic of Korea (Senior Secretary for Crisis Management, Blue House)
- [[Zheng Weimin]] — People's Republic of China (Deputy Director, Central Foreign Affairs Commission)
- [[Elizabeth Cross]] — United States (Deputy Secretary of State)
- [[Nakamura Kenji]] — Japan (Vice Foreign Minister)

## Authority
- Strategic decisions: consensus of all four members
- Rules of engagement and first-contact protocols: unanimous approval required
- Deployment of assets through [[The Gate]]: committee authorisation
- Each nation retains operational control of its own forces
- No unilateral action within the AOT exclusion zone or Earth-side perimeter
