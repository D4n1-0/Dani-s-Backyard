# Marleyan Empire

## Overview
The world's dominant superpower, having overthrown the ancient Eldian Empire during the Great Titan War (Year 743). Marley maintains global hegemony through control of seven of the Nine Titans and the brutal subjugation of mainland Eldians, whom they confine in internment zones and deploy as biological weapons. However, the empire's over-reliance on Titan power has caused severe neglect of conventional military technology — a vulnerability other nations are rapidly exploiting.

## Structure
- **Military High Command:** General staff overseeing conventional forces and the [[Warrior Unit]]
- **Warrior Unit:** Eldian child soldiers wielding Titan Shifters under [[Zeke Yeager]] (Warchief)
- **Internment Zone Administration:** confinement and conscription of Eldians
- **Tybur Family:** shadow rulers; nominal figurehead monarchy

## Assets
- Four Titan Shifters: Beast ([[Zeke Yeager|Zeke]]), Armored ([[Reiner Braun|Reiner]]), Cart (Pieck Finger), Jaw (Porco Galliard)
- War Hammer Titan ([[Willy Tybur]], dormant/unrevealed)
- Mass conscript armies of Eldian soldiers
- Global navy (age of steam/coal)
- Colonial holdings across the known world
