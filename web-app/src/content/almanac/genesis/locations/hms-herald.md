# HMS Herald

## Overview
A fast cruiser of the Faithful [[Crown of England|Royal Fleet]], newer than *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]* with sharper lines. She was dispatched from the [[Fortress of the White Cliffs]] by [[Caius Blackwood|Inquisitor-Admiral Blackwood]] to carry the first Faithful scientific-theological delegation to [[The Portal Threshold]].

## Features
- Modern Faithful cruiser design (circa 1910)
- Carries twelve unarmed delegates: [[Holy Science Academy]] researchers, [[Sacred Order of the Dragon|Order]] chaplains, and Royal Fleet officers
- Equipped with aetheric gauges, sanctified spectrometers, and the [[Holy Science Academy]] mobile laboratory

## Inhabitants
- [[Edmund Lassiter|Doctor-Abbot Lassiter]] — chief scientific delegate
- [[Father Beauchamp]] — spiritual lead (transferred from *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*)
- Royal Fleet crew

## Current Events
Anchored two thousand yards from [[The Portal Threshold]] on the Faithful side. Her boats have shuttled the Faithful delegation across into modern Earth.
