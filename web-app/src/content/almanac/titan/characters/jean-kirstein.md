# Jean Kirstein

## Overview
Soldier of the Survey Corps, member of the 104th Training Corps. Age 17. Survived the Battle of Trost (Year 850), the Uprising, and the Battle of Shiganshina. Originally motivated by a desire for a safe life in the Military Police, his experiences at Trost and during the 57th Expedition transformed him into a committed tactical leader. Known for sharp judgement under pressure, pragmatic decision-making, and a leadership style that prioritises the lives of his subordinates over ideology. Attended the first CATF–Survey Corps rendezvous as part of Hange's delegation.

## Background
Born in Trost District, Wall Rose. Joined the 104th Training Corps alongside Eren, Mikasa, Armin, and Connie. Initially clashed with Eren over matters of courage and principle, but their relationship evolved into mutual respect across years of shared combat. After Shiganshina, Jean's natural leadership instincts and tactical clarity made him one of the Survey Corps' most trusted field officers.
