# Park Do-hyun

## Description
Thirty-one years old. *Gyeongwi* (Lieutenant) in the [[Korea Coast Guard]]. Career officer, never seen combat. Lean build, clean-shaven, dark hair worn short. Speaks functional Maritime English and passable Japanese. Commands [[KCG 506]], a Taegeuk-class medium patrol vessel (500 tons, 28 crew). Three days into a routine fisheries patrol when [[The Gate]] opens.

## Background
Graduate of the Korea Coast Guard Academy. Served on patrol vessels in the Yellow Sea for six years. Familiar with the cat-and-mouse game of Chinese fishing incursions and maritime boundary disputes, but has never faced anything beyond bureaucratic brinkmanship. His defining trait is composure under pressure — when the Gate appears, he is terrified, but his crew follows his orders because his voice does not shake.

## Relationships
- Senior Petty Officer [[Kang Min-jun]] — watch officer; trusted subordinate
- [[Chen Shaoqiang]] — Chinese counterpart; adversarial but mutually respectful through the standoff

## Current Status
On station at [[The Gate Threshold]] for seven days. Designated co-enforcer of the exclusion zone alongside [[Chen Shaoqiang]]. Primary operator of the first UAV and ROV deployments through the Gate. Has not slept a full night since the Gate opened. His English has improved through necessity; his partnership with Chen has deepened into something neither government anticipated.
