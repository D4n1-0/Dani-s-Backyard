# Grail-spore

## Summary
A dangerous bioweapon of the Cult of the Black Grail, unleashed by Beelzebub during the Corpse Wars.

## Details
Grail-spores are metastatic agents that infect living tissue, transforming victims into vessels for the Black Grail. A Grail-spore bloom is a visible sign of widespread corruption. The weapon caused tens of millions of casualties during the Corpse Wars (1346–1429).

## Context
Captain Roth of HMS *Retribution* recalls witnessing a Grail-spore bloom, indicating the Crown of England has faced this weapon first-hand.
