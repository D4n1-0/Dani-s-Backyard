# Heinrich Vogt

## Overview
Private, Garrison Regiment cavalry auxiliary. Age 18. Seven months out of the Training Corps, assigned to a patrol unit monitoring the corridor between Wall Rose's eastern gate and the new Paradisian railway. Served as the point-of-view character for the first contact event in [[Chapter 06 – A Voice in the Dark]]: discovered the [[First Contact Pod]], called for [[Armin Arlert]], guarded the pod through the night, and waited three days for the rendezvous party to return. His decision to report rather than ignore the anomalous object set the entire first contact sequence in motion.

## Background
GreW up in a farming village in Wall Rose territory. Lost relatives to Titan incursions before the Wall Maria reclamation. Joined the Training Corps at 17, graduated mid-tier, assigned to the Garrison because the Survey Corps intake was frozen post-Shiganshina. Considers himself unremarkable — a competent horseman, an average marksman, a soldier who does his job without distinction. The events of the first contact permanently altered his self-assessment.
