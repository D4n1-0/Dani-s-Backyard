# USS Annapolis (SSN-760)

## Description
A Los Angeles-class nuclear-powered fast attack submarine, commissioned into the United States Navy. Displacement: ~6,900 tonnes submerged. Armament: Mk 48 torpedoes, Tomahawk land-attack cruise missiles. Propulsion: S6G nuclear reactor driving a single shaft. The *Annapolis* is designed for covert operations — acoustic signature reduction, anechoic coating, passive sonar suites that can track surface contacts at ranges exceeding fifty nautical miles. In any naval theatre she is a hunter. In the AOT world, she is an impossibility.

## Function
Shadow vessel for the CATF Marley contact mission. Ordered to trail the three-ship diplomatic group from Grande Terre to Odiha at periscope depth — approximately two hundred metres below the surface, undetectable by any Marleyan sensing capability. Her orders were to remain silent, remain submerged, and remain invisible. In the event the contact turned hostile — Marleyan forces attempting to board or seize the CATF vessels — the *Annapolis* was authorised to surface, extract the diplomatic delegation, and withdraw. No offensive action was authorised. No engagement was planned. The boat was insurance, not an instrument. Her presence was known to [[Satomi Ishihara|Dr. Satomi Ishihara]] alone among the delegation.

## History
Assigned to the CATF task group under classified annex of the Quadripartite Committee's force posture agreement. Her transit from Pearl Harbor to the Gate took eighteen days. She has been operating in the AOT world's southern ocean since, maintaining a continuous presence as CATF's silent strategic hedge. At Odiha, she held position beyond the breakwater for the duration of the contact — sonar array painting the harbour, weapons systems cold, the captain drinking coffee and watching contacts and waiting for a signal that never came.
