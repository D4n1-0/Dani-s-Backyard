# Sarah Vance

## Description
Commander in the Royal Navy Operations Directorate. Analytical, pragmatic, the calm voice in the room during a crisis. Works out of [[Northwood Headquarters]].

## Background
[[Sarah Vance|Vance]] is the senior operations officer coordinating the modern British response to [[The Anomaly]]. She receives [[Alice Hartley|Hartley]]'s report, directs the deployment of *[[HMS Dragon|HMS Dragon]]*, *[[HMS Defender|Defender]]*, and *[[HMS Duncan|Duncan]]*, and authorises the first contact signal protocol.

## Relationships
- [[Alice Hartley]] — received her initial report
- [[James Hawthorne]] — gave him his orders via the chain of command

## Current Status
At Northwood Headquarters, overseeing the ongoing standoff.
