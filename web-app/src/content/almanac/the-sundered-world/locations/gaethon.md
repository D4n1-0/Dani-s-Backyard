# Gaethon

## Overview

Gaethon is the world of the Sundered Realms — an Earth-like planet whose landmasses splinter into three major continents and countless archipelagos, all watched over by the central continent-island of [[Kaelen]]. It is a world of vast oceans, continental interiors, mountain catacomb-cities, vitrified craters, and the ever-present hum of surveillance.

## Features

### The Three Continents

- **[[Occidens]]** — the sunset expanse. Temperate and varied, home to the maritime empire of the [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]].
- **[[Oriens]]** — the dawn cradle. Burnt-forest highlands and mineral-rich prairies, home to the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] and the [[Iron Concordat]].
- **[[Meridies]]** — the scorched south. Equatorial coastlines and trade routes, home to the [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] and the industrial catacombs of the [[Technate of Vorr]].

### The Tethyan Ocean

The central ocean of Gaethon, named for the ancient sea of a forgotten mythology. At its heart sits Kaelen, the Victor Island. The Tethyan periphery is the world's great shipping corridor — Valdran-flagged airships and pre-dreadnought squadrons trace careful routes that never approach within visual range of Kaelen's sea-wall.

### The Wound

The [[The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr|Sunken Kingdom of Myrr]] — a shallow sea of vitrified craters and radioactive islands where a peninsula once stood. A pilgrimage site for the obedient and a refuge for the desperate. The [[The Unbound|Unbound]] do their most dangerous work here.

## Inhabitants

Gaethon's total population is unknown — the [[The Census|census]] caps make accurate counting a political impossibility — but estimates range from 800 million to 1.2 billion. The five great powers dominate geopolitics, but dozens of smaller states, protectorates, and ungoverned zones fill the gaps.

## Current Events

Year 312 Post-Convergence. The world is tense. The Technate of Vorr has tested a stratospheric airship. The Concordat masses troops on the Amber League's border. Valdris debates diplomacy with Kaelen. And in the cratered wastes of Myrr, an Unbound cell believes it can decode the [[The Kaelic Melody|melody]].
