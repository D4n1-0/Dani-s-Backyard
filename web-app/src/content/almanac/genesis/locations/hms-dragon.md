# *[[HMS Dragon|HMS Dragon]]*

## Overview
A Type 45 destroyer of the modern [[Royal Navy (Modern)|Royal Navy]]. Lead ship of the three-vessel response to [[The Anomaly]]. Grey, low-profile, faceted tower amidships, armed with Sea Viper missiles.

## Features
- Modern signal lamps and VHF communications
- Rigid-hulled inflatable boat (RIB) used for the first contact party
- Wildcat helicopter embarked

## Inhabitants
- [[James Hawthorne]] — captain
- [[Rebecca Osei]] — first contact officer

## Current Events
Holding station five kilometres east of [[The Anomaly]], maintaining the standoff.
