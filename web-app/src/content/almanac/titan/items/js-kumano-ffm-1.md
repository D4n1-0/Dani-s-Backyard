# JS Kumano (FFM-1)

## Description
Lead ship of the Mogami-class — a 133-metre multi-mission frigate of the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force. Commissioned 2022. Displacement ~5,500 tonnes, combined diesel-and-gas propulsion. Armed with a 127 mm main gun, vertical launch system, anti-ship missiles, and a mine-hunting capability — all concealed within angular stealth panels. Crew complement of approximately 90. Smaller and more nimble than an AEGIS destroyer, designed for forward presence, patrol, and escort rather than fleet air defence.

## Function
The first permanent CATF warship stationed on Marleyan soil — forward-deployed to the [[South Bank (Odiha)]] pier under the [[CATF–Marley Memorandum of Understanding]]. Her mission is presence: patrolling the southern approaches, coordinating with Marleyan naval command on search and rescue, and serving as the command platform for the CATF military advisory cell. Her arrival replaced the rotating US destroyer escort that had accompanied the *Maersk* delivery. The *Kumano* is a statement of permanence — a CATF warship tied up at a CATF pier on AOT mainland soil.
