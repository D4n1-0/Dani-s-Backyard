# Joyce Messier

## Description

*Senior negotiator and executive for the Wild Pines Group. Elegant, composed, ruthless in the way only the truly powerful can afford to be — calm, unhurried, never raising her voice. Speaks with the polished Revacholian French of the Moralintern's executive tier. Smokes constantly, a prop as much as a habit. Her kindness is a weapon; her patience is a threat.*

## Background

*Wild Pines' lead representative in Martinaise during the dockworkers' strike. Hired mercenaries from Krenel to protect company interests, a decision that led directly to the Tribunal and the deaths of the Hardie Boys. Negotiated the Coalition's intervention to retake the terminal when the strike demands escalated to "every worker is a board member." Has a personal and professional hold over Lukas Delacroix — recruited him from Koenigstein as a second-year intern, groomed him through the remainder of his studies, advanced his career at unnatural speed, and maintained a Polaroid archive from every session across three years. She did not simply hire him. She built him. She documents him.*

## Relationships

- [[Lukas Delacroix]] — *Protégé, engineer, compromised asset. She owns him and values him in that order.*
- [[Henrik Varga|Commander Varga]] — *INSURCOM officer. A service provider — paid, professional, beneath her.*
- [[Evrart Claire]] — *Union adversary. The only person in Martinaise she treats as an equal threat.*

## Current Status

*On-site in Martinaise, having overseen the terminal's reclamation. Leaving for Grand Couron to handle the human side of the security operation — guard hiring, operating protocols, the policy architecture that translates Lukas's hardware into enforceable control. She builds the rules; he builds the cage.*
