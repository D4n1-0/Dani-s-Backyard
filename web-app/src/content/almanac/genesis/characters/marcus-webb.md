# Marcus Webb

## Overview
CIA intelligence analyst assigned to Carrier Strike Group Two as the USIC liaison during the portal crisis. Mid-level. A career analyst who spent fifteen years in the Directorate of Intelligence before being seconded to the fleet — not a field operative, not a political appointee, but a man who reads patterns and writes assessments that no one reads until they need them. He proposed the Ark Strategy during the post-collision council.

## Personality
Webb is quiet, meticulous, and acutely uncomfortable with social silence. He processes out loud when the room lets him; he shuts down when it doesn't. His insight is genuine — he sees strategic leverage where others see only moral dilemmas — but he lacks the political instincts to know when an idea is *too* sharp, *too* cold, to be spoken aloud. The awkwardness that follows his proposal in the council chamber is a cost he did not anticipate and does not know how to absorb.

## Role in the Coalition
Webb's job is technical: assess the Faithful Crown's military capability, map its command structure, evaluate the Heretic Legion threat from signals intelligence. He was not supposed to propose grand strategy. But the pattern was there — in the intercepts, in the Faithful responses to the collision, in the theological language that saturated every transmission — and he named it before anyone else could. His proposal became the Ark Strategy. He has not decided whether he is proud of this or ashamed.

## Relationships
- [[Terrence Kohler]] — Kohler brought him aboard the strike group. Webb respects Kohler's operational judgment and trusts him to read an assessment without flinching.
- [[Stephen Davenport]] — Webb sees Davenport as the man who will have to carry the moral weight of the Ark Strategy. He does not envy him.
