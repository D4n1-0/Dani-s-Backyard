# Undersea Sensor Array

## Summary
A network of forty passive acoustic hydrophone sensors laid in a 100-kilometre arc north of [[The Gate]] on the AOT side. Connected by armoured fibre-optic cable to a processing station on the seafloor near the [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]]. Designed for dual purpose: acoustic surveillance of AOT-native maritime traffic (specifically Marleyan steamships) and perimeter security around the Gate.

## Capabilities
- Detection range: ~200 kilometres for single reciprocating steam engines
- Passive only — no active sonar, zero emission footprint
- Continuously monitored by CATF acoustic analysts
- First contact detected Day 22: routine steamship traffic 185km north
- Detected the *Pelikan* on Day 33 — single reciprocating steam engine, irregular cadence, course south toward the islands

## Deployment
- Laying commenced Week 3 of CATF operations
- Achieved initial operating capability Day 22
- Cables buried and nodes bottom-mounted for minimum detectability
