# Jean-Paul Delacroix

## Overview
France's ambassador to NATO during the first contact crisis. A career diplomat who executes the legal strategy designed by [[Étienne Morel]]'s ministry. At the emergency North Atlantic Council session, he presents the formal French position: the portal is in international waters under UNCLOS, no single nation holds jurisdiction, and a working group on jurisdiction must precede any command structure.

## Personality
Delacroix is cool, precise, and legally rigorous. He does not raise his voice. He does not need to. His arguments are built on treaty text and precedent, and he deploys them with the confidence of a man who knows the law is on his side.
