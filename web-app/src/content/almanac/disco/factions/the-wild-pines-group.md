# The Wild Pines Group

## Overview
An international corporate conglomerate responsible for logistics and shipping at the Greater Revachol Industrial Harbour (GRIH). The primary antagonist of the [[Dockworkers' Union]] during the recent strike. Represents ultraliberal corporate interests in [[Revachol]].

## Structure
- Corporate hierarchy with local operators (Joyce Messier is their Revachol representative)
- Mercenary security contractors
- Shipping and logistics infrastructure at GRIH

## Assets
- International reach and capital
- Mercenary forces (deployed during the strike)
- Control of supply-chain infrastructure
- Access to the Moralintern-adjacent networks

## Relationships
- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — direct economic conflict; Union demands board seats for workers
- [[Coalition of Nations]] — benefits from Coalition's laissez-faire economic policies
- [[Moralist International]] — aligned with the economic status quo the Moralintern maintains
- [[Joyce Messier]] — local representative and liaison
