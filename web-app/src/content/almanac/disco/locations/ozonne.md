# Ozonne

## Summary
An archipelago 80–100 kilometres from [[Revachol]]. Site of the mass graves where the [[Coalition of Nations|Coalition]] buried communards, loyalists, and civilians after the Landing of '08. Major cities: Fond de l'Air, Virmandeux.
