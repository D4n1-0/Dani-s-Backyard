# Alfred Faure Forward Operating Base

## Overview
The CATF forward operating base on the [[Inverted Alfred Faure Islands]], approximately 20 kilometres west of [[The Gate]] on the AOT side. Constructed over Weeks 2–5 of CATF operations under the [[Construction Progress]] timeline, commanded by Captain [[Marcus Rivera]] (USN Civil Engineer Corps).

The FOB is the forward edge of CATF's AOT-side presence — the closest installation to the Gate threshold and the first point of defence. Its expeditionary runway enables C-130J Hercules cargo aircraft to transit the Gate and deliver heavy equipment directly to AOT soil.

## Construction Timeline
- **Day 15 (Week 2):** First LCAC lands SEABEE detachment at 03:17. Three prefab shelters erected. Runway survey begins.
- **Day 16:** Bulldozer, rock crusher, desalination unit arrive.
- **Day 20:** Expeditionary runway compacted and tested with light aircraft.
- **Day 24:** First C-130J transits Gate and lands — runway fully operational.
- **Day 25–28:** Helicopter pad, field hospital, and vehicle depot completed.
- **Day 30:** Full operational capability — 500 personnel, all modules, satellite terminal linked to Grande Terre via undersea fibre.
- **Day 33:** Fuel farms reach full capacity.

## Features
- **Expeditionary runway:** 1,200 metres of compacted volcanic scree, C-130J-capable
- **Helicopter pad:** two MH-60S Seahawks on permanent rotation
- **Field hospital:** level I trauma care, quarantine capability
- **Vehicle maintenance depot**
- **Signals station:** linked to Grande Terre via undersea fibre, to Earth via satellite relay
- **Fuel farms:** 500,000 litres diesel and aviation fuel in bladder tanks
- **Personnel:** 500 personnel across four nations, rotating through 90-day tours
- **Perimeter:** defended by infantry company and drone surveillance grid

## Inhabitants
- CATF multinational military and support personnel
- No native population (islands were uninhabited pre-CATF)

## Current Events
Fully operational as of Day 30. The *Pelikan* crew were intercepted approaching the FOB on Day 36 — the first contact event of the Gate era. The FOB remains undetected by the broader AOT world.
