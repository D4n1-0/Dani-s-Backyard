# Iron Concordat

## Overview

The Iron Concordat is the great land predator of the Scattered Realms — a stratocratic oligarchy ruling the prairies and highlands of the [[The Vorsteppes|Vorsteppes]] in northern [[Oriens]]. It wages limited wars of absorption, peels off border territories from weaker neighbours, and exploits every grey zone in the [[Kaelen Mandate]] with an audacity that alarms its rivals. Its culture venerates the [[The Iron Saint|Iron Saint]], and its youth are raised on the dream that humanity will one day match [[Kaelen]]'s might.

## Structure

- **Marshal-Preceptor:** Supreme commander, elected by the council of corps commanders. Serves for life or until removed by unanimous vote.
- **Council of Corps Commanders:** Seven generals, each commanding a corps of approximately 35,000. The true ruling body.
- **Homesteader Collectives:** Nominally independent agricultural and industrial settlements that supply the Concordat's war machine — the foundation of [[Grey-Zone Manufacturing]].

## Assets

- **Army:** 250,000 long-service professionals — the cap maintained with implacable precision. Every soldier is a career warrior. Equipment includes semi-automatic battle rifles, light machine guns, and the *[[Cavalier Light Tank|Cavalier]]*-series light tanks (riveted hulls, 47mm guns).
- **Cavalry:** The only major power to maintain a significant mounted arm. Horse cavalry screens the flanks against unarmoured foes — still viable in the steppe warfare the Concordat prefers.
- **Grey-Zone Industrial Base:** Dispersed factories across homesteader collectives producing war materiel beyond what a single state of 250,000 soldiers would normally require.

## Relationships

- [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] — pragmatic trade; Valdran shipping moves Concordat exports, but no formal alliance
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — mortal ideological enemy; the Theocracy's theology of submission is the Concordat's anathema
- [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] — current target; 200,000 troops massed on the League's hinterland demanding "transit rights"
- [[Technate of Vorr]] — unspoken affinity; both push Mandate boundaries, but the Concordat does it with tanks and the Technate with physics
- [[The Unbound]] — plausible deniability; the Concordat does not officially support the resistance, but Unbound cells receive "lost" shipments of equipment with suspicious regularity
