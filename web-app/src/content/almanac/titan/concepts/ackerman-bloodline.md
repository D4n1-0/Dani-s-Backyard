# Ackerman Bloodline

## Summary
A genetically modified bloodline created by ancient Titan science to serve as Eldian royal bodyguards — immune to Founding Titan memory manipulation and incapable of Titanisation, yet able to access Titan-level combat prowess through an "awakening."

## Details
Ackermans are Subjects of Ymir connected to the Paths but genetically locked against the Founder's control. When awakened — triggered by extreme survival instinct and a command from a perceived host — they gain instantaneous access to the accumulated combat experience of every past Ackerman. They become, in effect, a Titan's destructive power compressed into human form.

Known living Ackermans: [[Levi Ackerman]], [[Mikasa Ackerman]]. Both were persecuted by the old royal government specifically because they could not be memory-wiped.

## Context
For the modern world, Ackermans represent a secondary biological anomaly — super-soldiers that violate known human physical limits, yet are resistant to the very mechanism (Founding Titan control) that makes the rest of Paradis's population a strategic liability. They are potentially invaluable allies or nightmares to contain.
