# Free Cities of the Amber Coast

## Overview

The Free Cities of the Amber Coast are a loose mercantile league of prosperous port-cities along the northern shore of [[Meridies]]. They are the world's economic artery — their airship yards at [[Hanavel]], their telegraph cables, and their merchant banks connect the continents. Their neutrality is purchased by generous tribute to any power that threatens their caravans. Their true genius is information: the Coin-Lords maintain a vast spy network that also obsessively catalogues [[Kaelen]]'s leaked signals.

## Structure

- **High Syndic:** Rotating annual chair, elected by the Syndics' Council. Authority is limited — the High Syndic can convene and propose but cannot compel.
- **Syndics' Council:** Representatives from each member city. Voting weight proportional to tribute contribution.
- **Coin-Lords:** The true power — merchant princes who control the banking houses. They do not hold office but fund those who do.
- **City Guards:** Each member city fields a household guard of 2,000–10,000. Combined, the League could field 300,000 men — exceeding the Mandate's cap — but each city is a sovereign entity, a legal fiction Kaelen has tolerated.

## Assets

- **[[Hanavel]] Airship Yards:** The greatest lighter-than-air construction facility on [[Gaethon]], producing the magnificent *[[Cloud-King-class Zeppelin|Cloud-King]]*-class passenger zeppelins.
- **Telegraph Network:** Trans-oceanic cables carrying commercial and diplomatic traffic. The League charges by the word and reads every message.
- **The Signal Tower:** A directional aerial array maintained by the [[The Signal Seekers|Signal Seekers]], receiving and recording Kaelen's emissions.
- **Intelligence Network:** The Coin-Lords' merchant-spies operate in every major port and capital city.

## Relationships

- [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] — closest economic partner; Valdran naval protection for trade access
- [[Iron Concordat]] — currently threatened; 200,000 troops on the hinterland
- [[Technate of Vorr]] — wary trading partner; Vorr buys envelope materials from Hanavel, pays in refined metals
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — tolerated at arm's length; the Inquisition is banned from League territory, but individual city governments sometimes grant access under pressure
- [[The Signal Seekers]] — hosted within the League; the Seekers' monastery at Hanavel operates under League protection
