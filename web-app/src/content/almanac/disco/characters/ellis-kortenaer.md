# Ellis Kortenaer

## Summary
An Oranjese citizen, born ~'09, found in a leaf compactor and signed into Lelystad County Neonatal Care Unit. Became a mercenary. Sent to [[Martinaise]] to investigate [[Klaasje Amandou]]. Found hanged outside the [[Whirling-in-Rags]], 4 March '51. Shot by [[Iosef Lilianovich Dros]] before the hanging — the Deserter's final act of the [[Antecentennial Revolution]].
