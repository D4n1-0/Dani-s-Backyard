# Paradis Island

## Overview
A large island nation surrounded by ocean, home to the last remnants of the Eldian people who retreated behind three concentric Walls (Maria, Rose, Sina) one century ago under King Karl Fritz. The island contains massive reserves of [[Iceburst Stone]] and millions of dormant Colossal Titans embedded within the Walls themselves. Population of roughly one million, concentrated within Wall Rose and Wall Sina after the fall of Wall Maria.

The island is technologically backward — approximately 1920s-equivalent in some areas, medieval in others — having been deliberately isolated from the outside world by the Reiss monarchy's memory manipulation and propaganda. No navy exists. No aviation. No radio. The people of Paradis only learned the outside world survived beyond the Walls at the basement of Grisha Yeager.

## Features
- **Wall Sina:** innermost; contains Mitras, the capital
- **Wall Rose:** middle ring; most population centres, agricultural land
- **Wall Maria:** outermost; recently reclaimed, territory cleared of Titans
- **Shiganshina District:** southern gate of Wall Maria; site of the breach and the final battle
- **Underground City:** beneath Mitras; former refuge for the destitute
- **Iceburst Stone deposits:** deep underground volcanic calderas, frozen by the Founder's power

## Inhabitants
- ~1 million Eldians (Subjects of Ymir)
- Government: Military junta under Premier [[Darius Zachary]], Queen [[Historia Reiss]]
- Military: [[Survey Corps]], Military Police, Garrison
- Titan Shifters: [[Eren Yeager]], [[Armin Arlert]]; crystalised [[Annie Leonhart]]

## Current Events
Post-Shiganshina. The truth of the world is now known to the military leadership. Wall Maria has been cleared of Titans and resettlement is underway. Paradise is frantically preparing for a war it barely understands against an enemy it has never seen — an enemy that has already breached its walls once and will come again.
