# Elias Vogler

## Overview
Physical oceanographer, age 39. Published seventeen papers on the circulation of the world's oceans. Led the Marleyan Academy of Sciences polar expedition aboard the *Pelikan* — a 47-day voyage to map the AOT world's northern ice shelf. The expedition drifted catastrophically off course, carried into the CATF exclusion perimeter by currents his navigational charts could not explain.

Intercepted by CATF on Day 36 and interviewed by [[Tanaka Kenji|Lieutenant Colonel Tanaka Kenji]]. Given full disclosure: the Gate, the other Earth, the five-week survey, the drones that mapped his entire world. Accepted CATF employment as a scientific consultant. Works with [[Ahn Jae-hyun|Dr. Ahn Jae-hyun]] and [[Wang Lijuan|Dr. Wang Lijuan]] on the joint survey.

A genuine empiricist first, Marleyan patriot second. Has seen enough of his empire's internal contradictions — Liberio, the Eldian camps, the war — to question the ideology he was raised in. The first AOT native to willingly cooperate with CATF.
