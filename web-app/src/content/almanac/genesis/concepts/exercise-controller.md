# Exercise Controller (EXCON)

## Summary
The command authority responsible for managing a military exercise — injecting scenarios, adjudicating outcomes, and controlling the flow of the drill. In the opening of [[Chapter 06 – The Order to Sail]], the EXCON releases the Truman and her screen from the exercise serial when the real-world order arrives from [[US Fleet Forces Command|FFC]].

## Details
The EXCON's role ends when the exercise ends. The release of the Truman from the drill — the moment when the exercise controller says *Understood, Truman. Good hunting* and steps aside — marks the transition from training to reality. The EXCON is the last voice of the old world before the new world begins.
