# Pale

## Summary
The separative tissue between isolas — an achromatic, odourless, featureless nothing that covers 72% of [[Elysium]]'s surface, expanding at an unknown rate, and intrinsically tied to human consciousness.

## Details
The Pale suspends properties: physical, epistemological, linguistic. The further in, the steeper the suspension — numbers stop working at the "number barrier." Transit requires aerostatic airships and pale latitude compressors. Over-radiation from prolonged exposure causes mental degradation; civilians are limited to 6 days/year of Pale travel.

## Context
The Pale is simultaneously a physical hazard and a metaphysical phenomenon. It carries echoes of the past and, rarely, transmissions from the future. The Moralintern maintains repeater stations, contingency spreads, and surveillance over entroponetic anomalies. Dialectical materialists believe it is "manifested past" — rarefied history, not rarefied matter.
