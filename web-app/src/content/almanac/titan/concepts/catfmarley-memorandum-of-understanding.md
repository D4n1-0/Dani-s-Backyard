# CATF–Marley Memorandum of Understanding

## Summary
The first bilateral agreement between the [[Marleyan Empire]] and the [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)]], signed in Admiral [[Konrad Verner]]'s office at [[Odiha]] on Day ~138. Signed by Commander [[Theo Magath]] (as authorised representative of the Marleyan General Staff) and Dr. [[Satomi Ishihara]] (as Chief of Mission, CATF). Five pages, five principal articles plus a verbal annex on Paradis.

## Articles

### Article One — South Bank Lease
A 99-year renewable lease on approximately forty square kilometres of undeveloped coast on the south bank of the Odiha River mouth. Marleyan sovereignty maintained; CATF operational authority within the compound perimeter. The compound is a leased facility under Marleyan jurisdiction, not a diplomatic mission or territorial exclave.

### Article Two — Technology Transfer
Phased technology access:
- **Phase 1 (immediate):** Medical supplies and equipment — morphine, antibiotics, antiseptics, surgical tools, basic diagnostic devices
- **Phase 2 (3 months):** Radio communications — relay station at the compound linking Marleyan naval command to the southern fleet in real time
- **Phase 3 (6 months):** Military advisory cell — CATF liaison officers available for consultation on conventional military modernisation

### Article Three — Trade Compact
Marleyan merchants authorised to access Gate trade lanes under CATF oversight. CATF goods available to Marleyan purchasers at negotiated rates. Includes a standing weapons procurement agreement — small arms, ammunition, communications equipment, transport vehicles — subject to CATF nation export controls.

### Article Four — Recognition
CATF formally recognises Marleyan sovereignty over its claimed territory and waters. The south bank facility is not a territorial claim. The Gate is not a territorial claim. CATF does not contest Marleyan borders or support insurgencies.

### Article Five — Paradis (Verbal)
Not in the signed text; recorded in session minutes. CATF discloses its existing engagement with [[Paradis Island|Paradis]], refuses to discuss details, and establishes that its Paradisian relationship is independent and not subject to Marleyan conditions. Marley records the engagement as a concern and reserves the right to raise it through the established channel.

## Strategic Significance
The MOU transforms CATF's presence in the AOT world from a covert operation into a legally recognised bilateral relationship with the AOT world's dominant power. The south bank compound provides CATF with its first permanent forward base on the AOT mainland — reducing logistical pressure on [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub|Grande Terre]] (3,000 km south) and establishing a diplomatic and operational bridgehead for future engagement. For Marley, the technology transfer and trade compact represent the first meaningful external support for its struggling conventional military modernisation — a shift away from Titan dependency that Commander Magath has advocated for years.
