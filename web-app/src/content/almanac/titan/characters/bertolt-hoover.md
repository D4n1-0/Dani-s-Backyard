# Bertolt Hoover

## Description
Former holder of the Colossal Titan. Tall, dark-haired, perpetually anxious. Killed at Shiganshina — consumed by Armin Arlert in his Pure Titan form, a death he begged his former comrades to spare him from. His Colossal now lives in Armin.

## Background
Warrior candidate alongside Reiner, Annie, and Marcel. The quiet one — his destructive power was immense but his will was fragile. Breached Wall Maria at age 12 on Marley's orders. Carried the guilt in silence for five years. At the end, he accepted his role with something resembling clarity: "No one is in the wrong. There's just nothing we can do."

## Relationships
- [[Reiner Braun]] — best friend; Bertolt's death haunts Reiner
- [[Annie Leonhart]] — fellow Warrior; unspoken affection
- [[Armin Arlert]] — Armin now carries Bertolt's Titan and his memories

## Current Status
Dead. Consumed by Armin at Shiganshina. His memories live on in the Colossal Titan.
