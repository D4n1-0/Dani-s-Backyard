# Insulindian Phasmid

## Summary
A cryptid of the order Phasmatodea — extraordinarily well-camouflaged — that was photographed alive by [[Harrier Du Bois]] and [[Kim Kitsuragi]] in [[Martinaise]], March '51. Speaking through Harry's mind, it revealed that the [[Pale]] is a human creation and that the natural world regards humanity as an existential threat.

## Details
Existence proven: previously thought extinct or mythical. The phasmid's dialogue (experienced through Harry's perception) is the single most significant revelation about the Pale's origin: "The pale, too, came with you. No one remembers it before you. The cnidarians do not, the radially symmetricals do not. There is an almost unanimous agreement between the birds and the plants that you are going to destroy us all."

## Context
The phasmid is both a scientific discovery (cryptozoology validated) and a metaphysical one (the Pale as "human pollution" confirmed). It is unclear whether the scientific community will accept the evidence, but the Moralintern surely knows.
