# The Unbound

## Overview

The Unbound are a cellular resistance network operating in the shadows of the [[The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr|Sunken Kingdom of Myrr]] and deep catacombs across the Scattered Realms. Founded by defecting [[The Thought Cabinet|Thought Cabinet]] members from the [[Technate of Vorr]], ex-Myrrian refugees who survived the destruction of their homeland, and heretics fleeing the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame|Last Flame]]'s Inquisition, they are the only organised attempt to actively break the [[Kaelen Mandate]] rather than merely bend it.

## Structure

- **Cellular Network:** No central leadership. Cells of 3–12 members operate independently. Contact between cells is mediated by "Couriers" who know only their immediate contacts.
- **The Founders' Council:** A mythologised founding body whose original members are mostly dead or in hiding. Their hand-copied circuit diagrams and theoretical papers are the Unbound's sacred texts.
- **Technical Cadres:** Scientists, engineers, and mathematicians working in hidden laboratories. The only people on [[Gaethon]] actively attempting to develop post-Mandate technology.

## Assets

- **The Cavity Magnetron Diagram:** A single, hand-copied circuit diagram — the blueprint for radar — venerated as a holy relic. Passed from cell to cell in a steel tube. No working prototype has been built.
- **Null-Field Generator (Theoretical):** A concept for a device that could saturate satellite receivers with noise, theoretically blinding the [[Eyes of Heaven]] without violating the ban on radar (it is a jamming device, not a detection device — or so the legal argument goes). No prototype has been tested.
- **Safehouses:** Hidden laboratories and meeting rooms in the Deep Fjords of Myrr, the catacombs of the [[The Vorran Massif|Vorran Massif]], and the cellars of sympathetic merchants in the [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast|Amber Coast]].
- **The Melody Project:** An active effort to decode the [[The Kaelic Melody]] and, potentially, broadcast a response. The risk is incalculable. The Unbound consider it humanity's best hope.

## Relationships

- [[Technate of Vorr]] — hidden origin; the Thought Cabinet likely still provides covert support
- [[Iron Concordat]] — plausible deniability; "lost" supply shipments reach Unbound cells with suspicious regularity
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — hunted enemy; the Inquisition considers Unbound members the highest-priority heretics
- [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] — officially condemned as terrorists; unofficially, some Valdran intelligence officers maintain contact
- [[Kaelen]] — the target; the Unbound's entire purpose is to find a way to neutralise or circumvent the Mandate
