# Holy Science Academy

## Summary
The Faithful institution blending scientific inquiry with theological rigour — the techno-clergy of the Trench Crusade world. Its members wear grey cassocks and carry sanctified instruments for measuring both physical and metaphysical phenomena.

## Details
The Academy studies the natural and supernatural world through a lens that treats faith and empiricism as inseparable. Its researchers reverse-engineer captured Heretic machinery, develop orichalcum alloys, and investigate anomalies that resist purely theological classification. In the Genesis scenario, [[Edmund Lassiter|Doctor-Abbot Lassiter]] leads the Academy's mobile contingent to study [[The Anomaly]].

## Context
The Academy represents the TC world's unique fusion of science and religion — instruments are blessed, experiments are accompanied by prayer, and discovery is understood as an act of worship.
