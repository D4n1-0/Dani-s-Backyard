# Technate of Vorr

## Overview

The Technate of Vorr lives at the very edge of the [[Kaelen Mandate|Edict]] — a technocratic directorate ruling the mineral-rich [[The Vorran Massif|Vorran Massif]] in southern [[Meridies]]. Its factories are cathedrals of steam and steel. Its engineers have built biplane fighters with metal monocoque fuselages, "land ironclads" with sloped armour, and 105mm howitzers that reach *exactly* 9.9 kilometres range. Its cities descend into the mountains in deliberate provocation of the [[The Census|census cap]]. And deep within its highest peak, the [[The Thought Cabinet|Thought Cabinet]] preserves forbidden knowledge — theoretical physics, transistor logic, jet propulsion — waiting for the day the iron cage rusts.

## Structure

- **Board of Applied Industries:** A seven-member technocratic directorate. Membership is by co-option — existing members select successors based on technical achievement.
- **Industrial Guilds:** Organised labour and management by sector — metallurgy, chemistry, mechanics, armaments. Guild masters sit on advisory councils.
- **The Thought Cabinet:** A clandestine order of mathematicians and theoretical physicists who, using only paper and ink, have deduced the principles of jet propulsion, transistor logic, and radar. Membership is secret; knowledge is passed as a holy succession.

## Assets

- **Vorran Artillerie Corps:** 250,000 personnel, famous for the [[Vorran 105mm Howitzer|105mm howitzers]] with clockwork fire-control computers and exactly 9.9 km maximum range.
- **Industrial Base:** Steam-powered factories stamping out biplane fighters, light tanks, howitzers, and precision instruments. The most advanced industrial economy permitted under the Mandate.
- **Microwave Telegraph Network:** An experimental relay system operating at frequencies just below radar bands. Technically not radar.
- **Stratospheric Airship Programme:** A meteorological research platform that lingers near [[Silent Chorus]] drone altitudes — officially civilian, practically a provocation.
- **Catacomb-Cities:** Urban centres built downward into mountains, with populations deliberately ambiguous.

## Relationships

- [[Iron Concordat]] — unspoken affinity; both push Mandate boundaries, but wary of each other's ambitions
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — existential enemy; the Theocracy's Inquisition would burn every Thought Cabinet member if it could find them
- [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] — nervous diplomatic distance; Valdris fears the Technate will trigger a response that endangers everyone
- [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] — trading partner; Hanavel supplies envelope materials, Vorr pays in refined metals
- [[The Unbound]] — hidden support; the Unbound were founded by defecting Thought Cabinet members and likely still receive covert assistance
