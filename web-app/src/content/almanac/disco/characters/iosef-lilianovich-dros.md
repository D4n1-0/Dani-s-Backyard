# Iosef Lilianovich Dros

## Summary
The last communist. Born ~'92 in the Last Century. Recruited into the ICM in '07, trained at the École de Contrôle Aérien. Consigned to emergency defence duties in '08. Survived the Landing. Went into hiding. Killed the mercenary Ellis Kortenaer from the [[Sea Fortress]], 44 years later. The final act of the [[Antecentennial Revolution]].

## Background
After the Landing, Dros hid on Résurrection until it became a spa in '18. Then on islet E48 until the sea washed over it in '29. Then spent 22 years in the Sea Fortress in [[Martinaise]]. He watched. He waited. He shot Kortenaer out of ideological rage — the mercenary represented everything the Commune died to destroy.

## Relationships
- [[Ellis Kortenaer]] — killed by Dros
- [[Harrier Du Bois]] — the detective who found him

## Current Status
Arrested by the [[Revachol Citizens Militia|RCM]]. Awaiting his fate. The last man still fighting a war that ended 43 years ago.
