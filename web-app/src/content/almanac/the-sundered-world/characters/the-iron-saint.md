# The Iron Saint

## Description

The Iron Saint is a folk-heroine of the [[Iron Concordat]] whose legend places her somewhere around Year 300 Post-Convergence — a figure so heavily mythologised that separating fact from faith is impossible. In Concordat iconography, she is depicted as a young artillery officer in a soot-stained uniform, a howitzer shell cradled under one arm and a burning cache of forbidden technology at her back, her face turned toward the sky in defiance.

## Background

### The Legend

According to Concordat oral tradition, the Iron Saint commanded a howitzer battery during a border skirmish in the northern [[The Vorsteppes|Vorsteppes]] when an [[The Observers|Observer]] materialised on the battlefield. While her comrades froze in terror, she ordered her gun crew to fire upon it — a direct hit with a 105mm shell at extreme range. The Observer was "wounded" or "disrupted" (accounts vary), and a retaliatory strike was imminent.

Rather than wait for annihilation, the Saint ordered her camp's cache of forbidden technology — a hidden laboratory conducting vacuum-tube experiments that pushed against the [[Kaelen Mandate|Mandate]]'s limits — to be detonated. The resulting explosion, she gambled, would confuse the [[Eyes of Heaven|satellite]]'s targeting by creating a thermal bloom indistinguishable from the strike itself. The retaliatory missiles (accounts range from one to seven) struck the detonation site. The Saint was never found.

### What Is Known

Concordat military records from the period are incomplete — whether through deliberate suppression or the chaos of the era is unclear. No independent confirmation of the Observer-kill exists. However, the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame|Last Flame]]'s Inquisition investigated the incident and found "sufficient evidence of heresy to warrant the burning of the site and its survivors," which the Concordat interprets as reluctant confirmation that *something* significant occurred.

Satellite-tracking astronomers of the period recorded no gap in the Eyes of Heaven's constellation — if an Observer was destroyed, it was either replaced immediately or never existed.

## Relationships

- [[Iron Concordat]] — revered as a cultural icon; her legend is taught to every Concordat recruit as proof that Kaelen can be challenged
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — condemned as a heretic; the Inquisition's files on her are classified at the highest level

## Current Status

Unknown. The Concordat's official position is that she "transcended" — a theological dodge that avoids declaring her dead (which would admit Kaelen killed her) or alive (which would require producing her). Folk tradition holds that she walks the Vorsteppes still, waiting for the day humanity is ready to rise.
