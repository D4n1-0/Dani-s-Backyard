# Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW)

## Summary
The branch of naval warfare concerned with detecting, tracking, and engaging submarines. In the context of the portal crisis, ASW takes on new urgency: the modern fleet must account for the possibility that threats may move beneath the surface — and the Heretic Legion's silence includes the deep.

## Details
Standard ASW doctrine relies on sonar (active and passive), acoustic analysis, maritime patrol aircraft, and coordinated multi-ship search patterns. The fleet assembling at the portal maintains a continuous ASW screen, though no one has confirmed whether the other side has subsurface assets — or whether the laws of physics governing the deep apply equally on both sides of the threshold.
