# Pieck Finger

## Description
An Eldian Warrior and inheritor of the Cart Titan. Known for her exceptional endurance and ability to remain in Titan form for months at a time.

## Background
Selected as a Warrior by the Marleyan Empire, Pieck inherited the Cart Titan. Her unique ability to sustain Titan form for extended periods makes her invaluable for transport, reconnaissance, and mounted weapon platforms.
