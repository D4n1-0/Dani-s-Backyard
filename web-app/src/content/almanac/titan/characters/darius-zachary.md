# Darius Zachary

## Description
Premier of the Eldian military government — the highest political authority on Paradis Island following the Uprising. Cynical, intellectual, and quietly sadistic toward those who abused power. He is a practical man who cares more about institutions than ideals, and he understands that Paradis survives on borrowed time.

## Background
The architect of the military junta that replaced the Reiss monarchy. Orchestrated the propaganda campaign against the former government. His personal office features a chair carved from the titanised corpse of the former Royal Council chairman — a gesture of ironic vengeance that reveals his cruelty and his theatricality.

## Relationships
- [[Historia Reiss]] — nominal subordinate; Zachary holds real power
- [[Hange Zoë]] — Survey Corps commander under his authority
- [[Eren Yeager]] — Zachary sees Eren as Paradis's ultimate asset and ultimate liability

## Current Status
Ruling Paradis. Processing the basement revelations. Will be the key decision-maker on Paradis's side in any first contact scenario — and his cynicism may serve or doom them.
