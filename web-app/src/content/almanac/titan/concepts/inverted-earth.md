# Inverted Earth

## Summary
The Attack on Titan world shares identical landmasses to Earth but is inverted north-to-south — the Antarctic becomes the North Pole, the North Pole becomes the Antarctic.

## Details
All continental shapes, coastlines, and geological features are preserved but mirrored across the equator. Climate zones shift accordingly. The Known World (Marley, Paradis, the Mid-East, Hizuru) occupies the reversed geography of Earth. The Yellow Sea on modern Earth corresponds to an equivalent body of water in the AOT world's oceanic region near inverted Alfred Faure islands — a stretch of sea far from inhabited landmasses or trade routes.

## Context
The inversion complicates navigation, satellite imagery, and military planning for the modern side. Coordinates, star charts, and seasonal expectations all require recalibration. More profoundly, it raises the question of whether the AOT world is a natural alternate Earth or something constructed.
