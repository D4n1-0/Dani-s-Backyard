# Exclusion Zone

## Summary
A 5-nautical-mile exclusion perimeter established around [[The Gate]] by mutual agreement between Seoul and Beijing, enforced by the coast guard vessels already on scene — [[KCG 506]] and [[Haijing 2306]].

## Details
The zone extends five nautical miles from the Gate in all directions. No military vessel of any nationality is permitted to enter. Enforcement is shared between the ROK and PRC coast guard commanding officers — [[Park Do-hyun|Lieutenant Park]] and [[Chen Shaoqiang|Senior Captain Chen]] — operating under identical orders from their respective commands. Approaching warships (ROKN destroyer, PLAN Type 055 cruiser, US Navy Arleigh Burke) receive instructions to hold outside the perimeter.

The zone serves three purposes:
1. Prevents accidental escalation between converging naval forces
2. Preserves the scientific baseline by limiting water disturbance near the gate
3. Establishes a de facto precedent for cooperative management before the international community imposes its own framework

## Context
The zone is informal — a handshake between [[Lee Jun-ho]] and [[Zheng Weimin]], communicated through naval commands with no public announcement. It is understood by both sides to be a temporary measure pending the formation of a broader international protocol. Its durability depends on mutual restraint: if any power breaches the perimeter, the framework collapses.
