# Oliver Cross

## Overview
Neurologist, age 46. Veteran of the DARPA SUBNETS program (Systems-Based Neurotechnology for Emerging Therapies), which developed closed-loop neural interfaces for PTSD and traumatic brain injury. Later recruited into the intelligence community's biological research directorate. No idealism left from his clinical days — just the conviction that precision electrodes and real-time neural read/write are categorically different from the crude tools (drugs, electroshock, sensory deprivation) that defined earlier eras of neural intervention. Scientific curiosity drives him more than patriotism. The ethics of any given project are an inconvenience to be managed, not a barrier to be respected.

## Key Role
The analyst who first flagged [[Levi Ackerman|Levi]]'s cerebellar superstructure as a potential BCI target. His argument: the secondary motor layer is a structurally distinct neural region — a "natural landing pad" for electrodes that no normal human brain provides. Argues that the Ackerman Path-immunity protein likely does not block non-Founder neural signals, making the BCI theoretically viable.
