# 10 Downing Street

## Overview
The official residence and office of the Prime Minister of the United Kingdom. The private study on the first floor serves as [[Stephen Davenport|Davenport]]'s refuge — a small room where he retreats after the NATO session to confront the reality that the UK has lost control of the narrative.
