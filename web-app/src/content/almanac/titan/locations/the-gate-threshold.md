# The Gate Threshold

## Overview
The sealed boundary of [[The Gate]] — the 500m-wide, 200m-tall dimensional tear connecting the [[Yellow Sea]] on modern Earth to a remote stretch of subantarctic ocean in the AOT world. Both sides are ocean: neither offers a landing point. The Gate bisects the sea without disturbing wave patterns, but the bidirectional flow of water creates a thick mist along the seam where warm Yellow Sea water meets the 3°C southern Indian Ocean.

The visual boundary is a hard cut — a video-game texture seam between two realities. On both sides, a June night — but the stars do not match. The seabirds on the far side are species unknown to the Yellow Sea. The first living crossing was a wandering albatross gliding from the AOT side to the Earth side at dawn.

## Features
- 500m lateral span; 200m vertical from 100m below sea to 100m above
- No detectable radiation, no acoustic signature, no visible source
- Fully bidirectional — water, air, and matter pass freely in both directions
- Mist/fog at the seam from temperature differential (Yellow Sea ~18°C vs AOT ocean ~3°C)
- Visible only as a seam in the night — the familiar stars stop at the boundary and unfamiliar ones begin
- No transition zone — the boundary is a single plane

## Inhabitants
- Modern side: [[KCG 506]] ([[Korea Coast Guard]]), [[Haijing 2306]] ([[China Coast Guard]]) — standoff
- AOT side: none

## Current Events
Day 8. One week since the Gate opened. A functioning international exclusion zone is enforced by [[KCG 506]] and [[Haijing 2306]]. Three warships — ROKN, PLAN, and US Navy — orbit outside the five-nautical-mile perimeter. Two research motherships — the [[R-V Isabu]] and [[Xiang Yang Hong 06]] — conduct continuous drone, ROV, and ASV operations through the Gate. The [[Joint Scientific Committee]] meets daily. The [[Inverted Earth]] theory has been confirmed. The survey radius expands daily. The AOT world remains unaware.
