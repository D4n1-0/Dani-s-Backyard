# Harry Du Bois

## Description

*RCM detective, Lieutenant. A man who solved a murder in Martinaise while piecing together his own shattered memory. Brilliant, self-destructive, the kind of cop who finds the truth not because he's good at his job but because he cannot stop looking. His investigation into the hanged mercenary brought him to the Whirling-in-Rags; his presence there shaped everything that followed.*

## Background

*Protagonist of the events preceding this story. Arrived in Martinaise with catastrophic amnesia, rebuilt himself over the course of a week-long investigation, solved the murder of a Wild Pines mercenary, and bore witness to the Tribunal. Survived. Returned to Jamrock with his partner Kim Kitsuragi. The case is closed. His shadow over Martinaise is not.*
