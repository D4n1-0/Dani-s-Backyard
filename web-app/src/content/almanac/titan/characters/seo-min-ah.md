# Seo Min-ah

## Overview
Twenty-nine years old. Korean civilian linguist and translator, recruited by CATF specifically for the Marley contact mission. Spent four months studying the Marleyan language under [[Elias Vogler|Dr. Elias Vogler]]'s tutelage, supplemented by daily conversation drills with the detained [[Pelikan]] crew. By the time of the Odiha contact, she could form sentences in a language that had never been spoken on her Earth — and could distinguish between the formal register of the admiralty and the lower-deck slang of the sailors who would be watching from the pier.

## Background
Graduate of Seoul National University's Department of Linguistics. Specialised in language acquisition and contact linguistics before the Gate opened. When CATF put out a call for linguists willing to learn an entirely undocumented language from a detainee population, she was the first applicant. Her training was immersive and unprecedented — no textbooks, no grammars, only the recordings and written notes that Vogler produced during his own months of detention. She accompanied [[Satomi Ishihara|Dr. Ishihara]] to Odiha as the primary translator — though Ishihara's functional Marleyan proved sufficient for the diplomatic exchange, Seo's presence provided insurance against linguistic failure.

## Status
Returned to Grande Terre. Continues to refine the emerging CATF–Marleyan language corpus.
