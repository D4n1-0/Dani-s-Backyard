# Falco Grice

## Description
A young Eldian Warrior candidate in the Marleyan military. Kind-hearted and protective, especially of Gabi Braun. Later inherits the Jaw Titan.

## Background
Selected as a Warrior candidate for the Marleyan Empire, Falco was trained alongside Gabi Braun, competing to inherit the Armored Titan. He is the nephew of Colt Grice, a fellow Warrior candidate.
