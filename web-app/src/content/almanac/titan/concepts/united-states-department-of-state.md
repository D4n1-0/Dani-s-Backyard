# United States Department of State

## Summary
The executive department of the US federal government responsible for foreign policy and international relations. During the Gate crisis, the State Department coordinates the diplomatic dimension of the American response, working through both the Embassy in Seoul and the US mission to the UN.

## Details
The State Department is the primary channel through which Washington communicates with allies and rivals. In the Gate crisis, its role includes coordination with the [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (ROK)]] (alliance management), the [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (PRC)]] (crisis communication), and other diplomatic stakeholders.
