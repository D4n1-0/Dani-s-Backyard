# Oriens

## Overview

Oriens is the dawn cradle — the north-eastern continent of [[Gaethon]], a land of burnt-forest highlands, sweeping steppe prairies, and mineral-rich mountain knots. It is the heartland of two great powers — the [[Iron Concordat]] in the northern [[The Vorsteppes|Vorsteppes]] and the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] in the central highlands — whose ideological rivalry defines the continent's politics.

## Features

- **[[The Vorsteppes]]:** Northern prairies and highlands, rich in iron, coal, and strategic depth. The Concordat's industrial heartland.
- **The Burnt Forests:** High-altitude woodland cleared by centuries of controlled burning for agriculture and ritual — the Theocracy's sacred landscape.
- **The Flame Marches:** The contested borderlands between the Concordat and the Theocracy, where limited-objective wars flare every few years. Heavily patrolled by [[Silent Chorus]] drones.
- **[[Keth]]:** The crater of fused glass — a 15-kilometre radius of vitrified devastation where an [[The Observers|Observer]] was harmed in Year 287. Still faintly luminescent.

## Inhabitants

Two great powers and a belt of smaller buffer states exist in permanent tension. The Concordat dreams of industrial liberation; the Theocracy enforces technological stasis as holy writ. Their rivalry is the most dangerous fault line on Gaethon — a war between them could pull in every other power and, potentially, Kaelen's direct intervention.

## Current Events

The Concordat has massed 200,000 troops on the Amber League's hinterland — a move that the Theocracy's Synod has condemned as "provocation within permitted bounds," an endorsement that has alarmed every neighbouring state.
