# The Thought Cabinet

## Overview

The Thought Cabinet is a clandestine order of mathematicians and theoretical physicists within the [[Technate of Vorr]] who, using only paper and ink, have deduced the principles of jet propulsion, transistor logic, and radar — forbidden sciences preserved and advanced in secret for over two centuries. Their knowledge is passed down as a holy succession, each generation selecting and training the next. They are the intellectual seed bank of a pre-[[War of Convergence|Convergence]] world, waiting for the day the [[Kaelen Mandate|iron cage]] rusts.

## Structure

- **The First Calculator:** The order's senior member. Identity is a state secret of the Technate. Believed to be a former Director of Applied Mathematics on the Board of Applied Industries.
- **The Seven Chairs:** Senior Cabinet members, each responsible for a domain of forbidden knowledge: Electromagnetics, Fluid Dynamics, Solid-State Theory, Nuclear Principles, Information Logic, Materials Science, and Celestial Mechanics.
- **Initiates:** Junior members undergoing decades of training. Selected in childhood through standardised tests, then "recruited" into the Board's advanced engineering programme — a cover for Cabinet induction.
- **The Succession:** Knowledge is transmitted orally and through hand-copied manuscripts. No printing presses. No photographs. Each generation manually reproduces the entire Cabinet library — a safeguard against loss and a filter for understanding.

## Assets

- **The Thought Spire:** A sealed tower deep within the highest peak of the [[The Vorran Massif|Massif]]. Guarded by the Vorran Artillerie Corps' internal security division.
- **The Cabinet Library:** Hand-copied manuscripts covering electromagnetic theory, fluid dynamics, semiconductor physics, nuclear chain reactions, information theory, and orbital mechanics.
- **Theoretical Models:** Pencil-and-paper derivations of jet engine thermodynamics, transistor switching logic, radar range equations, and atomic critical mass calculations — verified through logic alone, never tested.
- **Defector Network:** Several Cabinet members have defected to found or join [[The Unbound]], taking fragments of the Library with them.

## Relationships

- [[Technate of Vorr]] — symbiotic; the Board provides cover and resources; the Cabinet provides theoretical breakthroughs that keep Vorran grey-zone technology at the Mandate's edge
- [[The Unbound]] — origin point; the Unbound were founded by defecting Cabinet members, and covert contact likely continues
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — the Inquisition's highest-value target; if the Theocracy ever confirmed the Cabinet's existence, it would declare a holy war
- [[Kaelen]] — the ultimate question; the Cabinet's entire project rests on the assumption that Kaelen's surveillance has limits, and that paper and ink in a sealed mountain are beyond them
