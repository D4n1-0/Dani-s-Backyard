# Ministry of Foreign Affairs (PRC)

## Overview
The diplomatic arm of the People's Republic of China, responsible for implementing foreign policy, managing embassies and consulates, conducting bilateral and multilateral negotiations, and representing Chinese interests internationally. In the Gate crisis, the MFA's role is to manage the diplomatic fallout of whatever decisions the CFAC makes.

## Structure
- Asian Affairs Department (led by Assistant Minister [[Guo Yong]])
- North American and Oceanian Affairs Department
- International Organisations and Conferences Department
- Protocol Department

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- Assistant Minister [[Guo Yong]] is the MFA representative in the CFAC incident group.

## Relationships
- [[Central Foreign Affairs Commission (PRC)]] — implements CFAC directives
- [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (ROK)]] — counterpart
- [[United States Department of State]] — frequent interlocutor

## Current Status
Urging calibrated restraint. [[Guo Yong]] believes a premature military move would trigger American escalation and isolate China diplomatically before the international scientific framework is even established.
