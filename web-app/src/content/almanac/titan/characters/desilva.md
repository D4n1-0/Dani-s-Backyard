# DeSilva

## Overview
Ensign, United States Navy. Assigned to the first-contact interview of [[Frieda]] and the Eldian engineers because she was the youngest and least threatening officer on the CATF personnel roster. Informed the eight Eldians of their sanctuary status and CATF's non-recognition of racial distinction.
