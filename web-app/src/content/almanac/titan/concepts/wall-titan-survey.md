# Wall Titan Survey

## Summary
Ground-penetrating radar survey of Paradis Island's three concentric Walls conducted via a low-altitude KUS-15 drone sortie under cloud cover. Confirmed that the Walls are not stone structures but contain hundreds of thousands of Colossal Titans embedded in hardened crystal. The Rumbling is not a myth — it is a physical weapon system.

## Details
- **Method:** KUS-15 drone with geological GPR pod, night sortie at 2,000 ft AGL under cloud cover, no active emissions
- **Findings:** Continuous organic-mass signatures throughout Wall Maria, Rose, and Sina — humanoid forms ~50 m tall, shoulder-to-shoulder, one per ~15 m of Wall frontage
- **Estimate:** 300,000–600,000 Colossal Titans across all three Walls
- **Outer circumference of Wall Maria:** ~480 km → ~32,000 Colossals in the outermost ring alone
- **Implication:** If activated simultaneously, the Wall Titans would flatten all AOT world continents within months

## Context
Filed under classification RUMBLE-1. The Wall Titan Survey is the foundational finding behind all CATF strategic planning regarding Paradis Island — it confirms the Rumbling as an existential extinction-level threat and the Founding Titan as the single most powerful weapons system on either Earth.
