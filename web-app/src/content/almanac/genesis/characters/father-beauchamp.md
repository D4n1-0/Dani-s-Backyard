# Father Beauchamp

## Description
Chaplain of *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*, a priest of the [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]]. He wears armoured vestments stitched with silver-thread prayers of warding and carries a processional crucifix of [[Orichalcum]] steel.

## Background
[[Father Beauchamp|Beauchamp]] performs the Rite of Discernment on [[Alistair Pembroke|Captain Pembroke]]'s orders, casting divinations that detect no infernal taint from [[The Anomaly]]. He volunteers to cross the threshold as the Faithful's first envoy, facing [[Rebecca Osei|Lieutenant Commander Osei]] in the first face-to-face contact between two worlds.

## Relationships
- [[Alistair Pembroke]] — his captain
- [[Rebecca Osei]] — his counterpart in first contact; has given her his [[Orichalcum]] crucifix as she prepares to cross
- [[Daniel Foster]] — the modern chaplain whose "hidden God" theology shook Beauchamp's understanding of faith

## Current Status
Aboard *[[HMS Resolute]]* with the Faithful command. Has received [[Davenport's Reply|the Prime Minister's reply]] to [[King Robert's Letter]] — an honest, secular refusal of the Providence framing wrapped in the language of friendship. Wrestling with the implications for his [[The Ark|ark theology]]. Has heard [[Bishop Aldric|Aldric]]'s [[The Theology of Waiting|theology of waiting]] — the argument that the moderns are neither ark nor test but witnesses, a people who have lived through the hiddenness of God, and whose meaning must be waited for rather than judged. [[Father Beauchamp|Beauchamp]] recognises the force of the argument even as it complicates his own.
