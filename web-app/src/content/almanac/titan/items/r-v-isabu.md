# R/V Isabu

## Description
Korean oceanographic research vessel operated by the [[Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology]] (KIOST). Modern multi-purpose design with a full suite of wet and dry laboratories, a moon pool for deep-water ROV deployment, and a containerised drone control module. Equipped with multi-beam sonar, CTD rosette, coring winches, and a deployable hydrophone array. Carries a scientific complement of approximately 25 researchers alongside a crew of 30.

## Function
Deep-sea exploration, ocean circulation studies, marine geology, and biological sampling. Diverted from a planned survey of the Yellow Sea to the Gate site under KIOST emergency tasking.

## History
Arrives at the exclusion zone perimeter on Day 3 following the Gate's opening, carrying Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]] and a full scientific team. Becomes the forward operating base for Korean drone and ROV operations through the Gate, launching the KUS-15 medium-altitude UAV for extended-range survey of the AOT-side ocean.
