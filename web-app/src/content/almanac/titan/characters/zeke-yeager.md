# Zeke Yeager

## Description
Mid-twenties. Tall, blond, bespectacled, casually sardonic. The Warchief of the Marleyan Warrior Unit and holder of the Beast Titan. His royal bloodline (descended from the Fritz family through his mother Dina) gives him the unique ability to create and command Pure Titans from Eldians who have ingested his spinal fluid. He is also Eren Yeager's half-brother — a fact that neither side of the ocean fully grasps yet.

## Background
Son of Grisha Yeager and Dina Fritz, raised in the Liberio Internment Zone. Betrayed his parents to the Marleyan authorities as a child to protect himself and his grandparents. Was mentored by Tom Ksaver, the previous Beast Titan, who taught him Titan biology and seeded in him the belief that the Eldian race should be euthanised out of existence — the Euthanasia Plan. Zeke carries this nihilistic mission beneath a facade of genial detachment.

## Relationships
- [[Eren Yeager]] — half-brother (not yet met face to face); Zeke believes Eren is brainwashed by Grisha
- [[Reiner Braun]] — subordinate; Zeke holds Reiner in barely concealed contempt
- [[Pieck Finger]] — fellow Warrior; genuine camaraderie
- [[Tom Ksaver]] — mentor (deceased); Zeke's true father figure
- [[Levi Ackerman]] — nemesis; Levi swore to kill Zeke after Shiganshina

## Current Status
In Marley, recovering from his near-death at Shiganshina. Continues to serve as Warchief while secretly advancing the Euthanasia Plan. Has not yet made contact with Paradis through the Anti-Marleyan Volunteers. The Gate may accelerate or derail that timeline.
