# Grisha's Books

## Description
Three leather-bound journals hidden by [[Grisha Yeager]] in the basement of his Shiganshina home. The first is a memoir — Grisha's life in [[Marley]], his sister's death, his radicalisation, the Restorationist movement, his marriage to Dina Fritz, Zeke's betrayal, and his exile to Paradis. The second describes the world beyond the Walls — geography, nations, technology, the global hatred of Eldians. The third is a photograph of Grisha, Dina, and a young [[Zeke Yeager]] — proof that humanity survived beyond the Walls.

## Function
The single most important intelligence asset on Paradis Island. These books shattered the false reality the Reiss monarchy maintained for a century. They are the foundation of Paradis's emerging foreign policy — and the source of its existential dread.

## History
Smuggled to Paradis by Grisha when he fled Marley. Hidden in the Shiganshina basement for five years. Retrieved by [[Eren Yeager]], [[Armin Arlert]], [[Mikasa Ackerman]], [[Levi Ackerman]], and [[Hange Zoë]] after the Battle of Shiganshina. Currently under military analysis.
