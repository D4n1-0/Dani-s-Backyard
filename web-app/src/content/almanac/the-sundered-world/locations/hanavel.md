# Hanavel

## Overview

Hanavel is the airship capital of [[Gaethon]] — a Free City on the [[The Amber Coast|Amber Coast]] whose skyline is dominated not by buildings but by the envelopes of zeppelins under construction. Its yards produce the magnificent *Cloud-King*-class passenger airships that connect the continents, and its hydrogen plants are the most advanced chemical facilities permitted under the [[Kaelen Mandate]].

## Features

- **The Yards:** Vast hangars where airship frames are assembled from duralumin girders and fabric envelopes. Each *Cloud-King* takes two years to build and requires a workforce of 3,000.
- **The Docking Spires:** Mooring towers rising 150 metres above the harbour, where arriving airships exchange passengers and cargo. At any given time, a dozen vessels may be berthed.
- **The Hydrogen Works:** Electrolysis plants producing lifting gas. The purity of Hanavel hydrogen is legendary — a point of civic pride and, less publicly, a strategic reserve.
- **The Seeker Monastery:** Adjacent to the Signal Tower, a quiet complex where the [[The Signal Seekers|Signal Seekers]] maintain their directional aerials and archive rooms filled with wax-cylinder recordings of [[Kaelen]]'s emissions.

## Inhabitants

Airships engineers, hydrogen chemists, merchant factors, and the Seekers. Hanavel's population hovers near the million-soul cap; the city government maintains a waiting list for residency and an aggressive emigration incentive programme.

## Current Events

The Yards have received an unsolicited order from the [[Technate of Vorr]] for "high-altitude envelope materials" — components that could serve either meteorological research or something considerably more provocative.
