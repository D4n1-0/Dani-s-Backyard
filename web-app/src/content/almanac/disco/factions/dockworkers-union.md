# Dockworkers' Union

## Overview

*The labour union representing dockworkers in Martinaise — and the de facto government of the district. Under the Claire Brotherhood, the union has blurred the line between labour organisation and organised crime, controlling everything from payrolls to protection rackets. Led the strike against Wild Pines after the terminal dispute. Lost the Hardie Boys at the Tribunal. Lost the terminal to the Coalition. Has not lost control of Martinaise.*
