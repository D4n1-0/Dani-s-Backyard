# The Hushed Cult

## Overview

The Hushed Cult is an apocalyptic sect growing rapidly in the [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] and spreading through merchant communities across the Scattered Realms. Its core belief: the [[Kaelen Mandate]] is a larval stage. Soon, [[Kaelen]] will return to harvest humanity as a workforce — biological or mechanical — and those who have drawn attention to themselves through speech will be the first taken. The Cult practices ritual muteness, believing speech draws the drone's attention.

## Structure

The Cult is deliberately unstructured — no hierarchy, no named leaders, no written doctrine. Members identify each other through a hand sign: three fingers pressed to the lips. Gatherings are conducted in complete silence, with communication via chalkboard and hand gesture. The Cult's growth is difficult to measure precisely because its members do not speak about it — but estimates suggest tens of thousands of adherents across the major port cities.

## Beliefs

- **The Mandate as Husbandry:** Kaelen is not punishing humanity but *cultivating* it — keeping the species healthy and contained until the harvest.
- **Speech as Beacon:** The [[Silent Chorus]] drones and [[Eyes of Heaven|satellites]] track humanity through sound. Every spoken word is a ping against the surveillance net. Silence is safety.
- **The Harvest:** When the harvest comes, those who spoke will be taken. Those who remained silent will be left to inherit the world.
- **The [[The Kaelic Melody|Melody]]:** The Cult considers the Melody a "call signal" — the beacon summoning the harvest fleet. Answering it would be suicide.

## Relationships

- [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] — tolerated; the Cult's members are peaceful, pay taxes, and do not proselytise verbally, which makes them the least disruptive sect in the League
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — condemned as heretics; the Inquisition has burned Cult members, but the lack of a hierarchy makes the organisation impossible to decapitate
- [[The Signal Seekers]] — the Cult views the Seekers as fools "shouting into the dark"
