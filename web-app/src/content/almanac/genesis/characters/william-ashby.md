# William Ashby

## Overview
Commander of the Faithful torpedo-boat destroyer squadron stationed at the portal cordon. Younger than the other Faithful captains, less careful with his words, eager to prove himself in a crisis that has no precedent. His first reaction to [[Davenport's Reply|the Prime Minister's letter]] was blunt: *"It is empty of God."*

## Personality
[[William Ashby|Ashby]] is quick to judge and slow to retract. He is not a fanatic — he does not wish to fight the moderns — but he is suspicious of a power that offers alliance without theological foundation. To him, the moderns' secularism is not neutrality; it is a vacuum, and a vacuum invites the Adversary. He is overruled by the King but his unease remains, and it will surface again when the summit tests his loyalties.

## Role in the Council
During the summit council, [[William Ashby|Ashby]] objects to the inclusion of the [[Iron Sultanate]] — not because he rejects the Sultanate as an ally, but because he believes the Crown should not share a table with a power that does not share its faith. He is silenced by the King's authority but not convinced.
