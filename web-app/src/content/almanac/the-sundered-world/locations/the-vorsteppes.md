# The Vorsteppes

## Overview

The Vorsteppes are a belt of sweeping prairies and mineral-rich highlands in northern [[Oriens]], the heartland of the [[Iron Concordat]]. A land of long horizons, cavalry screens, dispersed factory collectives, and the dream — whispered in every barracks and homestead — that humanity will one day match [[Kaelen]]'s might.

## Features

- **The Foundry Belt:** A line of state-owned factories dispersed across "independent" homesteader collectives — the template for [[Grey-Zone Manufacturing]]. Each collective legally operates its own forges, but output flows to the Concordat's army.
- **The Cavalry Ranges:** Vast grasslands where horse cavalry — still tactically viable against unarmoured foes — trains alongside light tanks. The Concordat is the only major power to maintain a significant mounted arm.
- **The Iron Saint's Shrine:** A pilgrimage site at an undisclosed location, where young Concordat recruits swear the Oath of the Unbroken: *"We do not forget. We do not forgive. We prepare."*
- **The Border Marches:** Contested frontier with the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]], where limited-objective wars flare every few years under [[Silent Chorus]] drone surveillance.

## Inhabitants

The Concordat's stratocratic elite — corps commanders, marshals, and the Marshal-Preceptor — plus a population of homesteaders, factory workers, and career soldiers raised on the legend of the [[The Iron Saint|Iron Saint]].

## Current Events

200,000 troops massed on the Amber League's hinterland. The Concordat claims "transit rights." No one in the Vorsteppes believes this.
