# Moralist International

## Overview
The world's dominant political body, representing 1.2 billion people across annexed states in the Reál Belt. A union of centre-left and centre-right parties operating under the humanist ideology of [[Dolores Dei]]. Created by her ~380 years ago. Motto: "A blue forget-me-not; a piece of the grey sky." Unofficial: "For a moment, there was hope."

## Structure
- [[Coalition of Nations|The Coalition]] — military & occupation arm
- [[EPIS]] — international economic trade union
- ICP — interisolary law enforcement
- Institute of Price Stability — inflation control
- Committee of Responsibility of Revachol — key moralist authority on Revacholian matters
- Contingency spread modelling — predictive infrastructure

## Assets
- Coalition airships (warships) stationed over Revachol
- Signal-blue flag; the legal framework of the Reál Belt
- Arms restrictions: breech-loaders banned for peacetime law enforcement
- Tax-free, laissez-faire economic model for occupied Revachol

## Relationships
- [[Coalition of Nations]] — military execution arm
- [[Revachol Citizens Militia]] — RCM operates under Moralintern-delegated authority
- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — growing rift; Union represents populist forces rejecting moralist establishment
- [[Communists]] — historical enemies; Moralintern crushed the Commune
