# Measurehead

## Description

*A lorry driver and self-appointed gatekeeper of Martinaise. Massive in every dimension — not fat, not muscled, simply enlarged by some cosmic error of scale. Believes fervently in his own "advanced race theory," a pseudo-intellectual framework so dense and impenetrable that it functions as a kind of verbal fortification. Guards the gates. Both literally and ideologically.*

## Background

*Union-affiliated lorry driver. Guarded the entrance to Martinaise until the reclamation — detained by Coalition soldiers almost immediately and spent the day cuffed against a gate pillar, monologuing his "racial science" at anyone within earshot. His ideology is elaborate, meticulously cited, and entirely fabricated — a wall with a bibliography. Not dangerous in the physical sense. Not arrestable in the ideological sense. Impossible to shut up by any known method.*

## Relationships

- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — *Loyal, in his own incomprehensible way.*
- [[INSURCOM]] — *The Coalition soldiers now occupying the gate he once controlled. An ideological and physical standoff.*

## Current Status

*Detained by Coalition forces. Currently occupying a holding cell and the attention of every soldier within earshot. Still talking.*
