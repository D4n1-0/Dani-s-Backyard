# First Contact Pod

## Summary
A small, palm-sized metallic device deliberately dropped by CATF on a known Paradis patrol route to initiate contact with the Survey Corps. Contains a pre-recorded message from Dr. [[Elias Vogler]], a map of Paradis with CATF grid coordinates, and a blinking rendezvous marker. Designed to activate upon handling and to serve as a non-threatening introduction — the first physical bridge between modern Earth and the AOT world's inhabitants.

## Details
- **Dimensions:** approximately palm-sized, smooth-seamed, hinged design with an embedded screen
- **Power source:** internal battery, steady low heat signature when active (thermal output: ~38°C surface temperature)
- **Activation:** motion-activated or timer-based; opens along an equatorial seam upon detection of sustained handling
- **Display:** small flat screen showing a high-resolution map of Paradis Island annotated with CATF's surveillance grid coordinates; a blinking red dot indicates the rendezvous point
- **Recording:** pre-recorded voice message in carefully-accented Eldian from [[Elias Vogler]], running approximately two minutes; identifies CATF, disavows Marleyan affiliation, states peaceful intent, extends an invitation to a designated meeting site
- **Dropped:** Day 103, on a known cavalry patrol route along Paradis's new eastern railway, to ensure discovery by military personnel rather than civilians

## Context
The pod was the culmination of a month-long CATF deliberation (Day 75–Day 105) on whether to contact the AOT world and whom to contact. Marley was rejected on grounds of its fascist military dictatorship, internment zone system, and active weaponisation of Eldian Titan transformation. The Survey Corps was selected as the contact point because of its demonstrated capacity for curiosity, moral reasoning, and survival beyond ideology.

The pod was designed to communicate two things simultaneously: technological capability (through the precision of the map and the sophistication of the activation mechanism) and friendly intent (through the choice of language, the speaker's disavowal of Marley, and the voluntary submission to Paradisian control over whether to accept the invitation). It was successful: [[Armin Arlert]] recognised its implications, [[Hange Zoë]] accepted the invitation, and the [[CATF–Survey Corps Rendezvous]] followed two nights later.
