# Sea Fortress

## Summary
A Suzerain-era coastal fortification built by King Filippe II. Captured by communists in '02 and equipped with an anti-aircraft gun that failed during the Coalition's Landing in '08. Became the hiding place of [[Iosef Lilianovich Dros]] for 22 years.
