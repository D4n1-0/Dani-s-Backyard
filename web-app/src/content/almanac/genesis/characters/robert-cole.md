# Robert Cole

## Overview
President of the United States during the period of first contact with the Trench Crusade timeline. A career executive — boardrooms before politics — who approaches the portal as an operational problem requiring American leadership and American solutions.

## Personality
Cole is direct, impatient, and convinced that the United States bears the primary responsibility for global crisis management. He respects the UK as an ally but does not believe it has the resources or the political will to manage the portal alone. His push for a joint military command and a presidential visit to the Anomaly is driven by genuine fear of the Heretic Legion threat, but also by an American strategic calculus: whoever controls the portal controls the future.

## Relationships
- [[Stephen Davenport]] — uneasy partnership. Cole respects Davenport's competence but resents his caution.
- [[William Ashford]] — professional distance. Ashford's diplomatic caution is the opposite of Cole's instinct for action.
