# The New

## Summary
A cultural era — and the nickname of the Thirties — that came out of post-Revolutionary [[Revachol]] East. The decade of disco, decadence, and ultraliberalism, fuelled by cocaine and quantitative easing.

## Details
Revachol East transformed into the world's largest tax haven. The surge of untaxed income produced The New: a cultural explosion of disco music, nightlife, and ultraliberal ideology. The economic boom was always a "market mirage." The Forties dispelled it like a cold splash. By '51, Revachol is still in the ensuing recession.

## Context
The New is nostalgia. It is the Speedfreaks' neo-disco played through salvaged speakers. It is the abandoned church, the repurposed hostel, the half-renovated Martinaise. It is what people mean when they say Revachol was once something. Even the name of the scenario is a reference.
