# Titan Cosmology

## The Hallucigenia Entity

The source of all Titan powers is an ancient parasitic organism resembling the Cambrian-era *Hallucigenia sparsa*. It possesses a rudimentary biological imperative to survive and multiply. ~1,800 years ago, the dying Ymir Fritz fell into a subterranean pool beneath an ancient tree where the organism bonded with her spine, granting her regenerative immortality and the ability to manifest the first Titan — a colossal biological mecha formed in response to her desperate will for an undying body.

The entity perpetuates violence and cannibalism to ensure its continuous replication through Ymir's bloodline. Severing the organism from its host is the only theoretical mechanism to end the Titan curse.

## The Nine Titans

Upon Ymir's death, her power fractured into nine distinct Titan forms, each with unique abilities. Passed down through consumption of spinal fluid or cannibalistic inheritance, each Shifter is bound by the Curse of Ymir — a 13-year lifespan limit.

## The Paths

Invisible, multidimensional channels connecting every Subject of Ymir across space and time. Through the Paths, biological matter, ancestral memories, will, and regeneration are transmitted instantaneously. The nexus where all Paths converge is the **Coordinate** — a towering, ethereal tree of light synonymous with the Founding Titan, where the trapped consciousness of Ymir Fritz resides in perpetual servitude forging Titan bodies from otherworldly soil.

Time within the Paths operates outside linear causality. A Shifter heals a wound in seconds because Ymir spends eons sculpting the flesh. The Attack Titan uniquely perceives memories of its *future* inheritors, creating deterministic time loops.

## The Vow Renouncing War

Karl Fritz, the 145th King, embedded an ideological failsafe within the Founding Titan. Any royal-blooded inheritor is possessed by a pacifistic conviction — they accept the world's hatred as just punishment and refuse to command the Wall Titans for any purpose. This is why the Reiss family never used the Founding Titan's power against the Titans that plagued humanity.

### The Royal Bloodline Loophole

If a **non-royal** individual (Eren Yeager) possesses the Founding Titan and makes physical contact with a Titan of **royal blood** (Zeke Yeager, or a titanised Historia Reiss), the non-royal can access the Coordinate's full power without being bound by Karl Fritz's Vow.

## The Ackerman Clan

A bloodline resulting from ancient Titan science experiments designed to create royal bodyguards. Ackermans are Subjects of Ymir connected to the Paths but genetically immune to the Founding Titan's memory manipulation. When awakened, they access accumulated combat experience of all past Ackermans via the Paths — granting Titan-level strength, speed, and reflexes in human form. They cannot be Titanised.

Known living Ackermans: [[Levi Ackerman]], [[Mikasa Ackerman]].

## Pure Titans

Subjects of Ymir injected with Titan spinal fluid become mindless Pure Titans — giant, regenerating, sun-powered humanoids that instinctively hunt humans. They can be restored to human form only by consuming the spinal fluid of a Shifter. Zeke Yeager's Beast Titan can create and command Pure Titans from Eldians who have ingested his spinal fluid and been triggered by his scream.
