# Hardie Boys

## Overview

*The enforcers of the Dockworkers' Union. Led by Titus Hardie. A group of dockworkers who served as the Union's muscle — protection, intimidation, the physical architecture of Evrart Claire's power. Killed at the Tribunal four days before the Reclamation, in a shootout with Krenel's mercenaries outside the Whirling-in-Rags. Their deaths broke the strike's backbone and left the Union without its most visible enforcers, though Evrart Claire himself was unharmed.*
