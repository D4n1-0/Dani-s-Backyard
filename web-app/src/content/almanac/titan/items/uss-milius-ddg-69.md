# USS Milius (DDG-69)

## Description
Arleigh Burke-class guided missile destroyer, Flight IIA variant. Approximately 9,200 tons displacement, crew of roughly 300. Armed with a 5-inch (127mm) Mk 45 deck gun, two Phalanx CIWS mounts, and 96-cell Mk 41 Vertical Launch System carrying a mix of Standard Missiles, Tomahawk cruise missiles, and ASROC anti-submarine rockets. Home port: Yokosuka, Japan. Part of Destroyer Squadron 15, forward-deployed to the Seventh Fleet.

## Function
Multi-mission surface combatant — air defence, anti-submarine warfare, strike operations. Conducting a freedom of navigation operation (FONOP) in the Yellow Sea when the Gate opened. Diverted to the Gate site under Seventh Fleet directive.

## History
First US Navy vessel to arrive at the Gate. Captain communicates with [[Park Do-hyun]] and [[Chen Shaoqiang]] via bridge-to-bridge, offering to assist with aerial surveillance. Respects the exclusion zone established by Seoul and Beijing, holding outside the five-nautical-mile perimeter. Its presence — observing but not participating in the scientific framework — is a source of tension for Beijing.
