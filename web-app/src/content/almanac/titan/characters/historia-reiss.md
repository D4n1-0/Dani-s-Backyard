# Historia Reiss

## Description
Fifteen years old. Petite, blonde, blue eyes. The true heir to the Reiss royal bloodline — and by extension, the Founding Titan's legacy — though she chose to reject the inheritance. Now Queen of Paradis under a military junta led by Premier Zachary. Has discarded the "Krista Lenz" persona and lives authentically, for the first time, as herself.

## Background
Illegitimate daughter of the Reiss family. Raised on a rural estate, denied her identity. Joined the 104th Training Corps under the alias Krista Lenz, playing a "good girl" martyr persona. During the Uprising, she rejected her father Rod Reiss's demand to eat Eren and inherit the Founding Titan. Crowned Queen as a compromise between the military and the people.

## Relationships
- [[Eren Yeager]] — ally; Eren protected her from being sacrificed
- [[Ymir (Freckles)]] — closest bond (deceased/passed Titan); Ymir's letter told her to live with pride
- Premier [[Darius Zachary]] — nominal subordinate; the junta holds real power

## Current Status
Queen of Paradis. Figurehead monarch under a military government. Her royal blood makes her the biological key to activating the Founding Titan — a fact the military has not yet moved on, but will. The Gate's arrival may accelerate or derail whatever plans exist for her.
