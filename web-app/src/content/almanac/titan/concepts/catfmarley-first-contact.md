# CATF–Marley First Contact

## Summary
The first diplomatic contact between the [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)]] and the [[Marleyan Empire]], conducted at the port of [[Odiha]] on Days 115–127. Fourteen Marleyan crew of the *Pelikan* sailed home as CATF external consultants, escorted by a CATF diplomatic yacht and a commercial support ship. Admiral [[Konrad Verner]], Commander of the Odiha Naval District, received the delegation. A communication channel was established. CATF departed before the Marleyan High Command could issue a formal response.

## Details
- **Date:** Day 125–127 (~Year 850 TC / 2026-10-20–2026-10-22 Modern)
- **Location:** [[Odiha]], Marleyan southern port
- **CATF assets:** Diplomatic yacht (60 m, CATF flag), commercial support ship (5 armoured Toyota Land Cruisers, helipad with Airbus H125, fuel and generator bladders — all remaining on deck, never unloaded), USS *Annapolis* (SSN-760) submerged escort (known only to Ishihara)
- **CATF delegation lead:** Dr. [[Satomi Ishihara]] (Japanese Foreign Ministry)
- **Returning crew:** 14 Marleyan nationals (no Eldian blood, no Titan Security Division), led by [[Jürgen Haas]]
- **Marleyan reception:** Admiral [[Konrad Verner]], Odiha Naval District Commander
- **Notable exclusions:** [[Elias Vogler]] remained at Grande Terre; [[Klaus Dreher]] held at Grande Terre; all 8 Eldian engineers remained at Grande Terre

## CATF Objectives
1. Open a communication channel — Marley knows CATF exists and is real
2. Non-aggression understanding — Marley's southern ocean is a shared neighbourhood; CATF presence is permanent and not hostile
3. No demands, no trade agreements, no technology transfer at this stage — the channel exists; the content follows

## Outcome
- Communication channel established: radio frequency left with Verner; no reciprocal envoy or facility access offered
- The Southern Venture's deck cargo (Land Cruisers, H125) remains lashed — visible as technological proof, never demonstrated
- Verner's report to capital drafted; military faction response pending
- CATF remained at Pier 3 confined; departed after two days on Verner's advice — before the general staff could order seizure or confrontation
- [[Klaus Dreher|Dreher]] remains at Grande Terre as the one Marleyan officer not returned

## Strategic Significance
The Odiha contact completes CATF's initial outreach to the two major powers of the AOT world: Paradis (Chapter 06) and Marley (Chapter 08). Both channels are now open. Both powers now know CATF exists. Neither has a clear framework for what CATF represents. The next phase of the Gate era — negotiation, tension, and the potential for alignment or collision — begins here.
