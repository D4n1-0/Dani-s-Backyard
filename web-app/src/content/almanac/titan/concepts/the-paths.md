# The Paths

## Summary
Invisible, multidimensional channels connecting every Subject of Ymir across space and time. Through the Paths, biological matter, ancestral memories, will, and regeneration transmit instantaneously.

## Details
The nexus of all Paths is the Coordinate — a towering tree of light synonymous with the Founding Titan — where the consciousness of Ymir Fritz eternally sculpts Titan bodies from otherworldly soil. A Shifter heals in seconds because Ymir labours for eons within the Paths. The Attack Titan uniquely perceives memories of future inheritors, creating deterministic time loops.

## Context
The modern world has no equivalent mechanism. The Paths are the defining metaphysical boundary between the two realities — a closed system of biology, memory, and fate that the modern world cannot access, feel, or verify, yet must treat as real if it is to negotiate with Paradis.
