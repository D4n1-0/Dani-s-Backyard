# Klaus Dreher

## Overview
Oberleutnant, Marleyan Navy — Titan Security Division. Age 34. Assigned to the *Pelikan* polar expedition to ensure it did not discover anything politically inconvenient. A true believer in Marleyan supremacy and Eldian subjugation.

The first AOT-native military officer to encounter CATF. Upon intercept and full disclosure, Dreher submitted pragmatically — not surrender but reconnaissance. He is watching, learning, mapping the base, counting the ships, waiting for an opportunity. CATF considers him the most dangerous of the thirty-one detained Marleyan nationals — not because of immediate threat but because of long-term strategic risk. If he finds a way to communicate what he has seen to Marley, the strategic calculus of the entire Gate era shifts.

Held under [[First Contact Protocol]] at the [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]]. Interviewed by a Chinese political officer named Wei. Treated as a professional observer, not an enemy combatant — the distinction is deliberate and fragile.
