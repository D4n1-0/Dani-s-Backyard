# Cabinet Office Briefing Rooms

## Overview

The UK government's crisis management centre, located beneath the Cabinet Office at 70 Whitehall, London. A soundproofed, air-filtered bunker designed for nuclear war but repurposed across decades for every category of national emergency — terrorism, pandemic, financial collapse. A single long table of dark wood dominates the centre, ringed by leather chairs. Encrypted screens line one wall. A portrait of the King hangs opposite. The room has never before been convened for interdimensional first contact.

## Features

- **Briefing Room A (COBRA):** the main chamber where the Prime Minister convenes ministers, military chiefs, and intelligence heads during a national crisis. Accessed via unmarked doors and blast-proof corridors.
- **Encrypted telemetry screens:** real-time data feeds from the military cordon around the Anomaly, including vessel positions and portal status.
- **Ventilation system:** filtered air, silent cycling — the quietest mechanical system in British government.
- **Coffee urn:** a cheap aluminium vessel that has seen every prime minister since Thatcher. A minor but telling detail of institutional continuity.

## Current Events

The room has been convened by PM [[Stephen Davenport]] for an emergency session (Chapter 04) to address the theological and strategic implications of first contact with the [[Crown of England|Faithful Crown]]. It is the first time COBR has been asked to debate the nature of God.
