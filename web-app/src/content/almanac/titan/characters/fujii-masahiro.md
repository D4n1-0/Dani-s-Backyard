# Fujii Masahiro

## Overview
Fifty-seven years old. Japanese civilian master mariner with thirty-plus years of blue-water experience. Contracted by CATF to command the [[CATF Envoy]] — the sixty-metre motor yacht commissioned for diplomatic service. Prior to the Gate, captained superyachts and research vessels across the Pacific and Indian Oceans. Selected for the Odiha mission because he was experienced in independent long-range operations, unflappable under pressure, and civilian — a profile that fit the contact mission's deliberate non-military presentation.

## Background
Born in Kobe. Earned his master's ticket at thirty-one. Spent his career in the civilian maritime sector — never military, never government, which was precisely why CATF recruited him. The [[CATF Envoy|Envoy]] was the strangest command of his career: a ship built for diplomats, sailing into a harbour that no modern chart had ever recorded, following a steam vessel from the 19th century. He navigated the three-thousand-kilometre transit from Grande Terre to Odiha without incident, maintained the formation with the [[Pelikan]] at eight knots, and brought the [[CATF Envoy|Envoy]] into Pier 3 with the same calm he had used entering a hundred harbours before. His logbook from the voyage — written in Japanese, recording position, weather, and a single line in English at the bottom of the entry for Day 118: *Arrived Odiha. First contact. Ship safe.* — is now part of the CATF archive.

## Status
Returned to Grande Terre. The [[CATF Envoy|Envoy]] remains his command.
