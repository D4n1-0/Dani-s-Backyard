# Silent Chorus

## Summary

The Silent Chorus is the aerial drone fleet of [[Kaelen]] — matte-grey, near-silent craft shaped like elongated tears that cruise at altitudes beyond any biplane's ceiling, lingering over warzones, cities, and farmlands for hours or days. They form the regional surveillance layer of the Mandate's enforcement triad.

## Details

### Design and Behaviour

Silent Chorus drones are winged craft approximately 4–6 metres in wingspan, with no visible propulsion — no propellers, no exhaust, no engine noise beyond a faint electric hum audible only at close range. Their matte-grey finish resists optical tracking; anti-aircraft gunners report that they "disappear" against cloud cover and are nearly invisible against the night sky.

Flight patterns vary:

- **Circuit drones** follow predictable routes, appearing over the same regions on regular schedules. These are assumed to be routine surveillance platforms.
- **Response drones** appear without warning, loitering over specific sites for hours or days before departing. These are assumed to be investigating potential violations flagged by the [[Eyes of Heaven]].

### Capabilities

Drones carry optical and thermal sensors of unknown resolution. They have never been observed to carry weapons — their function is pure surveillance. However, their presence over a battlefield or industrial site is understood as a targeting feed: if a drone lingers, a [[The Silent Spires|Silent Spire]] has likely already calculated a firing solution.

### Operational Ceiling

Biplane interceptors cannot reach drone altitudes. The highest-performing biplane fighters of the [[Technate of Vorr]] have a service ceiling of approximately 9,000 metres; Silent Chorus drones cruise at an estimated 15,000–20,000 metres. Airship captains report seeing them drifting *above* their own maximum altitude. The drones are, for all practical purposes, untouchable.

### Cultural Impact

"Don't point a gun at the sky" is a Scattered Realms proverb taught to children. Soldiers on both sides of a battle will hold fire — even at the cost of tactical advantage — when a drone passes overhead, lest they be mistaken for attempting to neutralise an observer. Some veteran regiments have adopted the practice of lowering all weapons and standing at attention when a drone is sighted, a ritual born of pure survival instinct.

## Context

The Silent Chorus bridges the strategic surveillance of the Eyes of Heaven and the ground-level verification of [[The Observers]]. A drone sighting is a warning: *you are being watched*. A drone that lingers is a death sentence counting down.
