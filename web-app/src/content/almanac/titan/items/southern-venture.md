# Southern Venture

## Description
A medium-size commercial cargo vessel chartered out of Singapore for CATF support operations. Fitted with a containerised helipad for an Airbus H125 helicopter, deck tie-downs for five armoured Toyota Land Cruisers, fuel bladders, generators, water purification units, and medical supplies. Civilian-crewed, no visible armament. Functionally indistinguishable from any merchant ship in the southern trade routes — which was the point.

## Function
Capability-demonstration platform for CATF's first contact with Marley. The Land Cruisers and H125 were unloaded at Odiha as proof that the returning crew's story was true — not as a threat display, but as evidence. The *Southern Venture* also served as a logistics carrier, delivering enough supplies to sustain the diplomatic delegation for an extended stay if required.

## History
Chartered for the Odiha mission. Her deck cargo — five Toyota Land Cruisers, an H125 helicopter, and support equipment — was the material proof that Admiral [[Konrad Verner]] needed to confirm the *Pelikan* crew's account. The helicopter demonstration, launched from the *Southern Venture*'s deck, was the single most impactful visual of the entire contact.
