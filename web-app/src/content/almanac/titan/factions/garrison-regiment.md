# Garrison Regiment

## Overview
The largest of Paradis's three military branches, tasked with guarding the Walls and maintaining order within their territory. Garrison soldiers are stationed at every Wall gate, district checkpoint, and patrol route across Walls Maria, Rose, and Sina. Unlike the Survey Corps (which operates outside the Walls) or the Military Police (which maintains internal order within Wall Sina), the Garrison is a static defence force — its soldiers hold territory rather than advance beyond it. Post-Shiganshina, the Garrison's role expanded to include patrols of the new railway corridor and coastal positions as Paradis militarises against the Marleyan threat.

## Role
- Man Wall cannons and gate defences
- Patrol reclaimed territory in Wall Maria
- Secure the railway and coastal construction projects
- Provide auxiliary cavalry and logistical support to Survey Corps operations
