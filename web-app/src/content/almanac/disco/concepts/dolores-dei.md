# Dolores Dei

## Summary
The fifth Innocence — Innocence of humanism, internationalism, and the welfare state. She created the [[Moralist International]], codified parliamentary democracy, and ushered in the Interisolary Age. Reigned 22 years before being assassinated by her own security detail.

## Details
Dolores Dei was advisor to Queen Irene La Navigateur of [[Suresne]] before her election. She presided over the discovery of [[Insulinde]] and the first interisolary contact. The Dolorian Century is remembered as a golden age of discovery and innovation. Her churches — including the [[Dolorian Church of Humanity in Martinaise]] — dot the Reál Belt.

## Context
Dolorians (Moralists) believe they continue her humanist project. Others say they're just technocrats. Her assassination is a foundational trauma for the Moralintern. No Innocence since has had her scope of influence.
