# People's Liberation Army Navy

## Overview
Naval warfare branch of the People's Liberation Army. The world's largest navy by hull count, operating aircraft carriers, cruisers, destroyers, frigates, submarines, and a vast maritime militia auxiliary. Organised into three theater fleets: North Sea Fleet (Yellow Sea/Bohai), East Sea Fleet (East China Sea), and South Sea Fleet (South China Sea).

## Structure
- Northern Theater Command Navy (Yellow Sea/Bohai)
- Destroyer flotillas (Type 055 cruisers, Type 052D destroyers)
- Submarine force (nuclear and conventional)
- Naval aviation
- PLA Marine Corps
- Maritime militia auxiliary

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- A Type 055 cruiser has been dispatched and is en route.
- A maritime militia flotilla is assembling to "observe."

## Relationships
- [[China Coast Guard]] — separate service but coordinated; the CCG are first on scene
- [[Republic of Korea Navy]] — rival; overlapping EEZ claims in the Yellow Sea
- [[United States Seventh Fleet]] — strategic adversary; likely to converge on the Gate site

## Current Status
Vice Admiral [[Zhang Haifeng]] has ordered the [[CNS Nanchang (101)]], a Type 055 cruiser, to make best speed toward the Gate site. A maritime militia flotilla is assembling for "observation." The PLAN is poised to assert control — but the diplomatic dimension is still unresolved.
