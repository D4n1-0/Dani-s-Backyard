# Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)

## Overview
The four-nation combined military and scientific command established under the [[Four-Power Gate Protocol]]. Composed of the Republic of Korea, the People's Republic of China, the United States, and Japan. Governed by a [[Quadripartite Executive Committee]]: [[Lee Jun-ho]] (ROK), [[Zheng Weimin]] (PRC), [[Elizabeth Cross]] (US), and [[Nakamura Kenji]] (Japan).

CATF is both inheritor and expansion of the original Seoul-Beijing framework — the handshake between [[Lee Jun-ho]] and [[Zheng Weimin]] that held the Gate's exclusion zone for its first eight days now scaled to a four-nation standing command with assets on both sides of the threshold.

## Assets
- **Task Group CATF-Alpha:** four destroyers, patrol boats, support vessels — operating entirely on the AOT side
- **[[Alfred Faure Forward Operating Base]]:** 500 personnel, expeditionary runway, helicopter operations
- **[[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]]:** full-spectrum command, intelligence, logistics, and fleet anchorage
- **[[Undersea Sensor Array]]:** 40-node hydrophone network, 100-km arc
- **Drone Intelligence Section:** KUS-15 and other long-range UAVs, operated by [[Tanaka Kenji]]
- **Scientific complement:** joint research staff from [[Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology|KIOST]], [[State Oceanic Administration (PRC)|SOA]], and participating Japanese/US institutions

## Current Status
Fully operational. Undetected by the broader AOT world as of Day 43, though the capture of the *Pelikan* means first contact has occurred with thirty-one AOT-native individuals. The "undetected era" is over; the strategic clock is ticking.
