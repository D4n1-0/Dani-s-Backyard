# Innocences

## Summary
Quasi-messianic figures elected by the [[Founding Party]] — only six have been elected in ~8,000 years of recorded history. Each Innocence reshapes civilisation in their image.

## Details
An Innocence is not born — they are elected by the Founding Party, the world's oldest international organisation, created by the first Innocence (Pius, the Perikarnassian). The legal system of the Reál Belt is built from the ground up to accommodate innocentic rule if an Innocence is elected in this time.

The six known Innocences in order:
1. Pius (the Perikarnassian) — invented god, equality, gold standard
2–3. Unknown
4. [[Franconegro]] — ended serfdom, established the reál, invented the nation
5. [[Dolores Dei]] — humanism, internationalism, Moralintern; assassinated after 22 years
6. [[Sola]] — resigned after assassination attempt

## Context
There has not been an Innocence in the Current Century. The vacancy shapes interisolary politics — the Moralintern operates without its spiritual centre.
