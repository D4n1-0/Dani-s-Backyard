# Cuno

## Summary
A twelve-year-old boy of [[Martinaise]], born ~'39. Lives in the fishing village. Aggressively foul-mouthed, speed-addicted, father-beaten. One of the most memorable inhabitants of the district.

In some timelines, Cuno joins the [[Revachol Citizens Militia|RCM]] as a junior officer if Kim is shot. In all timelines, he is a child of the forgotten — the human cost of a district the Coalition abandoned.
