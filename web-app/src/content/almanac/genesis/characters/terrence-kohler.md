# Terrence Kohler

## Description
Rear Admiral, United States Navy, Commander Carrier Strike Group Two. A career surface warfare officer who rose through the destroyer fleet before shifting to carrier command. His face carries the permanent squint of someone who has spent thirty years staring at horizons. Speaks with the economy of a man who learned early that words on a bridge wing are currency — spend them carefully.

## Background
Kohler assumed command of Carrier Strike Group Two eighteen months before the Anomaly appeared. He knows [[Daniel Brennan|Brennan]] well — served as Brennan's operations officer earlier in his career — and trusts his captains to execute without hand-holding. His instinct in a crisis is to convene, not to direct. He doesn't believe in being the smartest person in the room; he believes in building a room that produces the smartest answer.

## Relationships
- [[Daniel Brennan]] — his most trusted captain, now his subordinate in the portal cordon
- [[James Hawthorne]] — respects the British captain's restraint and moral clarity
- [[Étienne Roussel]] — the French commander whose *laïcité*-driven arguments he finds principled but impractical
