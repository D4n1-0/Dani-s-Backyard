# Rendezvous Grassland

## Overview
An unremarkable patch of open grassland approximately 30 kilometres from the eastern railway terminus on [[Paradis Island]], outside the outermost Wall (Maria). The site is flat, treeless, and uninhabited — an empty stretch of volcanic soil and short grass selected by CATF because it offered unobstructed helicopter landing zones with 360-degree visibility and no civilian witnesses. The terrain is typical of Paradis's eastern wilderness: cleared of Titans during the post-Shiganshina purge, returning to its natural state now that constant human habitation is unnecessary.

## Features
- Flat, open terrain with unobstructed approaches from all directions
- Short grass on volcanic soil — firm enough for helicopter touchdowns
- No tree cover; the nearest forest is eight kilometres west
- No permanent structures; no patrol routes within five kilometres
- Approximately 30 km from the new Paradisian eastern railway and the coastal battery positions

## Significance
This site was chosen by CATF for the [[CATF–Survey Corps Rendezvous]] — the first face-to-face meeting between the [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)]] and the [[Survey Corps]]. The flatness of the terrain allowed two H125 helicopters to land simultaneously with clear sightlines, while the remoteness ensured that no Paradisian civilians witnessed the machines. After the first rendezvous, the site was designated as the default meeting point for all future CATF–Survey Corps contact (second rendezvous already scheduled for one week later).
