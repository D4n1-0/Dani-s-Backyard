# Tanaka Kenji

## Overview
Lieutenant Colonel, Japan Ground Self-Defense Force. Intelligence officer with ten years' experience analysing satellite imagery of North Korean missile sites and Chinese naval bases. Assigned to CATF on twelve hours' notice and told only that his target set was "undefined."

Commands the CATF drone intelligence section from the [[R-V Isabu]] and [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]]. First to spot the Marleyan ports, the Mid-East War, the Walls, Liberio, and Hizuru. Built the complete political map of the AOT world from seventy-two hours of UAV imagery. First CATF officer to speak directly to an AOT native — conducted [[Elias Vogler|Dr. Elias Vogler]]'s interview under the [[First Contact Protocol]].
