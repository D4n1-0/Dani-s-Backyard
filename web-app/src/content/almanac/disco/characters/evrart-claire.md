# Evrart Claire

## Description

*Union boss of Martinaise. Formidable in all directions, a monument to the thermodynamic principle that power accumulates. Speaks with jovial menace — every smile is genuine, which is worse than if it were fake. Runs the Dockworkers' Union alongside his brother Edgar. The Hardie Boys were his enforcers; their deaths at the Tribunal have not diminished him. If anything, he seems more secure.*

## Background

*One half of the Claire Brotherhood. Controls Martinaise through a combination of union legitimacy, organised crime connections, and brute political savvy. Represents everything the Moralintern both fears and relies upon — local power that cannot be eliminated without destabilising the entire district. Offered Lukas a ham sandwich and a threat in the same breath.*

## Relationships

- [[Joyce Messier]] — *Adversary. Treats her as an equal threat.*
- [[Lukas Delacroix]] — *Wild Pines' new man. Assessed him immediately — smart, compromised, useful.*
- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — *His domain. The Hardie Boys are dead, but the union remains.*
- [[Hardie Boys]] — *His former enforcers. Killed at the Tribunal by Wild Pines mercenaries.*

## Current Status

*Still in power. The Coalition retook the terminal but has not touched the Union office. Untouchable, for now.*
