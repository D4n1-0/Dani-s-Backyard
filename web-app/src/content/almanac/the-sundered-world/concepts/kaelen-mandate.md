# Kaelen Mandate

## Summary

The Kaelen Mandate is the law absolute enforced by [[Kaelen]] upon the Scattered Realms of [[Gaethon]] — a broadcast delivered in every surviving language immediately after the [[War of Convergence]], imposing strict and specific technological and military caps upon every nation, enforced by orbital surveillance and ballistic missile strike.

## Details

### The Articles

The Mandate's prohibitions are precise, leaving no room for creative interpretation:

| Prohibition | Practical Limit |
|---|---|
| Exit the Industrial Era | No transistor-based computation, no jet engines, no radar, no nuclear science. Steam, internal combustion, and vacuum-tube radio are permitted but stagnant. |
| Army size | No state may maintain armed forces exceeding 250,000 personnel. Militia, police, and gendarmerie must not be used as disguised regulars. |
| City population | No continuously built urban area may house more than 1,000,000 souls. |
| Artillery range | Maximum effective range of any gun or howitzer is capped at 10 km. |
| Ballistic missiles | Forbidden without exception. Rockets restricted to short-range solid-fuel signal flares and fireworks. |
| Capital ships | No dreadnought-class (all-big-gun, turbine-driven) battleships. Pre-dreadnoughts with mixed-calibre batteries are the ceiling of naval power. |
| Submarines | None may be constructed or operated. |
| Radar | Any device for radio detection and ranging is banned. Sound locators and optical rangefinders are used instead. |
| Long-range aircraft | Only lighter-than-air airships may traverse distances beyond 300 km. |
| Heavier-than-air craft | Only propeller-driven biplanes are permitted. Monoplanes, regardless of wing bracing, invite a strike. |

### Enforcement Mechanism

Any detected violation triggers a ballistic missile strike from the **[[The Silent Spires|Silent Spires]]** — conventionally armed but precise enough to erase a factory, a fleet, or an entire city district. The surveillance triad — [[Eyes of Heaven]], [[Silent Chorus]], and [[The Observers|Observers]] — provides targeting intelligence.

### Known Ambiguities

The Mandate's wording contains deliberate grey zones that the Scattered Realms exploit:

- Mercenary Free Companies, technically stateless, can fight for belligerents without counting toward official troop caps.
- "Continuously built urban area" creates debate over green belts and satellite towns — how wide must the gap be?
- Biplane fighters with metal monocoque fuselages (but still biplane wings) occupy a legal twilight.
- The [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]]'s combined guards technically exceed 250,000, but each city is a separate sovereign entity — a fiction Kaelen has tolerated.

## Context

To the Scattered Realms, the Mandate is not a political document but a law of physics. It is as immutable as gravity and enforced with the same inevitability. The [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] reveres it as scripture. The [[Technate of Vorr]] studies it for loopholes. The [[Iron Concordat]] dreams of the day it can be broken. But no one publicly questions its existence.
