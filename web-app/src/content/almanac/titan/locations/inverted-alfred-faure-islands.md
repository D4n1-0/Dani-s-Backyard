# Inverted Alfred Faure Islands

## Overview
The nearest landmass to the AOT side of [[The Gate]], approximately 20 kilometres west of the threshold. A small archipelago of volcanic islands matching the topography of the terrestrial Crozet Archipelago (specifically Île de la Possession and its satellite islets). Due to the [[Inverted Earth|north-south inversion]], these features occupy the AOT world's northern hemisphere rather than the southern.

The islands are barren, wind-scoured, and uninhabited — a landscape of dark basalt cliffs, sparse tussock grass, and nesting seabird colonies. No permanent human presence exists. No evidence of current or historical habitation is visible from aerial survey.

## Features
- Volcanic origin — basaltic ridges and scree slopes
- Sparse vegetation — tussock grass, mosses, lichens
- Seabird colonies — albatross, petrels, skuas
- No infrastructure, no harbour, no landing strip
- Cold subantarctic climate even in the AOT world's positional summer (austral winter equivalent)

## Inhabitants
- None (uninhabited)
- Wildlife: wandering albatross, king penguins, subantarctic fur seals

## Current Events
Surveyed by Korean and Chinese UAVs during the initial week of drone operations. Confirmed uninhabited. The matched topography to Earth's Crozet Archipelago becomes a key data point in confirming the inverted Earth theory.
