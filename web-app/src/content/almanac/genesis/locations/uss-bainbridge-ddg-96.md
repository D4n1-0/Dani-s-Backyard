# USS Bainbridge (DDG-96)

## Overview
An Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer of the United States Navy, assigned as an escort for Carrier Strike Group Two. The Bainbridge had recently completed a shaft repair prior to the emergency transit, which gave her a slight fuel advantage over her sister ships — she arrived on station with the highest fuel state of the three escorts.

## Features
- Aegis Combat System with SPY-1D radar
- 96-cell Mk 41 Vertical Launch System
- Gas turbine propulsion

## Current Events
Holding position in the anti-submarine screen around the portal cordon after a mid-transit UNREP. Was the last of the three escorts to finish refuelling, still connected to the USNS *Leroy Grumman* as the fleet assembled.
