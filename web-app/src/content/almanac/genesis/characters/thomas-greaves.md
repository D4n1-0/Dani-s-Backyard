# Thomas Greaves

## Description
A junior officer in the modern Royal Navy. Served as the boarding officer on the RIB that made first physical contact with the Faithful vessel *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*. Competent, calm under pressure, and deeply aware that his name will appear in history books his grandchildren will read. A minor but reliably present figure at the edges of the first contact narrative.

## Background
Standard Navy career, commissioned through Britannia Royal Naval College Dartmouth. Volunteered for the boarding party without hesitation, understanding the historical weight of the moment but not the theological weight that would follow. Since the first contact, he has been assigned to liaison duties between the Navy cordon and Northwood Headquarters. He has not crossed the Anomaly.
