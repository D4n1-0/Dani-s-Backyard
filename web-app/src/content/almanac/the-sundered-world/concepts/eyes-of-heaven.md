# Eyes of Heaven

## Summary

The Eyes of Heaven are the orbital surveillance layer of [[Kaelen]]'s enforcement triad — satellites in low orbit that streak across the night sky in unnatural constellations, assumed capable of reading the heat of a furnace, the massed gleam of bayonets, and the tell-tale pattern of a forbidden assembly line.

## Details

### Known Capabilities

The precise sensor suite of the Eyes is unknown — no Scattered Realm possesses the physics to reverse-engineer orbital technology — but centuries of observation have produced educated guesses:

- **Thermal imaging:** The destruction of the [[The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr|Sunken Kingdom of Myrr]] was preceded by months in which a [[Silent Chorus]] drone loitered over the region. The strike came only after the drone's presence increased. Scholars conclude the satellites can detect industrial-scale heat blooms from orbit.
- **Optical resolution:** Independent astronomers using terrestrial telescopes have observed that Kaelen's satellite constellation maintains a consistent 72-satellite mesh in polar orbits. Each satellite's optical resolution is estimated at sub-metre — sufficient to count individual vehicles, identify ship classes, and distinguish military formations from civilian gatherings.
- **Signals intelligence:** Kaelen's satellites likely monitor radio transmissions across the entire spectrum, flagging unusual signal patterns — particularly those in radar-like frequency bands.

### Orbital Behaviour

The constellation operates in predictable polar orbits, visible from every point on Gaethon. Some appear to manoeuvre — adjusting their positions to overfly areas of interest. Others maintain fixed orbital slots. The fuel required for such manoeuvres implies propulsion technology far beyond the Scattered Realms' comprehension.

### Cultural Impact

The Eyes are visible to the naked eye on clear nights — bright, steady points of light moving against the stars. Children in the Scattered Realms know constellations not of myth but of surveillance: "the Watcher's Chain," "the Midnight Sentry," "the Last Star of Dawn." Astronomy, one of the few sciences the Mandate does not explicitly prohibit, is haunted by the knowledge that the objects being studied are also studying back.

## Context

The Eyes are the Mandate's strategic layer — they establish that nothing on Gaethon is truly hidden. Combined with the [[Silent Chorus]] (regional loitering) and [[The Observers]] (ground-level verification), they form a surveillance net with no theoretical gaps.
