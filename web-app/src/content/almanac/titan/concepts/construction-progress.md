# Construction Progress

## Summary
The phased covert infrastructure build-up on the AOT side, executed by CATF over Weeks 2–5 of operations. The build-up was divided into four phases: beachhead, supply chain, permanent installation, and fleet deployment. All construction was conducted under strict emission control (night operations, thermal masking, encrypted comms, no active sensors) at a distance of 8,000+ kilometres from the nearest AOT-native population centre.

## Timeline

### Week 2 — Beachhead (Days 15–21)
- **Day 15:** First LCAC crosses the Gate at 03:17 local. SEABEE detachment lands on [[Inverted Alfred Faure Islands]]. First three prefab shelters erected by dawn.
- **Day 15:** Runway survey completed — 1,200 metres of compacted volcanic scree graded.
- **Day 16:** Advance team lands at Grande Terre. Harbour survey begins.
- **Day 16:** First heavy equipment (bulldozer, rock crusher, desalination unit) transits the Gate.
- **Day 18:** Pontoon causeway sections arrive at Grande Terre, assembled by combined Korean/Chinese engineering teams.
- **Day 18:** Satellite terminal operational (linked to Japanese relay satellite over western Pacific).

### Week 3 — Supply Chain (Days 22–28)
- **Day 18–20:** Pontoon causeway at Grande Terre — first truck drives onto AOT soil (five-ton flatbed carrying prefab command module).
- **Day 20–21:** [[Undersea Sensor Array]] initial deployment (40-node hydrophone arc).
- **Day 21:** Alfred Faure runway compacted and tested with light aircraft.
- **Day 22:** Hydrophone array achieves IOC — first AOT-native steamship detected (185 km north).
- **Day 22–24:** Grande Terre fuel farm erection (500,000+ litre bladder tanks).
- **Day 24:** C-130J makes first Gate transit and runway landing at Alfred Faure.

### Week 4 — Permanent Installation (Days 29–35)
- **Day 29–30:** Semipermanent modular pier at Grande Terre (steel/reinforced concrete).
- **Day 29:** Pontoon causeway superseded; pier capable of berthing two destroyers simultaneously.
- **Day 30–32:** Command complex — shipping container grid, operations centre, intelligence fusion cell.
- **Day 30:** Alfred Faure FOB reaches full complement (500 personnel, all modules, helicopter pad operational).
- **Day 30–33:** Fuel farms complete at both sites.
- **Day 33:** Hydrophone contact — *Pelikan* inbound.

### Week 5 — Fleet Deployment (Days 36–43)
- **Day 29–35:** Task Group CATF-Alpha assembled — one destroyer per CATF nation.
- **Day 36:** *Pelikan* intercepted and impounded at Grande Terre.
- **Day 36–38:** [[First Contact Protocol]] executed — thirty-one Marleyan nationals integrated.
- **Day 38–43:** [[Frieda]] begins gas turbine familiarisation aboard [[JS Suzutsuki (DD-117)]].

## Key Milestones
- 500+ personnel on AOT soil by Day 30
- 1,200-metre expeditionary runway operational by Day 24
- 500,000+ litres fuel storage by Day 33
- 40-node hydrophone arc covering 100-km radius
- Four CATF destroyers on station by Day 35
- First contact executed Day 36 — undetected era ends
