# Tethyan Ocean

## Overview

The Tethyan Ocean is the central sea of [[Gaethon]], surrounding [[Kaelen]] and separating the three continents. It is the world's great shipping corridor and its most dangerous water — every vessel on the Tethyan sails under the permanent, unspoken awareness that at the ocean's centre sits an island that could erase them with a thought.

## Features

- **The Periphery:** Shipping lanes that trace careful arcs around Kaelen's exclusion zone — a radius of approximately 1,000 kilometres that no vessel enters voluntarily. The boundary is marked on every chart as "The Limit."
- **The Limit:** An invisible line beyond which no ship has returned in three centuries. Marked on Valdran charts with a simple annotation: *Here there is no law. Here there is only attention.*
- **Island Chains:** Scattered archipelagos that serve as coaling stations, telegraph relays, and neutral ground for international negotiations. Many are themselves near the million-soul cap, their populations squeezed onto volcanic slopes.
- **Deep Trenches:** Unexplored abyssal zones. Pre-[[War of Convergence|Convergence]] sailors' tales speak of lights in the deep, but without submarines, verification is impossible.

## Inhabitants

No permanent habitation within the Limit. Outside it, island nations and floating airship tenders form a thin civilian presence. The [[Federated Crowns of Valdris|Valdran Navy]] is the dominant military force on the Tethyan, its pre-dreadnought squadrons the de facto guarantors of maritime order.

## Current Events

Valdran survey ships have reported unusual thermal blooms at the Limit's edge — possibly volcanic, possibly industrial. The Chamber of Magnates has classified all further observations. Some whisper that Kaelen is *expanding* the exclusion zone.
