# The Signal Seekers

## Overview

The Signal Seekers are a quasi-monastic order devoted to recording and decoding [[Kaelen]]'s electromagnetic emissions. Using enormous directional aerials — the largest at their monastery in [[Hanavel]], with smaller stations across the Scattered Realms — they have compiled a fragmented "Kaelic Lexicon": catalogued signals, recurring patterns, and the haunting seven-note [[The Kaelic Melody]]. They are tolerated by every major power because their data is too valuable to suppress and their neutrality too useful to violate.

## Structure

- **The Abbot:** Elected leader, serving a ten-year term. The current Abbot is a former Valdran naval signals officer.
- **Senior Seekers:** Veteran analysts who have spent decades listening to Kaelen's emissions. Each oversees a research track — pattern analysis, linguistic modelling, signal geography.
- **Field Listeners:** Roving Seekers who maintain remote listening posts in isolated locations where Kaelen's signals come through clearest — mountain peaks, uninhabited islands, the [[Tethyan Ocean]] periphery.
- **Archivists:** Maintain the order's library of wax-cylinder recordings, paper transcripts, and the Kaelic Lexicon — a growing dictionary of identified patterns.

## Assets

- **The Signal Tower ([[Hanavel]]):** The primary receiving station. A massive directional aerial array angled permanently toward Kaelen.
- **Remote Listening Posts:** A network of smaller stations in the [[The Vorsteppes|Vorsteppes]], the [[The Vorran Massif|Vorran Massif]], and isolated Tethyan islands.
- **The Kaelic Lexicon:** Thousands of catalogued signal fragments. The [[The Kaelic Melody|Melody]] is its crown jewel.
- **Neutrality:** The Seekers are the only organisation permitted to operate in every major power's territory without restriction — their data is universally valued.

## Relationships

- [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] — hosts; the Hanavel monastery operates under League protection
- [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] — major patron; Valdran naval intelligence routinely purchases Seeker data
- [[Technate of Vorr]] — covert consumer; the Thought Cabinet likely receives Seeker transcripts through intermediaries
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — tolerated; the Theocracy considers the Seekers' work theologically neutral (listening is not speaking)
- [[The Unbound]] — the Seekers are officially neutral, but individual Seekers have been known to share data with Unbound cells
- [[Kaelen]] — the subject; the Seekers have dedicated their lives to understanding the Island, and the Island has never acknowledged their existence
