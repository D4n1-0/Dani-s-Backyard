# Dockworkers' Union Office

## Overview

*Second floor of a building that has been a shipping company headquarters, a hospital during the Commune, and an administrative annex under the early Occupation. Now the seat of power in Martinaise. Evrart Claire's domain. Desk too small for the man behind it. Ham sandwiches of suspiciously high quality.*

## Features

- Evrart's office — desk, chair, the architecture of informal power
- Meeting rooms — where union business and organised crime blur into each other

## Inhabitants

- [[Evrart Claire]] — Union boss. In residence.
- Edgar Claire — Brother, co-leader, currently absent.

## Current Events

*Operational. The terminal was retaken but the Union office was not touched. Evrart remains in control of Martinaise's unofficial government.*
