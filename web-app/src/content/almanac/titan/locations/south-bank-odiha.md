# South Bank (Odiha)

## Overview
A forty-square-kilometre parcel of undeveloped coastal plain on the south bank of the Odiha River mouth, approximately twenty kilometres south of Odiha's city centre. The land is flat, grassy, and well-drained — never farmed, never settled. The river provides a natural boundary on three sides. The deep-water channel runs within fifty metres of the bank, allowing logistics vessels to dock without dredging. The mountains to the north shield the site from prevailing winds. Designated as the site of the first [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)|CATF]] compound on AOT mainland soil under a 99-year renewable lease granted by the [[Marleyan Empire]] through the [[CATF–Marley Memorandum of Understanding]].

## Features
- **Deep-water approach:** Natural channel within fifty metres of shore — roll-on/roll-off capable
- **Flat terrain:** ~2 km inland to foothills, suitable for prefab construction, helipad, and airstrip
- **Freshwater:** River provides drinking water and inland access
- **Defensible perimeter:** River on three sides, mountains to the north, a single road connection to Odiha
- **Compound plan:** Pier, helipad, prefab buildings (barracks, operations, medical clinic, communications relay), fenced perimeter, dual-flag flagpole (CATF + Marleyan ensign)

## Inhabitants
- Planned complement: ~200 CATF personnel (logistics, engineering, medical, diplomatic, military operations)

## Current Events
Lease terms agreed under the [[CATF–Marley Memorandum of Understanding]]. Construction to begin within four weeks of Quadripartite Committee authorisation. Pier and helipad operational within six weeks; full compound occupancy within twelve weeks.
