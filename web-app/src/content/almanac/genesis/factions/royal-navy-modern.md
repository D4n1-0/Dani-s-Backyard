# Royal Navy (Modern)

## Overview
The naval warfare force of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, as it exists in modern Earth. Completely secular, technologically advanced, and utterly unfamiliar with the supernatural.

## Assets
- Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carrier (*[[HMS Queen Elizabeth (R08)|HMS Queen Elizabeth]]*)
- Type 45 destroyers (*[[HMS Dragon]]*, *[[HMS Defender]]*, *[[HMS Duncan]]*)
- Poseidon MRA1 maritime patrol aircraft
- Royal Fleet Auxiliary tankers and support vessels
- Rigid inflatable boats (RIBs)
- Modern radar, thermal imaging, and communications

## Key Formations
- [[UK Carrier Strike Group]] — activated for the portal crisis, built around *[[HMS Queen Elizabeth (R08)|HMS Queen Elizabeth]]*
