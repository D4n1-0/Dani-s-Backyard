# Yoshida Rina

## Overview
Thirty-seven years old. Japanese civilian physician seconded to CATF for the Odiha contact mission. Board-certified in emergency medicine with field experience in disaster response. Her principal qualification for the mission was that she volunteered when no one else would — a first-contact voyage to an unknown world with unknown pathogens and unknown risks was not an assignment that attracted a long queue of applicants.

## Background
Trained at Kyoto University Hospital. Spent five years with Médecins Sans Frontières in postings across Southeast Asia and East Africa. When CATF put out a call for a medical officer to accompany the Odiha diplomatic mission, she answered within hours. Her brief was twofold: maintain the health of the delegation during an extended transit through an uncharacterised biological environment, and serve as a medical goodwill asset if the contact situation required it. In the event, the contact did not escalate to a medical emergency, and she spent the mission confined to the [[CATF Envoy|Envoy]] — running daily health checks on the delegation and the fourteen Marleyan crew, and waiting for a crisis that never came.

## Status
Returned to Grande Terre. Awaiting next assignment.
