# Singapore

## Overview
City-state in maritime Southeast Asia. Selected as the neutral site for the negotiation and signing of the [[Four-Power Gate Protocol]] on Days 8–14 of the Gate era. The treaty was negotiated at a secure diplomatic facility in the Tanglin district — soundproofed, electronically shielded, a low-rise colonial building repurposed for classified work.

## Current Events
Hosted the first and most consequential diplomatic negotiation of the Gate era. The facility has since returned to its routine function. No public record of the meeting exists.
