# Federated Crowns of Valdris

## Overview

The Federated Crowns of Valdris are the premier maritime power of the Scattered Realms — a constitutional diarchy whose navy dominates the sea lanes of [[Occidens]] and [[Oriens]] and whose diplomats host perpetual peace congresses to limit wars before they escalate and attract [[Kaelen]]'s attention. They are the great compromisers, and they have built the most stable society under the [[Kaelen Mandate|Mandate]] — not through ideology, but through the practical necessity of the [[Valdran Provision System]].

## Structure

- **The Diarchy:** Two hereditary monarchs — the Diarch of the Eastern Crown (maritime affairs, navy) and the Diarch of the Western Crown (continental affairs, diplomacy). Both must concur on major decisions.
- **Chamber of Magnates:** 144 noble houses representing the Crowns' constituent territories. Legislative body; the true arena of Valdran politics.
- **Census Bureau:** The secret-police apparatus responsible for population monitoring. Frankly more powerful than the Chamber — for letting [[Valdris Prime]] tip over the million mark is a capital crime, and the Bureau decides who counts.
- **Provision Office:** Manages housing allocation, ration-card distribution, and the [[Valdran Provision System]] that keeps transmigrant families fed and sheltered when they arrive in a new town with nothing but a suitcase.

## Assets

- **Valdran Navy:** 248,000 personnel, twelve *[[Steel Sovereign-class]]* pre-dreadnoughts, a swarm of torpedo sloops, and a fleet air arm of biplane scouts launched from catapult-equipped cruisers.
- **Mercantile Fleet:** Valdran-flagged airships and steamers carry cargo and passengers across the [[Tethyan Ocean]] periphery.
- **Diplomatic Network:** Permanent embassies in every major power's capital. The Valdran diplomatic corps is the closest thing the Scattered Realms have to a multilateral institution.

## Relationships

- [[Iron Concordat]] — wary respect; Valdris supplies the Concordat with maritime transport, carefully avoiding any appearance of military alliance
- [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] — economic partners; Valdran naval protection in exchange for favourable trade terms
- [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame]] — frigid tolerance; Valdris denies Inquisition access to its territories, which the Theocracy resents but cannot challenge without provoking a naval confrontation
- [[Technate of Vorr]] — nervous fascination; Valdran intelligence monitors Vorran grey-zone exploits with a mixture of admiration and dread
- [[Kaelen]] — the permanent question; the Chamber is currently debating whether to send the first diplomatic mission in two centuries
