# USS Stout (DDG-55)

## Overview
An Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer of the United States Navy, assigned as an escort for Carrier Strike Group Two. The Stout was the lowest on fuel of all three escorts during the transit to the portal, arriving on station with approximately nine percent remaining before a mid-transit UNREP raised her to sustainable levels.

## Features
- Aegis Combat System with SPY-1D radar
- 90-cell Mk 41 Vertical Launch System (forward loadout reduced compared to later flights)
- Gas turbine propulsion
## Current Events
Damaged in a collision with *[[HMS Resolute]]* while pursuing *[[The Heretic Submarine]]* at the portal threshold. The *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|Stout]]*'s bow was damaged in the impact, but she successfully reacquired and destroyed the Heretic vessel with a single Mk 54 torpedo. Currently undergoing damage assessment and temporary repairs at the portal cordon.
