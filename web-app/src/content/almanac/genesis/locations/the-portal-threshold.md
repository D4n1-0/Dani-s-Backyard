# The Portal Threshold

## Overview
The exact midpoint of [[The Anomaly]] at sea level, where the waters of both worlds meet in a glassy stillness. It carries the scent of coal-smoke, incense, and dried blood on the Faithful side, and the clean salt air of the North Atlantic on the modern side. A pair of buoys — one modern, one an iron cask painted with a cross — mark the boundary. It has become a de facto neutral zone between two Earths.

## Features
- The water becomes unnaturally still near the threshold
- Air temperature shifts between the two sides
- Distorted vision through the shimmering curtain
- Buoys marking the exact midpoint for navigation
- Boats from both worlds shuttle along the boundary without crossing

## Inhabitants
Neither side has claimed it. It is a neutral zone, now serving as an improvised diplomatic station with rotating watch boats from both navies.

## Current Events
First contact parley occurred here between [[Rebecca Osei]] and [[Father Beauchamp]]. As of Chapter 02, the first larger Faithful delegation — twelve unarmed scientists and priests — has crossed into modern Earth. The threshold now functions as the busiest border crossing in human history.
