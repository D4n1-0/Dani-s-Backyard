# Le Retour

## Summary

*The Return. The expiration of the Coalition's mandate to occupy Revachol, scheduled for May 1951. A political event of uncertain outcome — liberation, chaos, or something worse. Looms over every calculation in Revachol.*

## Details

*Le Retour is the date when the Coalition's legal authority over the Zone of Control formally expires. What happens after is unknown. Some expect the Coalition to simply renegotiate and stay. Others anticipate a power vacuum that will be filled by violence — the Communists, the Royalists, the FALN insurgents, or some force not yet named. Harry Du Bois has predicted the atomic destruction of Revachol by '73. Le Retour may be the first domino.*

## Context

*Everyone in Revachol is living on borrowed time. The occupation has lasted forty-three years. Its end — whatever form it takes — defines the horizon of every character's ambition. For the Dockworkers' Union, Le Retour is an opportunity. For Wild Pines, a risk to manage. For the Coalition soldiers, a retirement date. For people like Lukas, it is the question of whether the cage he's building will even matter.*
