# Rules of Engagement

## Summary
The legal and military framework governing when and how the multinational fleet may use force at [[The Anomaly]]. As the fleet assembles, no one has yet written ROE for interdimensional contact — the gap between existing doctrine and the unprecedented reality.

## Details
Standard naval ROE covers piracy, self-defence, territorial waters violations, and escalation of force across established maritime scenarios. None of these frameworks account for contact with a parallel civilisation, theocratic military vessels, or the possibility of adversaries operating outside the known laws of physics. The logistics officer who flags the ROE gap receives no answer — no authority has the mandate to write them, and no one wants to be the first to define the circumstances under which the modern world opens fire on the other side.

## Context
The absence of ROE is itself a decision — one that becomes more dangerous with every passing day the fleet sits on station without a clear framework for action.
