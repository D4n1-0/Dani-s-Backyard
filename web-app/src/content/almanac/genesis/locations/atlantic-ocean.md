# Atlantic Ocean

## Overview
The body of water where [[The Anomaly]] appears — approximately one hundred and fifty nautical miles southwest of Cornwall, in the Celtic Sea region of the North Atlantic. The site of first contact between modern Earth and [[Trench Crusade Earth]]. Both the Faithful fleet (*[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*, *[[HMS Herald|HMS Herald]]*) and the modern Royal Navy (*[[HMS Dragon|HMS Dragon]]*, *[[HMS Defender|HMS Defender]]*, *[[HMS Duncan|HMS Duncan]]*) converge on this location following the Anomaly's appearance.

## Features
- [[The Anomaly]] — the mile-wide portal between worlds
- [[The Portal Threshold]] — the neutral zone at the portal's midpoint
- Naval cordon — modern Royal Navy exclusion zone
- Faithful fleet anchorage — TC-world vessels holding station

## Context
The Anomaly's position in international waters (beyond any state's twelve-nautical-mile territorial limit) becomes a flashpoint in [[Chapter 05 – Gathering Storm]], when France's [[Étienne Morel|Foreign Minister Morel]] argues under [[UNCLOS]] that no single nation holds jurisdiction.
