# Curse of Ymir

## Summary
No Shifter of the Nine Titans may live beyond 13 years from the moment of inheritance. When death occurs without consumption, the Titan power transfers to a random newborn Subject of Ymir.

## Details
The Curse is the biological cost of the Hallucigenia entity's bond — Ymir Fritz died 13 years after acquiring her powers, and the body of any Shifter degrades on that same schedule. The Curse defines the pace of the AOT world's politics: Shifter succession is timed, violent, and inevitable.

## Context
For modern negotiators, the Curse means every Titan Shifter has a known expiration date. Alliance guarantees, deterrents, and threats all carry a biological fuse. It also creates a perverse incentive cycle — you cannot stockpile Shifters indefinitely; succession requires consumption.
