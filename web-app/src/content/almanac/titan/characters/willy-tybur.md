# Willy Tybur

## Description
The head of the Tybur family, the Eldian noble house that secretly controlled Marley from behind the scenes. Inheritor of the War Hammer Titan.

## Background
The Tybur family were the first Eldians to turn against the Eldian Empire during the Great Titan War, collaborating with Marley. Willy Tybur served as Marley's de facto leader from the shadows until he revealed himself and declared war on Paradis Island, triggering Eren Yeager's attack on Liberio.
