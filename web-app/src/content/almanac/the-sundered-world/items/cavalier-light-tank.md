# Cavalier Light Tank

## Description

The *Cavalier*-series is the [[Iron Concordat]]'s primary armoured fighting vehicle — a light tank built on riveted hulls, mounting a 47mm cannon in a fully rotating turret, with a co-axial light machine gun for infantry suppression. Powered by a six-cylinder internal combustion engine producing approximately 140 horsepower, the Cavalier achieves a road speed of 35 km/h and an operational range of 180 kilometres. Armour thickness ranges from 8mm to 25mm of face-hardened steel plate — sufficient against small arms and shell splinters, inadequate against dedicated anti-tank guns.

## Function

Cavalier squadrons operate as mobile reserves, exploiting breakthroughs created by infantry and artillery. In Concordat doctrine, tanks are not independent strike forces but infantry-support weapons — they advance with the foot soldiers, suppress machine-gun nests, and engage enemy armour at short range. The 47mm gun is effective against other light tanks and fortified positions but would be useless against a hypothetical heavy tank — a vehicle no Scattered Realm can build under the Mandate's artillery range and armour constraints.

## History

The Cavalier series began with the Mark I in Year 245 PC — a crude box of riveted steel with a 37mm gun and a tendency to shed tracks on rough terrain. Successive marks have refined the design: the current Mark V incorporates sloped glacis armour (a Vorran innovation, quietly copied), improved suspension, and a more reliable engine. Approximately 600 Cavaliers of various marks are in active service, with perhaps 200 more in reserve or undergoing refit.

The Concordat's dispersed factory system makes precise production figures difficult to ascertain — individual homesteader collectives report their output independently, and no central registry exists. Foreign intelligence estimates vary by as much as 30%.
