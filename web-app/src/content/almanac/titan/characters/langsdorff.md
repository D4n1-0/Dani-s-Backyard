# Langsdorff

## Overview
Captain of the Imperial Marleyan Navy, commanding officer of the [[Steinadler]] frigate. Late forties, balding, a career naval officer who has been stationed at [[Odiha]] for three years. A professional mariner who follows lawful orders and does not engage in politics. He is not an ally of Admiral [[Strobl]] or the military faction — he is a sailor who was given an order and executed it until the order became untenable.

## Background
Commissioned as a midshipman and rose through the ranks over twenty-five years of service. Commanded the *Steinadler* for the past three years. Prior to Odiha, served in the Mid-East theatre aboard convoy escorts. His defining characteristic: he is competent enough to command a frigate and politically innocent enough to be used as a pawn by faction commanders.

## Relationships
- [[Theo Magath]] — professional respect earned through the *Steinadler* standoff; neither man regards the other as an enemy
- [[Konrad Verner]] — served under Verner's district command for three years; Verner has known him since his assignment to Odiha
- [[Strobl|Admiral Strobl]] — received the *Steinadler* patrol order from Strobl's office; executed it despite his own reservations about the political intent

## Status
Still commands the *Steinadler* at Odiha. Filed a report describing the CSG encounter as "a force exceeding the defensive capacity of the Southern Command." Unlikely to be promoted. Unlikely to be transferred. Unlikely to anchor in a construction channel again.
