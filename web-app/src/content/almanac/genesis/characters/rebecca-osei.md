# Rebecca Osei

## Description
Lieutenant Commander in the Royal Navy. Calm, diplomatic, and steady under pressure. Leads the RIB boat team to the threshold of [[The Anomaly]].

## Background
[[Rebecca Osei|Osei]] is selected as the modern Earth representative for first contact. She faces [[Father Beauchamp]] across the shimmering curtain and navigates the delicate theological questions posed by the Faithful, answering with integrity and restraint.

## Relationships
- [[James Hawthorne]] — her commanding officer
- [[Father Beauchamp]] — her counterpart in first contact; received his [[Orichalcum]] crucifix and blessing before crossing

## Current Status
Has crossed the portal and returned — the first modern human to do so. Spent seven days in TC London as a guest of the Faithful Crown, observing their streets and speaking to their King. Upon return, voluntarily underwent [[The Sifting]] at [[Inquisitor Paulus|Paulus]]'s hands — a deeper Inquisition protocol that found neither taint nor blankness, but *presence*: a soul touched by the Crown's suffering without being claimed by it. Delivered [[King Robert's Letter]] to [[Stephen Davenport|Davenport]] aboard the *[[USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)|USS Gerald R. Ford]]*, carrying the King's words across worlds. Now debriefed and awaiting further assignment.
