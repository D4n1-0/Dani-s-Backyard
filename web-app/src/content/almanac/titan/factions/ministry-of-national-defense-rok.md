# Ministry of National Defense (ROK)

## Overview
The cabinet-level ministry responsible for the military defence of the Republic of Korea. Oversees the Republic of Korea Armed Forces (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps). The Minister of National Defense is a civilian political appointee (often a retired general) who reports directly to the President.

## Structure
- Office of the Minister
- Vice Minister
- Defence Policy Office
- Force Planning and Management Office
- Joint Chiefs of Staff (operational command)

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- Minister [[Kim Dong-wook]] is the senior defence official in the crisis management meeting

## Relationships
- [[National Security Office (ROK)]] — coordinates crisis response
- [[Republic of Korea Navy]] — operational control through the Joint Chiefs
- [[United States Seventh Fleet]] — allied; Combined Forces Command

## Current Status
Minister [[Kim Dong-wook]] is advocating for immediate ROKN deployment and early notification of US Forces Korea, in tension with the more cautious approach of [[Lee Jun-ho]] and Admiral [[Yoon Seok-jin]].
