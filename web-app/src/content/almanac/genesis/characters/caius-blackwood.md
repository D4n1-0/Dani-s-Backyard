# Caius Blackwood

## Description
Inquisitor-Admiral of the Faithful Fleet, commanding from the [[Fortress of the White Cliffs]] at Dover. A tall, gaunt man of sixty-three, he wears the dual vestments of a naval admiral and an ordained priest — a cleric-naval hybrid whose authority spans both the military and the spiritual. Cold, calculating, and utterly devout. He commanded the Faithful Fleet at the Battle of Cordoba (1910).

## Background
[[Caius Blackwood|Blackwood]] has spent forty years fighting a war that is theology made manifest. He has witnessed the Tyrants of Hell firsthand and ordered ships to their deaths with a prayer on his lips. His office — Inquisitor-Admiral — is unique to the Trench Crusade timeline, combining the investigative authority of an inquisitor with the strategic command of a fleet admiral. He is answerable directly to King Robert "the Longsword" and the Synod of the Unified Church.
