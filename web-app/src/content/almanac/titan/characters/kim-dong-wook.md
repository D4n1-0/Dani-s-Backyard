# Kim Dong-wook

## Description
Sixty-three years old. Minister of National Defense of the Republic of Korea. Former ROK Army general — commanded the III Corps before retiring and entering government. Broad-shouldered, blunt-spoken, a man who has never learned to soften his opinions for political consumption. Believes in the alliance with the United States as an article of faith. His instinct in any crisis is to call Washington first and apologise later.

## Background
Graduate of the Korea Military Academy (Class of '84). Served thirty-six years, rising to lieutenant general. Commanded the 1st Infantry Division and later III Corps along the DMZ. Appointed Minister of National Defense eighteen months before the Gate opened. Known for clashing with the Foreign Ministry over issues of alliance management.

## Relationships
- [[Lee Jun-ho]] — respects Lee's intellect but finds him too cautious
- Admiral [[Yoon Seok-jin]] — professional relationship; both ex-military but from different services

## Current Status
In the crisis meeting at the Blue House, advocating for immediate US involvement and naval deployment.
