# Survey Corps

## Overview
The offensive and exploratory branch of the Paradis military. Tasked with venturing beyond the Walls to reclaim territory, study Titans, and uncover the truth of the world. Has suffered the highest casualty rate of any military organisation in known history — and achieved the most.

## Structure
Commander: [[Hange Zoë]] (14th Commander). Key officers: Captain [[Levi Ackerman]], Squad Leaders. Post-Shiganshina, the Corps is a fraction of its former size — most veterans dead, replaced by fresh recruits and traumatised survivors. The basement revelation has fundamentally changed the Corps's mission: from fighting Titans to navigating global genocide politics.

## Assets
- Elite ODM combatants ([[Levi Ackerman|Levi]], [[Mikasa Ackerman|Mikasa]])
- Two Titan Shifters: [[Eren Yeager]] (Attack/Founding), [[Armin Arlert]] (Colossal)
- Crystalised [[Annie Leonhart]] (Female Titan) — prisoner
