# CATF Envoy

## Description
A sixty-metre white-hulled motor yacht, repurposed and reflagged for CATF diplomatic service. Gas-turbine propulsion, a cruising speed of twenty-two knots, and a range of six thousand nautical miles. No visible armament. The CATF insignia — four interlocked rings — is painted on her bow. Her hull lines are clean and modern, immediately distinguishable from any vessel in the Marleyan or Hizuran naval registers.

## Function
First-contact diplomatic platform. Designed to present a civilian face for CATF's outreach operations — a statement that the coalition comes to talk before it comes to fight. Carries a small complement of diplomatic staff, communications equipment, and a secure data link to Grande Terre.

## History
Assigned to the Marley contact mission as the primary diplomatic vessel. Carried Dr. [[Satomi Ishihara]] to Odiha for the first CATF–Marley contact. The *Envoy*'s arrival at Odiha, following the *Pelikan* into harbour, was the moment Admiral [[Konrad Verner]] understood that the returning expedition was not alone — and that whatever had found the *Pelikan* was powerful enough to follow it home.
