# Insulindian Citizens Militia

## Summary
The army of the Commune of [[Revachol]] during the [[Antecentennial Revolution]] ('02–'08). Its décomptage dual-leadership system, developed from University of Koenigstein experimental psychology, was inherited by the [[Revachol Citizens Militia|RCM]].
