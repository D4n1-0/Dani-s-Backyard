# Anna Strauss

## Overview
Chancellor of Germany during the first contact crisis. A centrist from the centre-right, a lawyer by training, in her second term. She believes — with the conviction of a woman who has studied it, defended it, and lived through its tests — that the Grundgesetz is the most carefully constructed framework for liberal democracy in the world.

## Personality
Strauss is direct, precise, and constitutionally scrupulous. She does not offer what she cannot deliver. Her honesty about Germany's legal constraints — the parliamentary army requirement of Article 87a, the absolute conscientious objection right of Article 4(3) — is not defeatism but realism. She believes that democracies must remain democracies even in crisis, and that bypassing the constitution to respond to an interdimensional threat would be a victory for the wrong kind of thinking.

## Relationships
- [[Stephen Davenport]] — respectful. She offers him the truth she wishes other allies would offer.
- [[Robert Cole]] — distant. She finds his impatience with democratic process alarming.
