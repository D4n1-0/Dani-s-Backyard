# Alice Hartley

## Description
Flight Lieutenant in RAF Maritime Patrol. Captain of Poseidon MRA1 "Guernsey 01". Professional, calm under pressure, with sharp instincts.

## Background
A seasoned maritime patrol pilot who has flown countless surface-search patterns over the North Atlantic. She is the first human to detect and report [[The Anomaly]], observing it from 2,000 feet before descending to investigate.

## Relationships
- [[Sarah Vance]] — reported the anomaly to her command at Northwood

## Current Status
On station at the anomaly, monitoring from the air.
