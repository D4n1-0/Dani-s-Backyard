# Antecentennial Revolution

## Summary
The interisolary wave of communist revolutions that began in [[Graad]] in '02, sparked by a pandemic of tzaraath. Led by [[Kras Mazov]]. In [[Revachol]], it overthrew the Suzerainty, established the Commune ('02–'08), and ended with the brutal Coalition Landing.

## Details
In Revachol, the Revolution manifested as a civil war between Communists (Communards) and Royalists. The March Decree (7 Mar '02) announced the Commune of Revachol to the world. The [[Insulindian Citizens Militia]] (ICM) was the Communard army. King Frissel I was deposed and executed.

The Coalition's Operation Death Blow — the Landing of 13 May '08 — crushed the Commune with a three-part aerostatic bombing of [[Martinaise]], Stella Maris, and the Delta. Mass executions followed. Two million died in the Insulindian Deluge. The Commune officially surrendered in '10, but the Communist Party never formally capitulated.

## Context
The Revolution is the defining trauma of Revachol. Every institution, every faction, every character's family has a position on '02 and '08. The ghosts of the Commune are everywhere — in the ICM-derived [[Revachol Citizens Militia|RCM]] décomptage system, in the unrepaired monuments, in the mass graves at [[Ozonne]], in the living memory of [[Iosef Lilianovich Dros|the Deserter]].
