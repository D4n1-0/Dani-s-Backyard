# Étienne Morel

## Overview
Foreign Minister of France during the first contact crisis. A former professor of international law who entered politics late, he carries the precision of the lecture hall into every conversation. He is spare, intellectual, and utterly convinced that France's secular republicanism — laïcité — is not negotiable.

## Personality
Morel approaches the portal as a legal and philosophical problem, not a military one. He is the first modern leader to articulate the deep objection to the ark theology: that it reduces humanity to "cargo" in a divine vessel, which he views as an assault on human dignity. His insistence on a human rights framework for contact is sincere, not tactical. France will not accept a foreign church's eschatology as the basis for international relations.

## Relationships
- [[Stephen Davenport]] — professional. He respects Davenport's secular engagement policy but believes it is a holding action that cannot contain the theological dimension.
- [[William Ashford]] — occasional counterpart in diplomatic channels.
- [[Jean-Paul Delacroix]] — his ambassador to NATO, who executes the legal strategy Morel designed.
