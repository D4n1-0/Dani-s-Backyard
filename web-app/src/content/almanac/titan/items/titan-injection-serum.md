# Titan Injection Serum

## Summary
Extracted spinal fluid from a Titan Shifter — when injected into a Subject of Ymir, triggers transformation into a Pure Titan. The most terrifying biological weapon in the AOT world.

## Details
The serum must be extracted from a living Titan Shifter. Marley mass-produces general-purpose serum from Titan spinal fluid to create Pure Titans for battlefield deployment. Special variants exist: [[Zeke Yeager]]'s spinal fluid, when ingested (not injected), allows Zeke to trigger transformation remotely via his scream — a mechanism used to subvert entire populations.

Paradis possesses a limited stock captured from Marleyan forces, including the single dose used to transform [[Armin Arlert]].

## Context
For the modern world, Titan Injection Serum represents a biological weapon of mass effect — an agent capable of turning any Eldian into a 3m–15m class man-eating giant. This fact alone will dominate early military and ethical assessments of the AOT world.

## History
Developed by the Eldian Empire centuries ago. Refined by Marleyan Titan research. Used extensively in the Mid-East War — airborne Pure Titan drops onto fortified positions.
