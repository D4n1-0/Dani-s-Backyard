# The Vorran Massif

## Overview

The Vorran Massif is a mineral-rich mountain knot in southern [[Meridies]], riddled with coal and iron, where the [[Technate of Vorr]] presses against every boundary of the [[Kaelen Mandate|Edict]]. Its cities are built vertically downward into the mountains — catacomb-districts that blur the definition of "continuously built urban area" — and its factories are cathedrals of steam and steel.

## Features

- **The Catacomb-Cities:** Vorran urban centres descend multiple levels into the mountains. Each "district" is technically a separate administrative entity, connected by tunnels whose legal status as "continuous" is deliberately ambiguous. The true population of the Massif is unknown — estimates range from four to eight million, making it the most audacious census violation on [[Gaethon]].
- **The Foundry Levels:** Steam-powered factories stamping out biplane fighters with metal monocoque fuselages, "land ironclads" with sloped armour, and the famous [[Vorran 105mm Howitzer|105mm howitzers]] that reach *exactly* 9.9 km range.
- **The Thought Spire:** A sealed tower deep within the highest peak, where the [[The Thought Cabinet|Thought Cabinet]] preserves forbidden knowledge — theoretical physics, transistor logic, jet propulsion equations — passed down as a holy succession on paper and ink.
- **The Microwave Array:** An experimental relay network operating at frequencies just below radar bands. Technically not radar. Technically.
- **The Stratospheric Platform:** A test facility for the Technate's controversial meteorological airship programme, lingering near [[Silent Chorus]] drone altitudes.

## Inhabitants

The Board of Applied Industries, factory workers organised into industrial guilds, and the clandestine Thought Cabinet. No outsiders are permitted beyond designated trade zones.

## Current Events

The stratospheric airship test has been publicly announced as a success. The [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame|Theocracy]] has condemned it as a "stepping stone to a weapons platform." Vorr has not denied this.
