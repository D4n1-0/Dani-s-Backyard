# Étienne Roussel

## Description
Capitaine de vaisseau of the Marine Nationale, commanding a division of Horizon and Aquitaine-class destroyers detached from Brest. A Breton from a naval family — father served on the *Richelieu*, grandfather on the *Jean Bart*. He carries France's maritime tradition in his bearing and its *laïcité* in his spine.

## Background
Roussel was preparing for a scheduled Atlantic patrol when the orders arrived from the Ministry of the Armed Forces: detach immediately, proceed independently to the international waters off the UK's western approaches, and join the multinational cordon assembling around [[The Anomaly]]. He is among the first French officers to see the portal with his own eyes.

## Relationships
- [[Étienne Morel]] — the Foreign Minister whose legal arguments created the space for France's independent naval presence
- [[Daniel Brennan]] — opposite number in the US task force

## Current Status
Underway from Brest, leading his division toward the portal cordon.
