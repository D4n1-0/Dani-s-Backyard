# Niccolo

## Overview
Former Marleyan naval chef, captured during the Survey Corps' anti-patrol operations off Paradis's eastern coast. Initially held as a prisoner of war. His culinary skill — introducing sophisticated Marleyan cuisine to Paradisian ingredients — has transformed his status from prisoner to tolerated presence. Forms a bond with [[Sasha Braus]] over shared appreciation of food.
