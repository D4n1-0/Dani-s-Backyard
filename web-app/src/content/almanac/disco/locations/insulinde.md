# Insulinde

## Overview
The isola discovered ~380 years ago by the 8th expedition of Irene La Navigateur, Queen of [[Suresne]]. Called "the freckled face of god" for its 78,000 uninhabited islands in the Insulindian Archipelago. Home of [[Revachol]] on the island of Le Caillou.

## Features
- Le Caillou — main island, home of Revachol
- [[Ozonne]] — archipelago 80–100 km from Revachol; mass graves of the Commune
- Résurrection — spa destination established '18
- Laurentide — other end of Le Caillou; major city Deora-of-the-Seven-Seas
- [[Archipelagos]] — region with Croyant-Morain

## Current Events
The Zone of Control over Revachol is the dominant political reality. The rest of Insulinde exists in varying degrees of neglect, independence, and Coalition oversight.
