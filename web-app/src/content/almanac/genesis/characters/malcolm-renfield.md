# Malcolm Renfield

## Description

Fifty-seven. Career diplomat — served in five embassies, three war zones, one constitutional crisis. Deputy Under-Secretary for Unprecedented Situations, a title invented forty-eight hours before his crossing to give him authority to negotiate with a parallel civilisation. Calm, observant, secular. Stopped being afraid of being afraid decades ago.

## Background

Elevated by [[William Ashford|Ashford]] to serve as the modern world's first formal envoy to the [[Crown of England|Faithful Crown]]. Crossed the portal by modern helicopter into Trench Crusade London, where he witnessed the brutal machinery of the Faithful state — public penance, the [[Office of the Propagation of Virtue]]'s interrogation chambers, and the casual cruelty of a theocracy that has confused mercy with weakness. Filed a report concluding that the Crown literally does not see what it has become — eight centuries of war have rendered the society unable to distinguish between justice and oppression. Rejected the offer to stay. Departed after less than a day.

## Relationships

- [[William Ashford]] — superior; trusts Renfield's judgment implicitly
- [[Rebecca Osei]] — encountered her in TC London, where she had been living for seven days since her crossing; provided the ground-level assessment that informed his report
- [[Stephen Davenport]] — ultimately receives his report

## Current Status

Returned to the modern side. His report — condemning what the Crown cannot see — is the first crack in the diplomatic framework.
