# CNS Nanchang (101)

## Description
A Renhai-class (Type 055) guided-missile cruiser of the [[People's Liberation Army Navy]]. Displacement of 13,000 tons, length 183 metres. Commissioned 2020 as the PLAN's most advanced surface combatant — powerful enough to serve as an independent presence platform or carrier escort. Armed with 112 vertical launch system cells, long-range surface-to-air missiles, anti-ship cruise missiles, land-attack cruise missiles, and a 130 mm main gun.

## Function
Long-range power projection and anti-access/area denial (A2/AD) operations. Its presence at the Gate site serves as a strategic signal: Beijing is asserting a permanent naval presence.

## History
Dispatched by Vice Admiral [[Zhang Haifeng]] during the early hours of the Gate crisis to make best speed toward the Yellow Sea anomaly. Ordered to hold outside the five-nautical-mile [[Exclusion Zone]] established by the Seoul-Beijing hotline agreement.
