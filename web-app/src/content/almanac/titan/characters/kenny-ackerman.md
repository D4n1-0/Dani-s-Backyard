# Kenny Ackerman

## Overview
Serial killer of the Underground, later recruited by the Interior Military Police as leader of the Anti-Personnel Control Squad. Raised [[Levi Ackerman|Levi]] in the Underground — the only father figure Levi ever knew, and the man who taught him that survival was the only law. Ackerman bloodline, awakened. Died in Year 850 during the Uprising, entrusting a Titan serum to [[Levi Ackerman|Levi]] with his final words: *"Everyone is a slave to something."*

## Ackerman Status
As an Ackerman, Kenny shared the same biological architecture later confirmed in [[Levi Ackerman|Levi]]'s CATF biological battery: the cerebellar motor superstructure, the Path-immunity protein, and the stable germline modification inherited across 1,800 years. His abilities manifested as supernatural marksmanship, failed assassination of [[Historia Reiss|Historia]], and survived wounds that would have killed an ordinary human.
