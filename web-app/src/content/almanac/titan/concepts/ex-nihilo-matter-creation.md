# Ex Nihilo Matter Creation

## Summary
The discovery that Titan transformation creates mass ex nihilo — a 65 kg human body becomes a 32-tonne Titan with no observable energy source, no material exchange with the environment, and no thermal or chemical signature consistent with conversion. The process directly violates the first law of thermodynamics. The mechanism is unknown to modern physics. Only Subjects of Ymir can access it.

## Details
The finding was established by the CATF joint biological analysis team under Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]] and Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]], with theoretical consultation from KAIST physicist Dr. Nathan McCullough. Key observations:

- Mass increase: ~500× (65 kg → 32,000 kg) at baseline Pure Titan scale
- Energy input: the entire solar radiation received by the Titan is a fraction of what would be required to sustain its metabolic rate, and negligible compared to the mass-creation energy
- Conversion model: E=mc² does not account for the mass — converting the original human body would release ~5.8 EJ (1,400 MT), not 32 tonnes of stable biological tissue
- Candidate mechanism: transdimensional matter channel (the Paths) — the Titan is a conduit, not a source; mass flows from Ymir Fritz's timeline into the physical body
- Defensive implication: if the mechanism can be understood, it represents unlimited matter-synthesis capability

## Context
The EXNIHILO-1 finding reframes the Eldian population from a biosecurity problem into a scientific singularity. Every Subject of Ymir is a latent access point to a physics-violating energy source. Whoever controls the understanding of this mechanism controls a technology that makes fusion obsolete.
