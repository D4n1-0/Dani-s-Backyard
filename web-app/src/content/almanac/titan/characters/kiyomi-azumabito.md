# Kiyomi Azumabito

## Description
Ambassador of the Azumabito clan, representing the Nation of Hizuru. Skilled diplomat and merchant-royalty. Primary agent for negotiating Hizuru's trade relationships across the AOT world. Mid-fifties, sharp-featured, elegantly dressed, with thirty years of experience navigating Marleyan politics and Eldian diplomacy simultaneously.

## Background
Head of the Azumabito clan's foreign operations. Maintains the clan's trading presence across multiple ports including the Azumabito hangar at [[Odiha]] — a twelve-year lease that gives Hizuru a permanent foothold in the Marleyan southern continent. Has not yet made contact with Paradis at the time of the Gate's opening (arrival scheduled for Year 852 in canon), but the clan's intelligence network is centuries old and reaches every major port on the continent.

## Current Status
Made first contact with CATF at Odiha during the *Steinadler* standoff. Offered Hizuru as an alternative trade partner to Marley. Returned three weeks later with a full draft compact — iceburst refinement, aviation technology, diplomatic mediation — and a liaison office at the Azumabito hangar. [[Satomi Ishihara|Dr. Ishihara]] is recommending acceptance to the Quad Committee. The Azumabito hangar is being reactivated. Kiyomi's card is in Ishihara's pocket. The door is open.
