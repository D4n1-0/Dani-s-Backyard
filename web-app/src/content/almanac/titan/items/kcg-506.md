# KCG 506

## Description
Taegeuk-class medium patrol vessel, 500 tons displacement, crew of 28. Armed with a single 20mm Sea Vulcan forward mount. Length approximately 60 metres. Equipped with standard maritime radar, VHF/UHF communications, and a rigid-hull inflatable boat for boarding operations. Home port: Incheon.

## Function
Routine fisheries patrol, search and rescue, maritime law enforcement in the Yellow Sea. Not equipped for combat operations — designed to chase fishing boats, not warships.

## History
Assigned to [[Park Do-hyun|Lieutenant Park Do-hyun]]'s command for a standard three-day patrol when [[The Gate]] opened. First vessel on scene. Currently holding station at [[The Gate Threshold]].
