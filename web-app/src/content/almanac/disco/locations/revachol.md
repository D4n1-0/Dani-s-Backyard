# Revachol

## Overview
The capital of the world — once. Now a Zone of Control (ZoC) under [[Coalition of Nations|Coalition]] occupation since '08. A city of nine million (sixteen in the greater metro), built at the delta of the River Esperance on the north coast of Le Caillou, [[Insulinde]].

## Features
- **Revachol East:** The affluent side. Le Jardin, Saint-Batiste, Stella Maris. Headquarters of two of the world's five largest companies.
- **Revachol West:** Jamrock, Faubourg, Martinaise. The bombed, the burned, the abandoned. Giraffes roam at night — escapees from the Royal Zoo, 50 years ago.
- **La Delta:** The financial district. The Bank of the World, INSURCOM's command, the Coalition Warships overhead.
- **GRIH:** Greater Revachol Industrial Harbour — the contested heart of the economy.

## Inhabitants
Nine million people living under foreign occupation. The [[Revachol Citizens Militia|RCM]] keeps order. The [[Dockworkers' Union]] runs [[Martinaise]]. [[The Wild Pines Group]] runs logistics. Everyone else runs on speed, pyrholidon, or sheer defiance.

## Current Events
The Dockworkers' Union strike has concluded. [[Le Retour]] is ~1 month away. The Coalition mandate expires in May '51. The city is holding its breath.
