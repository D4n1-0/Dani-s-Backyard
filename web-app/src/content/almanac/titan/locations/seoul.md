# Seoul

## Overview
Capital and largest city of the Republic of Korea. Population ~9.5 million (metro area ~26 million). Political, economic, and cultural centre of South Korea. Located on the Han River, approximately 50 kilometres south of the Korean Demilitarized Zone. During the Gate crisis, Seoul is the nerve centre of the South Korean response — the [[National Security Office (ROK)]] operates from the Blue House, and the [[Ministry of National Defense (ROK)]] coordinates from Yongsan-gu.

## Features
- Blue House (Presidential office and residence)
- Yongsan-gu (Ministry of National Defense, Joint Chiefs of Staff)
- Jongno-gu (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)
- Incheon International Airport (major hub)

## Current Events
The crisis management meeting convenes in the Blue House at dawn, 06:15 KST on the morning after the Gate's discovery.
