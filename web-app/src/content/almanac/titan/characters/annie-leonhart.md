# Annie Leonhart

## Description
Seventeen years old. Blonde, cold blue eyes, compact fighter's build. Holder of the Female Titan — the most versatile of the Nine, capable of limited mimicry of other Titan abilities through consumption. Crystalised herself in Stohess after her defeat by Eren, entombed in an impenetrable hardened shell underground. Has been in stasis for months.

## Background
Raised by an adoptive father who trained her as a weapon, then — at the last moment before her deployment — begged her to come back alive, treating her as a daughter for the first time. That plea is the only thing she truly cares about. Her infiltration of the 104th was cold and precise; her defeat was caused by Eren's raw rage overcoming her technique.

## Relationships
- [[Reiner Braun]] — comrade; complicated
- [[Bertolt Hoover]] — fellow Warrior (deceased); unspoken bond
- [[Armin Arlert]] — Armin visited her crystal; she is aware, somehow
- Her adoptive father — the sole person she wants to return to

## Current Status
Crystalised. Alive but unreachable beneath Paradis. Her crystal is impenetrable by all known means — including the Jaw Titan's bite. Marley considers her lost. Paradis considers her a prisoner. The Gate may change whether anyone bothers to wake her.
