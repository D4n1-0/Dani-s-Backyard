# Theo Magath

## Overview
Commander, Marleyan Army. A competent field officer who rose through merit rather than political connections — a distinction that has kept him in the mid-tier of the Marleyan command structure while less competent men advanced around him. Pragmatic, analytical, and ideologically flexible: he serves the empire because it is his country, not because he believes its propaganda. Known for seeing through the narratives that sustain Marleyan policy — the Eldian devil myth, the Titan supremacy doctrine, the assumption that Marley is the world's only power.

## Background
Born in the Marleyan capital. Commissioned as an artillery officer. Served in the Mid-East war theatre where he witnessed both the effectiveness and the fragility of Marley's Titan-dependent military doctrine. His analysis of the Paradis expedition's failure — the loss of the Colossal and Female Titans — was circulated through the General Staff but not acted upon, earning him a reputation as a Cassandra. When Verner's report from Odiha reached the capital, Magath was the only senior officer who argued for observation over escalation. The General Staff sent him south to assess and decide — a compromise between the military faction and the cautious faction, neither of which trusted him fully but neither of which could offer a better alternative.
