# Inquisitor Paulus

## Description

An inquisitor of the [[Office of the Propagation of Virtue]], dispatched from the [[Principality of New Antioch]] to conduct an independent assessment of the modern world. Grey-robed, carrying an Orichalcum-tipped staff and a reliquary on a chain around his neck. Grey eyes that study the world with the detached curiosity of a scholar who has learned that truth is more dangerous than any blade. His voice is soft — the voice of a man who has spent years asking questions and has learned that the quietest voice often produces the truest answers.

## Background

Answers to the Patriarch of New Antioch, not to the [[Crown of England]] or [[King Robert the Longsword|King Robert]]. Dispatched following the Office's cable: *Verify before you venerate.* Arrived at the portal immediately after the parley and demanded to cross into the modern world to see it for himself — not hostile, but independent. Warned [[Alistair Pembroke|Pembroke]] that if his findings contradict the Crown's assessment, everything — the ark theology, the Royal Decree of Hospitality, [[Father Beauchamp|Beauchamp]]'s testimony — will be re-examined coldly and without sentiment. Represents the first fracture in the united Faithful front: the Office and the Crown moving in different directions.

## Relationships

- [[Caius Blackwood]] — the Inquisitor-Admiral who originally mobilised the Office's doctrinal analysis
- [[Alistair Pembroke]] — the Faithful captain he informed of his intentions; respects Pembroke but does not defer to him
- [[Bishop Aldric]] — fellow Faithful envoy to the modern world, but with a different mandate: Aldric seeks understanding, Paulus seeks verification

## Current Status
Has crossed into the modern world and performed [[The Sifting]] on [[Rebecca Osei|Lieutenant Commander Osei]] in the bowels of the *[[USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)|USS Gerald R. Ford]]* — the first time the deeper Inquisition protocol has been performed on a modern soul. The Sifting found something he cannot categorise: a soul that has been moved by the Faithful Crown's world without being claimed by it. Left the carrier disturbed and without conclusion. Departed for parts unknown. His report to the Patriarch of New Antioch — and its effect on the Faithful front — is pending.
