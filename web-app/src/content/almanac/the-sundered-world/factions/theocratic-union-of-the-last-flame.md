# Theocratic Union of the Last Flame

## Overview

The Theocratic Union of the Last Flame is the [[Kaelen Mandate]]'s self-appointed enforcer of orthodoxy — an absolute theocracy ruling the burnt-forest highlands of central [[Oriens]]. Its faith, [[Pyricism]], teaches that the [[War of Convergence]] was divine chastisement for human hubris and that [[Kaelen]] is the instrument of an angry God. The Edict's articles are holy scripture. The Inquisition of the Flame hunts heretics across national borders. And no one knows whether Kaelen's silence in response to Inquisition burnings is endorsement or mere documentation.

## Structure

- **The Hierophant:** Absolute spiritual and temporal authority. Believed to receive direct revelation from the divine flame.
- **Synod of Intercessors:** Advisory council of senior clergy. Divided between "Purists" (who believe the Mandate is eternal) and "Preparationists" (who believe God may one day lift it, and the Church must be ready).
- **Inquisition of the Flame:** The theological police. Authority to conduct purges in neighbouring lands, arriving with pro forma lists and leaving ashes. Feared across the Scattered Realms.
- **Flameguard:** A theologically vetted standing army of exactly 250,000 zealots. Soldiers must pass doctrinal examinations as well as physical training.

## Assets

- **Flameguard:** 250,000 soldiers. Lightly armoured by Concordat standards but compensated by fanatical morale. Specialised in counter-insurgency and urban purges.
- **Inquisition:** Embedded agents in every major nation's academic and engineering communities. Informant networks that make open scientific research impossible.
- **The Pyric Canon:** An authorised body of theological, legal, and scientific texts — the only "safe" intellectual tradition on Gaethon.

## Relationships

- [[Iron Concordat]] — mortal enemy; the Concordat's grey-zone exploits and liberation rhetoric are the Theocracy's definition of heresy
- [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] — frigid tolerance; Valdris denies Inquisition access, the Theocracy resents it
- [[Technate of Vorr]] — anathema; the Technate's Thought Cabinet and boundary-pushing are existential threats to the faith
- [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] — tolerated; the League's information networks are too useful to alienate
- [[Kaelen]] — the central theological question; the Synod is divided on whether Kaelen still houses living inhabitants, and whether it matters
