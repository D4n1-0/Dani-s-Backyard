# Martinaise

## Overview

*A coastal district of Revachol. Dock cranes, corrugated roofs, tenement blocks, and the persistent smell of salt, diesel, and failure. Once the heart of the city's maritime trade. Now a belt of rusting infrastructure and stubborn people — the kind of place that survives because it has forgotten how to die. Controlled in practice by the Dockworkers' Union under Evrart Claire, in theory by the Coalition, and in reality by neither.*
