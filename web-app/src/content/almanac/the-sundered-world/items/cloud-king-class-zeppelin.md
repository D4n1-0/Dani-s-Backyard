# Cloud-King-class Zeppelin

## Description

The *Cloud-King*-class is the premier lighter-than-air passenger and cargo vessel of the Scattered Realms — a rigid-frame zeppelin constructed at the [[Hanavel]] Airship Yards, with a duralumin skeleton, fabric envelope, and hydrogen lifting gas. Each vessel stretches approximately 220 metres in length, with a diameter of 30 metres, and carries a useful lift of 40 tonnes — sufficient for 60 passengers and their luggage, or an equivalent weight in high-value cargo. Four piston engines driving wooden propellers provide a cruising speed of 90 km/h and an operational range of 4,000 kilometres between refuelling stops.

## Function

*Cloud-Kings* are the long-distance connectors of [[Gaethon]]'s economy. They traverse the [[Tethyan Ocean]] periphery (always skirting [[Kaelen]]'s exclusion zone by a respectful margin), link the major cities of [[Occidens]], [[Oriens]], and [[Meridies]], and provide the only rapid passenger service beyond 300 kilometres — the Mandate's cap on heavier-than-air craft range makes airships the sole means of intercontinental flight. Valdran-flagged *Cloud-Kings* also serve as diplomatic transports, their neutrality respected by all powers.

## History

The *Cloud-King* design was standardised in Year 220 PC after a series of catastrophic hydrogen fires in earlier zeppelin classes. The current Mark IV incorporates improved gas cell segmentation, fire-suppression systems, and weather-resistant envelope materials — innovations driven by the lethal lessons of lost vessels. Approximately forty *Cloud-Kings* are in service across various merchant fleets, with eight to ten under construction at Hanavel at any given time.

The recent Vorran order for "high-altitude envelope materials" has raised questions at the Yards — the specifications exceed any civilian requirement.
