# Ahn Jae-hyun

## Description
Fifty-four years old. Director-General of the Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology (KIOST). Physical oceanographer by training, PhD from MIT. Spent his career studying the Yellow Sea's currents, sediment transport, and microplastic contamination — and is now staring at data that makes his entire field obsolete. Civilian in a room of generals and ministers, acutely aware he is the only person present who understands what the Gate means scientifically. Has not slept.

## Background
Post-doctoral work at Scripps Institution of Oceanography. Returned to Korea to lead KIOST's ocean circulation programme. Published extensively on Yellow Sea hydrodynamics. Appointed Director-General three years ago. Never expected his agency to become a first-contact organisation.

## Relationships
- [[Lee Jun-ho]] — respects Lee's deference to scientific advice
- [[Wang Lijuan]] — Chinese oceanographic counterpart; will soon become a colleague on the joint scientific committee

## Current Status
Deployed aboard [[R-V Isabu]] at the exclusion zone perimeter. Co-chair of the [[Joint Scientific Committee]] alongside Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]]. Commanding Korean drone, ROV, and hydrophone operations through the Gate. Has confirmed the [[Inverted Earth]] theory through cross-referencing bathymetric and topographic data against terrestrial databases. Has not slept more than three hours a night since the Gate opened.
