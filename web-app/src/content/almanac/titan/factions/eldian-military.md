# Eldian Military

## Overview
The military government of Paradis Island, established after the Uprising that overthrew the Reiss monarchy. A tripartite structure of Survey Corps, Military Police, and Garrison — now united under Premier Zachary's junta with Queen [[Historia Reiss]] as figurehead monarch.

## Structure
- **Premier:** [[Darius Zachary]]
- **Survey Corps:** [[Hange Zoë]] — offensive/exploratory
- **Military Police:** internal security, anti-corruption purged
- **Garrison:** wall defense and territorial control
- **Queen:** [[Historia Reiss]] — ceremonial; holds no military authority but essential royal blood

## Assets
- Three concentric Walls (Maria, Rose, Sina) — each containing thousands of dormant Colossal Titans
- Two Titan Shifters (Eren, Armin) + one crystallised prisoner (Annie)
- [[Iceburst Stone]] reserves
- Garrison artillery and cannon emplacements
- ~1 million population, approximate ~1920s technology minus naval/aviation

## Relationships
- [[Paradis Island]] — sovereign territory
- [[Marleyan Empire]] — existential enemy
- [[Hizuru]] — nascent alliance (not yet established at Gate-opening)
- [[Anti-Marleyan Volunteers]] — not yet contacted
