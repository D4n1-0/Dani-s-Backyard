# Eren Yeager

## Description
Fifteen years old. Dark hair, intense green eyes, lean build hardened by combat. Carries the Attack Titan and the dormant Founding Titan. After the Battle of Shiganshina and Grisha's basement, Eren now knows his father was an Eldian Restorationist from Marley, that the world beyond the walls is vast and hostile, and that he holds the power to flatten it all. He has not yet disclosed his future memories to anyone — if the Gate opens before the medal ceremony, he may not have kissed Historia's hand and received his vision of the Rumbling yet.

## Background
Born in Shiganshina District. Witnessed his mother eaten by a Titan at age 10. Vowed extermination of all Titans. Joined the 104th Training Corps, discovered his Titan abilities at Trost, and has since killed more Titans and fellow humans than any soldier his age. His moral trajectory is the central axis of the entire AOT world.

## Current Status
On Paradis Island, post-Shiganshina. Has just learned the truth of the world. Is processing the fact that his enemy is not mindless Titans but all of humanity beyond the sea. Has not yet received the future memories that will set him on the path to the Rumbling.
