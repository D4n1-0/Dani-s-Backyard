# Kessler

## Overview
General of the Marleyan Armoured Corps. Member of the military faction within the General Staff. The army's voice in the faction's leadership — a mechanised warfare advocate who sees the CATF technology transfer as a threat to Marleyan military independence. Less politically active than Strobl or Richter; his influence comes from commanding the empire's most modern conventional force.

## Background
Commands the Armoured Corps — the most mechanised element of the Marleyan military. Has watched the empire's conventional capability atrophy while the Titan programme consumed the budget. The CATF technology transfer could modernise his corps, but he resents needing foreign assistance to do it. Ambivalent about the MOU: he wants the technology but hates depending on the coalition.

## Relationships
- Admiral Strobl — military faction leader; Kessler supports his procedural obstruction
- [[Theo Magath]] — has known Magath since his artillery days; respects his competence but disagrees with his politics
- [[Hoffmann]] — General Staff chairman; Kessler's demands are always tempered by practicality
