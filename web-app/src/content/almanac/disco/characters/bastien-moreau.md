# Bastien Moreau

## Description

*Senior electrician, Wild Pines Group. Thirty-eight years old, fifteen years with the company. Build of a man who has spent two decades carrying equipment up and down industrial stairwells — thick shoulders, worn elbows on his jumpsuit, hair receding in the resigned way of someone who stopped caring about it in his early thirties. Eyes the colour of old steel: flat, hard, offering nothing. Competent to the point of being threatening. Quiet to the point of being dangerous.*

## Background

*Wired terminals in three districts. Has worked under eleven different project leads — most good, some bad, all of them with something he didn't have: connections, degrees, the right handshake at the right fundraiser. Watched Lukas Delacroix get the position that should have gone to a veteran and understood exactly why. Will not cause trouble. Will not be insubordinate. His professionalism is itself a form of protest — every perfect cable run an argument, every under-budget assessment a question: what exactly do you do, Mr. Delacroix, that I could not do better?*

## Relationships

- [[Lukas Delacroix]] — *Project lead. Twenty-five, brilliant, compromised. Moreau sees him clearly and holds him in quiet contempt.*
- [[Joyce Messier]] — *Executive. Knows exactly how she operates. Says nothing. Waits.*

## Current Status

*Deployed to Martinaise as part of the specialist team. Running power diagnostics, laying cable, watching the man in charge and filing his own silent assessment.*
