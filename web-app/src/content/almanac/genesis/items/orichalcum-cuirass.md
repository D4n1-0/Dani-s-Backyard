# Orichalcum Cuirass

## Description
A steel breastplate with a faint golden lustre, made of orichalcum steel. Worn by Chaplain-Lieutenant Morgan Pell over his black cassock.

## Function
Orichalcum is effective against all missile weapons, including infernal projectiles. Extremely costly to produce.

## History
The formula for orichalcum steel was discovered in 1503 by War Prophet Angelos, guided by St. Elegius.
