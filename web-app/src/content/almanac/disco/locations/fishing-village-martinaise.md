# Fishing Village (Martinaise)

## Overview

*A clutter of shacks and drying racks and boats pulled up on the shingle like sleeping animals. The tide goes out and leaves the flats silver and empty. The air smells of brine and diesel and the faint sourness of fish too long in the sun. A stone chapel with a listing cross — votives flickering inside, warmth and superstition. The jetty where Joyce's boat docked at dawn.*

## Features

- The jetty — small, concrete, slick with moss
- The chapel — squat stone, wooden cross leaning left
- Drying racks and net-stations — Tommy Le Homme's domain

## Inhabitants

- [[Tommy Le Homme]] — Fisherman, former dockworker
- Local fishing families — suspicious of outsiders, stoic about occupation

## Current Events

*Recovery mode. The villagers watched the Coalition retake the terminal. They have seen this before.*
