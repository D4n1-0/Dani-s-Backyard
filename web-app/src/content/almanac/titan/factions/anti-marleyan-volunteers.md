# Anti-Marleyan Volunteers

## Overview
A clandestine network of conscripted soldiers from nations conquered by Marley, organised within the Marleyan military itself. Formally led by Yelena and Onyankopon, the Volunteers mutinied against their Marleyan commanders and defected to Paradis in Year 851 — but at the Gate's opening (post-Shiganshina, pre-basement expedition), this contact has NOT yet occurred. They are still operating in Marley, awaiting their moment.

## Structure
- **Yelena:** fanatical leader; secretly devoted to [[Zeke Yeager]]'s Euthanasia Plan
- **Onyankopon:** engineer; genuinely committed to helping Paradis
- **Rank-and-file:** soldiers from multiple conquered nations united by hatred of Marley

## Assets
- Knowledge of Marleyan military technology, shipbuilding, and firearms
- Access to modern (for AOT) radio communications
- Insider intelligence on Marleyan fleet movements and Warrior operations

## Relationships
- [[Marleyan Empire]] — enemy from within
- [[Zeke Yeager]] — Yelena's true master; the Volunteers are Zeke's tool
- [[Eldian Military|Paradis]] — will eventually defect to Paradis; not yet in contact

## Current Status
Arrived on [[Paradis Island]] Day ~48 of the Gate era (observed by CATF drone surveillance). [[Yelena]] and [[Onyankopon]] met by [[Hange Zoë]] and [[Survey Corps]] personnel at the eastern port. Brought Marleyan military technology, naval vessels, and insider intelligence. Now embedded within Paradis's military-industrial apparatus. The Gate's presence remains unknown to them.
