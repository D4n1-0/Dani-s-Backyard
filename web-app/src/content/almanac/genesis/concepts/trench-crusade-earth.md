# Trench Crusade Earth

## Summary
An alternate timeline where Hell was opened in 1099 and an endless war between the Faithful and the Heretic Legions has raged ever since.

## Details
In this reality, history diverged in 1099 when the Knights Templar opened the Gate of Hell in Jerusalem. Technology advanced into the 20th century under the pressures of constant war, but society is deeply theocratic. Nations like the Crown of England, the Principality of New Antioch, and the Iron Sultanate hold the line against the Heretic Legions.

## Context
Modern Earth encounters this world when The Anomaly opens in the North Atlantic in 1914 (TC timeline) / present day (modern timeline).
