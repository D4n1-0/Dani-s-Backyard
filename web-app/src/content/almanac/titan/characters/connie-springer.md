# Connie Springer

## Overview
Soldier of the Survey Corps, member of the 104th Training Corps. Age 17. Survived the Battle of Trost (Year 850) and the Battle of Shiganshina. Known for quick thinking, loyalty, and a straightforward pragmatism that cut through strategic complexity. Assigned as Paradis's liaison to CATF during the first rendezvous — a volunteer, not a hostage — departing with the helicopters to serve as the Survey Corps's eyes and ears at Grande Terre. Motivated in part by the hope that CATF's advanced science might offer insight into the Titan-curse affecting his mother, who was transformed into a Pure Titan in Ragako village and remains immobilised there.

## Background
Born in Ragako village, Wall Rose territory. His mother and siblings were transformed into Titans by the Marleyan Titan Security Division's spinal gas attack during the Beast Titan's assault on Wall Rose. Connie was one of the few survivors. The knowledge that his mother still exists as a Titan — immobile, unable to return to human form, her face frozen mid-transformation in the ruins of his childhood home — has been a persistent, unspoken motivation throughout his military career.
