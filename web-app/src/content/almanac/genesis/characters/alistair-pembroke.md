# Alistair Pembroke

## Description
Captain of *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*, a dreadnought of the [[Crown of England]]. A devout and battle-hardened officer, measured in his judgement, quick to act when threatened.

## Background
[[Alistair Pembroke|Pembroke]] has served the Faithful Crown for decades, commanding the Atlantic picket line against Heretic incursions. He is the first Faithful commander to witness [[The Anomaly]] and must decide whether the strangers beyond it are demonic glamour or something unprecedented.

## Relationships
- [[Father Beauchamp]] — his chaplain and spiritual advisor
- [[James Hawthorne]] — opposite number across the anomaly

## Current Status
Aboard *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]* at [[The Portal Threshold]], having sent his after-action despatch to [[Caius Blackwood|Inquisitor-Admiral Blackwood]]. Maintaining the Faithful picket line while the investigation proceeds.
