# Tommy Le Homme

## Description

*Fisherman, former dockworker. A lean man in his fifties with hands that move through netting with the muscle-memory of decades. His eyes are grey — the grey of the bay before sunrise — and hold a gaze without effort. Speaks in parables and verdicts. The closest thing the fishing village has to a conscience.*

## Background

*Twenty-two years on the docks before switching to fishing. Left because the docks killed men — not the work, but the ownership, the strikes, the mercenaries, the bodies that turned up in the bay every spring. Chose fishing so he could die of something ordinary. Sees in Lukas the return of everything he fled.*

## Relationships

- [[Lukas Delacroix]] — *Wild Pines' engineer. A mirror held up to the boy — "You're here to win."*
- [[Joyce Messier]] — *The woman whose mercenaries killed the Hardie Boys. Holds her in cold contempt.*

## Current Status

*Mending nets on the chapel steps. Waiting. Martinaise has taught him patience.*
