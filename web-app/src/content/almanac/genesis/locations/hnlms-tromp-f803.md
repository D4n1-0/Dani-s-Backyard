# HNLMS Tromp (F803)

## Overview
A De Zeven Provinciën-class air defence and command frigate of the Royal Netherlands Navy, detached from a NATO standing maritime group in the Norwegian Sea to join the portal cordon. The Tromp took position at the northern edge of the multinational formation, her distinctive integrated mast and shaped superstructure making her one of the most recognisable hulls in the fleet.

## Features
- APAR (Active Phased Array Radar) and SMART-L long-range radar
- Vertical launch system for Standard Missiles and Evolved Sea Sparrow
- Command and control facilities — capable of acting as a task group flagship
- Goalkeeper CIWS for point defence

## Current Events
Holding position at the northern edge of the portal cordon, contributing to the multinational sensor picture.
