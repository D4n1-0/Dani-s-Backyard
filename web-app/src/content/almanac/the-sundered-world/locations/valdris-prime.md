# Valdris Prime

## Overview

Valdris Prime is the capital of the [[Federated Crowns of Valdris]] and the largest city on [[Gaethon]] — legally. Its population stands at precisely 999,200, maintained by the most sophisticated census apparatus in the Scattered Realms. It is a city of six-storey buildings, green belts, tram lines, and the permanent, unspoken arithmetic of the [[The Census|million-soul cap]].

## Features

- **The Harbour District:** The Valdran Navy's home anchorage. Drydocks service the twelve [[Steel Sovereign-class]] pre-dreadnoughts; coaling barges and torpedo sloops crowd the inner basin.
- **The Precincts:** The city is divided into nine numbered precincts, each capped at 111,000 souls, separated by mandated green belts of parkland and agricultural reserve. No building rises above six storeys — taller structures risk "overcrowding" that could trigger the cap.
- **The Census Tower:** A granite monolith at the city's geographic centre, housing the Census Bureau's secret police, birth registry, and population projection offices. The most powerful institution in Valdris — and the most feared.
- **The Chamber of Magnates:** A domed assembly hall where the diarchy and noble houses debate policy beneath a ceiling painted with Gaethon's constellations — including the orbits of the [[Eyes of Heaven]].

## Inhabitants

The Valdran elite — two hereditary monarchs (the Diarchs of the Eastern and Western Crowns), a Chamber of Magnates representing 144 noble houses, and a permanent bureaucracy of census officials, naval officers, and diplomatic corps.

## Current Events

The Chamber is deadlocked on the "Kaelen Mission" proposal. The Openers argue that three centuries of compliance have earned the right to dialogue; the Stabilisers counter that the Mandate has never acknowledged a right to anything.
