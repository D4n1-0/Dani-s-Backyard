# General Hoffmann

## Overview
Seventy years old. Chairman of the Marleyan General Staff. Silver-haired, straight-backed, a survivor of forty years of imperial military politics. Recalled from retirement twice because the empire had no one to replace him. His defining trait: he has never committed to any position that could be proven wrong. A bureaucrat who understands that his role is to manage the empire's factions — not to lead them, but to keep them from tearing each other apart.

## Background
Commissioned as an infantry officer before the Great Titan War. Rose through the ranks during the decades of Marleyan expansion that followed. Retired at sixty-five. Recalled at sixty-seven when his successor died of a heart condition. Recalled again at sixty-nine when the next successor resigned over the Paradis expedition's failure. Has been Chairman for four years across two administrations. Knows every senior officer in the empire by name and knows which ones are dangerous and which ones are incompetent and which ones are both.

## Relationships
- [[Theo Magath]] — endorsed Magath's Odiha mission and later confirmed his authority after the *Steinadler* standoff. Respects Magath's competence but will abandon him if the politics demand it
- [[Strobl|Admiral Strobl]] — military faction leader; Hoffmann grants him procedural concessions to keep the General Staff united without granting him substantive power
- [[Konrad Verner]] — served alongside Verner in the Southern Command decades ago; a mutual respect between old officers who understand what the empire has lost

## Status
Conducted the compliance review at Odiha. Confirmed Magath's authority with conditions (a military faction observer). Returned to the capital. Watching.
