# Choi Min-ji

## Overview
Physician, Republic of Korea Navy — stationed at the CATF medical facility on [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub|Grande Terre]]. Age 37. Previously a naval surgeon with the ROK 1st Fleet. Transferred to CATF medical detachment six months after the Gate opened. Calm, professional, accustomed to seeing bodies that have survived things bodies are not supposed to survive. Her detachment is genuine — she has seen enough trauma that nothing shocks her. Or so she thought, until she examined [[Levi Ackerman|Captain Levi]].
