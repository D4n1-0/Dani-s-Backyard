# Nine Titans

## Summary
The nine unique Titan forms that emerged from Ymir Fritz's death — each with distinct abilities, passed down through cannibalistic inheritance, bound by the 13-year Curse of Ymir.

## Details
The Nine are: Founding, Attack, Colossal, Armored, Female, Beast, Jaw, Cart, and War Hammer. Each Shifter can transform at will, regenerate from near-fatal wounds, and access ancestral memories through the Paths. The Curse of Ymir limits each Shifter's lifespan to 13 years from inheritance. When a Shifter dies without being consumed, their Titan power passes to a random newborn Subject of Ymir.

At the time of the Gate's opening: Marley controls the Armored (Reiner), Beast (Zeke), Jaw (Porco Galliard), and Cart (Pieck). Paradis controls the Founding/Attack (Eren), Colossal (Armin), and Female (crystalised Annie). The War Hammer remains dormant in the Tybur family on Marley's mainland.

## Context
For modern military planners, Titan Shifters are human-sized delivery systems for tactical-scale devastation — not nukes but precision WMDs. The 13-year Curse introduces a biological countdown to every negotiation.
