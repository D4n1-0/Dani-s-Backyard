# Lee Jun-ho

## Description
Forty-nine years old. Senior Secretary for Crisis Management, National Security Office, Office of the President (Blue House). Career diplomat turned national security operator — Foreign Ministry for fifteen years before moving to the Blue House. Speaks fluent English and functional Mandarin. Thin, precise, wears glasses, never raises his voice. His defining trait is the ability to hold a room together when every person in it wants to pull in a different direction.

## Background
Son of a diplomat, raised partly in Washington D.C. Joined the Ministry of Foreign Affairs at twenty-six, served postings in Beijing, Tokyo, and Geneva. Recruited into the National Security Office for his ability to speak to both military and civilian principals. Survived three administrations by being indispensable rather than political. Has never managed a crisis of this magnitude — but then, no one has.

## Relationships
- [[Zheng Weimin]] — Chinese counterpart; first direct contact through the Seoul-Beijing hotline
- Minister [[Kim Dong-wook]] — Minister of National Defense; respectful but often in tension
- Admiral [[Yoon Seok-jin]] — ROK Navy Chief of Staff; professional ally
- Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]] — KIOST Director-General; respects his expertise

## Current Status
Managing the ongoing Gate crisis from the Blue House. Has received the confirmed [[Inverted Earth]] finding from Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]]. Continuing backchannel communications with [[Zheng Weimin]] as the framework faces its first test: the arrival of the [[USS Milius (DDG-69)]]. The exclusion zone holds, but the political strain is mounting.
