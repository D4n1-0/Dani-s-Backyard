# Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force

## Overview
The naval branch of the Japan Self-Defense Forces. One of the world's most capable blue-water navies, operating destroyers, submarines, maritime patrol aircraft, and an escort flotilla focused on anti-submarine warfare (ASW) and air defence. The JMSDF is a close operational partner of the [[United States Seventh Fleet]], with integrated command structures and regular joint exercises.

## Structure
- Escort Flotilla 1–4 (destroyer squadrons)
- Submarine Flotilla
- Fleet Air / Maritime Patrol
- Mine Warfare Force

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- Japan has placed its coast guard on alert. No JMSDF vessels have been dispatched yet.

## Relationships
- [[United States Seventh Fleet]] — primary ally; integrated command and operations
- [[Republic of Korea Navy]] — nominal ally but bilateral tensions over history and territorial disputes
- [[People's Liberation Army Navy]] — strategic rival; Japan's southwestern islands are the front line
