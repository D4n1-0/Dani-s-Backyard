# Henrik Varga

## Description

*Commander, INSURCOM. Mid-forties, thick through the shoulders but softening around the jaw. The tired eyes of a man who stopped distinguishing between policing and soldiering two occupations ago. Uniform is correct but not crisp — worn collar, scuffed boots, rank insignia slightly faded. A career officer. No ideology. No fire.*

## Background

*Two tours in the Samaran Isolas before reassignment to Revachol's Zone of Control. Has overseen multiple labour dispute interventions. Treats his role with professional detachment — the dockworkers are not his enemies, merely his assignment. Finds Wild Pines distasteful but pays the Coalition's bills. Dismissed Lukas on sight, recalibrated slightly upon learning his credentials.*

## Relationships

- [[Joyce Messier]] — *Client. Professional respect, no warmth.*

- [[INSURCOM]] — *His chain of command. Follows orders, files reports, goes home.*
