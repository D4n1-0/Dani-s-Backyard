# Onyankopon

## Overview
Broad-shouldered, practical, carrying the posture of an engineer and the wariness of a soldier. Arrived on Paradis alongside [[Yelena]] aboard the Volunteer defector vessel. Unlike Yelena, Onyankopon is genuinely committed to helping Paradis survive Marleyan aggression — his hatred of Marley is personal rather than ideological, and his loyalty is to the people he fights alongside, not to Zeke Yeager's secret agenda.

Brings technical expertise in Marleyan ship design, firearms, and mechanical engineering. Represents the moral counterweight to Yelena within the Volunteer faction — the man who joined a mutiny because he believed in something, not because he was serving a prophet.

## Observed by CATF
First identified via drone surveillance alongside Yelena. Carried technical instruments during disembarkation at Paradis port. CATF analysts flagged him as a "technical asset" — an engineer with Marleyan military knowledge now serving Paradis.
