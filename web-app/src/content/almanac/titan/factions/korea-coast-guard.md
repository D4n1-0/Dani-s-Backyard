# Korea Coast Guard

## Overview
Maritime law enforcement agency of the Republic of Korea. Responsible for coastal patrol, fisheries enforcement, search and rescue, and maritime security. Operates heavy (3,000–5,000 ton), medium (250–500 ton), and light (30–100 ton) patrol vessels. The KCG is an external branch of the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries.

## Structure
- Headquarters: Incheon
- 16 regional stations, 74 branch offices
- Fleet: 4 classes heavy, 3 classes medium, 3 classes light
- Aviation unit: 6 fixed-wing, 16 rotary-wing aircraft

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- [[KCG 506]] — Taegeuk-class medium patrol vessel — first on scene
- Commanding officer: [[Park Do-hyun|Lieutenant Park Do-hyun]]

## Relationships
- [[China Coast Guard]] — frequent operational friction in the Yellow Sea; overlapping territorial claims
- [[Japan Coast Guard]] — cooperative, occasional joint exercises
- [[United States Coast Guard]] — allied, low-level cooperation

## Current Status
First responder to [[The Gate]]. Maintaining cautious containment posture pending instructions from Seoul. The KCG is out of its depth — it is a fisheries agency, not a first-contact authority — but no one else has arrived yet.
