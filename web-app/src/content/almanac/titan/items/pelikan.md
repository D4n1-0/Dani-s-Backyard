# Pelikan

## Overview
The *Pelikan* is a Marleyan civilian polar research steamship, approximately 40 metres in length, with a reinforced icebreaker bow and a single reciprocating steam engine. Registered as a civilian Academy of Sciences vessel under the Marleyan flag. Sponsored by the Marleyan Academy of Sciences with quiet funding from the Titan Security Division — the expedition's official mission was a polar survey of the world's northern ice shelf; its unofficial objective was territorial mapping for Marleyan naval expansion.

## Voyage
The *Pelikan* departed on a 47-day polar survey of the AOT world's northern ice shelf. After an undetermined navigation error (possibly magnetic anomaly at the Gate's AOT-side location), the ship drifted catastrophically off course — not through the Gate, but thousands of kilometres south across the AOT world's equator into its southern ocean. Over eleven days of lost navigation, the stars changed from northern-hemisphere constellations to southern patterns that no Marleyan expedition had ever mapped. The water temperature dropped as the ship crossed into the subantarctic convergence zone — frigid waters, thick fog, seabird species unknown to the expedition's naturalist. By Day 33 the ship was approaching the [[Inverted Alfred Faure Islands]], 20 kilometres from the Gate's AOT-side exit, drifting directly toward the secret CATF perimeter. One lifeboat was lost during the initial drift.

## Capture
Detected by the [[Undersea Sensor Array]] at 03:12 on Day 36 — bearing 0-1-5, range 140 kilometres, course south toward the [[Alfred Faure Forward Operating Base]]. Intercepted by [[PKG 721]] at 06:48. Boarded under the [[First Contact Protocol]]. Thirty-one souls aboard: 22 Marleyan sailors, 8 Eldian engineers, 1 Marleyan naval observer.

## Current Status
Impounded at [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]]. Crew dispersed under [[First Contact Protocol]]:
- Dr. [[Elias Vogler]] — employed as CATF scientific consultant
- Oberleutnant [[Klaus Dreher]] — held in pragmatic submission
- [[Frieda]] and seven other Eldian engineers — granted sanctuary and CATF civilian employment
- Captain Bauer (merchant marine) — cooperative, held in crew quarters
- Remaining crew — housed pending status review
