# Coalition of Nations

## Overview

*The occupying government of Revachol since the Landing of '08. Actually a coalition of nations — its most powerful bloc is the Moralist International. Administers the Zone of Control through military occupation (INSURCOM), economic oversight (EPIS), and police supervision (ICP). The Coalition's mandate expires with Le Retour in May '51, a deadline that looms over every political calculation in Revachol.*

## Structure

- [[Moralist International]] — Dominant political bloc
- [[INSURCOM]] — Military occupation force
- EPIS — Economic oversight
- ICP — International Collaboration Police (oversees RCM)

## Assets

- Full military control of the Zone of Control
- Political legitimacy via international recognition
- The threat and promise of Le Retour

## Relationships

- [[Wild Pines Group]] — *Corporate ally. Freed the terminal at their request.*
- [[Revachol Citizens Militia]] — *Oversees but does not trust.*
- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — *Tolerated as a necessary evil for local stability.*
