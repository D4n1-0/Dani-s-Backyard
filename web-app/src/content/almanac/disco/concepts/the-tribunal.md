# The Tribunal

## Summary

*The violent confrontation between the Hardie Boys and Krenel's Wild Pines mercenaries outside the Whirling-in-Rags, four days before the events of Chapter 1. All Hardie Boys were killed. Casualties on the mercenary side are undisclosed. The Tribunal broke the back of the strike and set the stage for the Coalition reclamation of the terminal.*

## Details

*The confrontation occurred outside the Whirling-in-Rags in Martinaise, during the final days of the dockworkers' strike. The Hardie Boys, led by Titus Hardie, confronted Krenel's mercenaries — hired by Joyce Messier to protect Wild Pines assets during the labour dispute. Shots were fired. All Hardie Boys were killed. RCM detectives Harry Du Bois and Kim Kitsuragi were present but could not prevent the bloodshed. Their investigation into the original mercenary murder had triggered the chain of events that led here.*

## Context

*The Tribunal is Martinaise's fresh wound. The bullet holes are still in the Whirling's plaster. The blood was scrubbed but not removed. Every character in the district is living in its aftermath. The Coalition used the power vacuum created by the Hardie Boys' deaths to reclaim the terminal. Wild Pines sees opportunity. The Union sees martyrdom. The locals see the latest chapter in a story that never ends.*
