# United States Seventh Fleet

## Overview
The largest forward-deployed U.S. fleet, responsible for the Western Pacific and Indian Ocean. Operates 50–70 ships including a carrier strike group, amphibious ready group, destroyers, submarines, and support vessels. Headquarters in Yokosuka, Japan with bases across the region. Primary mission: maintaining freedom of navigation and deterring aggression in the Pacific theatre.

## Structure
- Carrier Strike Group (typically USS Ronald Reagan or rotating)
- Destroyer Squadron 15 (Arleigh Burke-class)
- Amphibious Ready Group
- Submarine Group 7
- Fleet Air

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- An Arleigh Burke-class destroyer has been announced for a "freedom of navigation operation" — ETA 48 hours.

## Relationships
- [[Republic of Korea Navy]] — primary ally; combined command structure in wartime
- [[People's Liberation Army Navy]] — strategic rival; routine close encounters in the South and East China Seas
- [[Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force]] — allied; based in Yokosuka
