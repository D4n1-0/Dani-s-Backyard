# Steel Sovereign-class

## Description

The *Steel Sovereign*-class is the premier pre-dreadnought battleship of the Valdran Navy — and, by extension, the most powerful capital ship permitted under the [[Kaelen Mandate]]. Twelve vessels of the class form the backbone of Valdran sea power. Each displaces approximately 16,000 tonnes, carries a mixed battery of four 12-inch (305mm) guns in twin turrets and twelve 6-inch (152mm) casemate guns, and is propelled by triple-expansion steam engines driving twin screws at a maximum speed of 18 knots. Coal-fired, armour-belted, and bristling with signal halyards, they are the apex of what the Mandate allows.

## Function

In Valdran doctrine, the twelve *Steel Sovereigns* are organised into three battle squadrons of four ships each, deployed to protect the [[Tethyan Ocean]] periphery, the [[Occidens|Occidentine]] approaches, and the [[Oriens|Oriental]] trade routes. They fight in line-of-battle, their mixed batteries thundering at ranges under 8 kilometres, with biplane seaplanes launched from catapult-equipped cruisers spotting the fall of shot.

## History

The class was laid down over a thirty-year period starting in Year 180 PC, replacing an earlier generation of pre-dreadnoughts that had reached the end of their service lives. Each vessel takes approximately five years to construct at the Valdris Prime Naval Yards. The lead ship, *Steel Sovereign*, was commissioned in Year 185 PC. The twelfth and newest, *Indomitable*, entered service in Year 305 PC.

Valdran naval architects have privately designed a "fast battleship" concept — an all-big-gun, turbine-driven vessel that would render every existing warship obsolete. The plans exist only as hand-drawn schematics in a sealed vault beneath the Census Tower. Building such a vessel would violate the Mandate's capital ship article. The Admiralty has never authorised construction. The plans remain — waiting.
