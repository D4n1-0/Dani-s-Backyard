# Wild Pines Group

## Overview

*A major Revacholian corporation with interests in shipping, logistics, and terminal operations. Ultraliberal in ideology, transnational in reach, indifferent to the human cost of its balance sheets. The dock terminal in Martinaise is one of its key assets. Hired Krenel's mercenaries during the strike, a decision that led directly to the Tribunal and the deaths of the Hardie Boys. Lobbied the Coalition to retake the terminal after the strike demands escalated.*

## Structure

- [[Joyce Messier]] — Senior negotiator, on-site executive
- [[Lukas Delacroix]] — Security engineer, Koenigstein graduate
- Krenel's Mercenaries — Hired security, withdrawn after the Tribunal

## Assets

- Martinaise Dock Terminal
- Corporate headquarters in Grand Couron
- Coalition political influence

## Relationships

- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — *Adversary. The strike was the latest chapter in a long war.*
- [[Coalition of Nations]] — *Client relationship. The Coalition freed the terminal at Wild Pines' request.*
- [[INSURCOM]] — *Enforcer. Paid to reclaim and hold the terminal.*
