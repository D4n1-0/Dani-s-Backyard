# Grisha Yeager

## Description
Father of Eren and Zeke Yeager. An Eldian doctor and Restorationist who infiltrated the Walls from Marley. Former inheritor of the Attack Titan and Founding Titan.

## Background
Born in the Liberio Internment Zone, Grisha joined the Eldian Restorationist movement. He inherited the Attack Titan from Eren Kruger and infiltrated Paradis Island, settling in Shiganshina. He later took the Founding Titan from the Reiss family and passed both Titans to his son Eren.
