# High Captain Ranga

## Overview
Commander of the Heretic Fleet, the naval arm of the [[Heretic Legion]]. A figure of dread across the Faithful naval command. Led Heretic forces at the Battle of the Bloodied Cliffs (1805 TC).

## Context
High Captain Ranga exists as a canonical figure in Trench Crusade lore, referenced in the vault's Heretic Legion faction entry and Timeline. Though not yet a narrative presence in the Genesis scenario, his existence shapes Faithful naval doctrine — every Faithful captain who sails the Celtic Sea knows that Ranga's fleet patrols the same waters on the other side of the world.
