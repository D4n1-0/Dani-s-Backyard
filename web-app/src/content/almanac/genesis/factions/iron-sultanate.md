# Iron Sultanate

## Overview
The third major power of the Trench Crusade world, alongside the Crown of England and the Principality of New Antioch. Little is known about the Sultanate by the modern Earth delegations at the time of first contact. A courier vessel despatched from the Faithful Crown's fleet anchorage on Day 5, bearing south-southeast toward "the Southern Reaches," is the first indication that the Sultanate exists and is watching events unfold.

## Context
The Iron Sultanate is referenced in the TC timeline as having been formed in 1109, a decade after the 1099 divergence. The Great Iron Wall, its legendary fortification, dates from the same period. Its relationship with the Crown of England and New Antioch — whether ally, rival, or neutral broker — is not yet known to modern Earth. Its name alone hints at a civilisation built on industry, fortification, and independence from the Catholic-Faithful structure of the northern powers.
