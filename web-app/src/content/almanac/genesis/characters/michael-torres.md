# Michael Torres

## Description
A US Navy supply officer assigned to NAVSUP Europe, embedded at Northwood as the logistics liaison between US Naval Forces Europe and the UK's Royal Fleet Auxiliary. Competent, weary, the kind of officer who makes the fleet run and receives no recognition for it.

## Background
Torres is the man who has to answer the question no one wants to ask: how long can the fleet stay on station? He tracks fuel states, ammunition inventories, UNREP schedules, and the gap between what the operational planners assume and what the supply chain can actually deliver. The portal crisis has made him the most important man in the room and the least listened-to.

## Relationships
- [[Daniel Brennan]] — the customer who needs Torres to work miracles
- Royal Fleet Auxiliary command — his UK counterparts, scrambling alongside him

## Current Status
At [[Northwood Headquarters]], tracking the logistics of a multinational fleet converging on the portal.
