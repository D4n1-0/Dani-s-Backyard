# USS Gravely (DDG-107)

## Overview
An Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer of the United States Navy, assigned as an escort for Carrier Strike Group Two under Captain [[Daniel Brennan]]. The Gravely was participating in a NATO anti-submarine warfare exercise alongside *[[USS Harry S. Truman (CVN-75)|USS Harry S. Truman]]* when the order came to break exercise and proceed east at best speed.

## Features
- Aegis Combat System with SPY-1D radar
- 96-cell Mk 41 Vertical Launch System
- Standard ASW suite with towed array sonar
- Gas turbine propulsion — limited endurance compared to the nuclear-powered Truman

## Current Events
Sprinted east with the Truman screen, arriving on station with marginal fuel states in the high twenties after a mid-transit UNREP coordinated by Captain [[Michael Torres]] from the USNS *Leroy Grumman*. Holding position in the anti-submarine screen around the portal cordon.
