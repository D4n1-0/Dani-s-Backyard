# Lukas Delacroix

## Description

*Twenty-five. Lean build, sharp features, dark hair kept short for the professional world he's trying to belong to. Wears coats that are slightly too thin — always underdressed for the cold, a habit borne from growing up without much and refusing to admit it. Speaks with the controlled precision of someone who learned to choose words carefully because the wrong ones could be used against him. Ecole d'Ingénieurs de Koenigstein graduate — technical brilliance, credentials that would open any door in Revachol if only they were all that mattered.*

## Background

*Born in Grand Couron to a lower-middle-class family. Scholarship student at Koenigstein. Joyce Messier recruited him as an intern during his second year — a guest lecturer with an eye for talent, and for leverage. The grooming began then: assignments, introductions, the slow architecture of dependency. By his final year the relationship had long passed professional boundaries. She has been shaping him for years, not months. After every session she takes a Polaroid — a ritual of documentation, a collection that grows session by session. He has never seen the full archive. He knows it exists. That is enough.*

## Relationships

- [[Joyce Messier]] — *Patron, employer, blackmailer, lover. The gravitational centre of his career.*
- [[Henrik Varga|Commander Varga]] — *Coalition officer. Dismissed him on sight.*
- [[Evrart Claire]] — *Union boss. A threat wrapped in a ham sandwich.*
- [[Tommy Le Homme]] — *Fisherman, former dockworker. A mirror Lukas is not ready to face.*

## Current Status

*Arrived in Martinaise with Joyce Messier during the Coalition reclamation of the dock terminal. Tasked with installing a comprehensive security apparatus to prevent future strikes — the technical side: cameras, automated doors, the physical architecture of surveillance. The human side — protocols, policies, guard hiring — remains with Joyce. Alone in a conquered building, holding schematics for a cage he is building but will not write the rules for.*
