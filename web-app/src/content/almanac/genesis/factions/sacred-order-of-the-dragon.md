# Sacred Order of the Dragon

## Overview
A knightly order serving the Faithful Crown, the Sacred Order of the Dragon halted the Heretic advance in 1573 after the fall of Byzantium. A million heretics were impaled in the hills of Wallachia by their hand.

## Structure
The Order includes chaplains (such as Chaplain-Lieutenant Morgan Pell) who serve aboard Faithful naval vessels, providing spiritual guidance, blessed weaponry, and orichalcum-wrought protection.

## Assets
- Orichalcum armour and weaponry
- Portable reliquaries
- Blessed shotguns and Maxim guns

## Relationships
- [[Crown of England]] — the Order serves the Crown
- [[Heretic Legion]] — sworn enemy
