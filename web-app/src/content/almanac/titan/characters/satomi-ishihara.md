# Satomi Ishihara

## Description
Forty-one years old. Career officer of the Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Fifteen years of diplomatic service, postings in Geneva, Brussels, and Singapore. PhD in international relations from the University of Tokyo — her doctoral thesis addressed first-contact protocols between nations of radically asymmetric capability. Speaks fluent Japanese, English, French, and functional Marleyan (learned from [[Elias Vogler]]'s language materials during a ten-day voyage). Short dark hair, calm expression, a slight apologetic quality to her speech that is deliberate — she has learned that a diplomat who sounds too perfect is suspected of being a spy.

## Background
Seconded to CATF three months after the Gate opened as part of the civilian diplomatic corps supporting the combined command. Selected for the Marley contact mission over more senior candidates because she is a civilian, a woman, and a scholar — a profile Marleyan military culture would read as non-hostile before any conscious calculation about threat assessment occurred. Her brief for the Odiha mission was simple: open a channel, demonstrate capability without threat, and leave before the empire could mobilise. She achieved all three.

## Relationships
- [[Elias Vogler]] — language tutor and cultural advisor; Vogler's recordings and written grammar formed the foundation of her Marleyan
- [[Konrad Verner]] — Marleyan counterpart at Odiha; mutual respect forged across three conversations
- [[Jürgen Haas]] — Pelikan sailor who drilled her on lower-deck Marleyan slang and engine-room vocabulary during the voyage

## Current Status
Returned to Grande Terre after successful first contact at Odiha. Communication channel with Marley established. Awaiting Quadripartite Committee debriefing and instructions for follow-up diplomatic engagement.
