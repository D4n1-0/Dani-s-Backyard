# Project HARNESS

## Summary
A covert CIA program to develop a brain-computer interface targeting the [[Ackerman Bloodline|Ackerman]] cerebellar superstructure. Three-tier software architecture — Low (surveillance + information suppression), Medium (reward/punishment compliance), Total (motor override). Authorised as a six-month feasibility study by [[Harris Vance|Director Harris Vance]] following the acquisition of [[Levi Ackerman|Levi]]'s biological data via ONI intelligence backchannel. The program already has a proof of concept: Subject Seven, a terminal ALS patient implanted with first-generation hardware at a development cost of $250 million.

## Objectives
- Establish whether the Ackerman Path-immunity protein blocks external neural signals or specifically the Founder's signal
- Culture Ackerman germline cells and measure response to calibrated electrical stimulation
- Determine whether Low (surveillance), Medium (incentive), or Total (override) modes are viable for Ackerman biology
- Assess the psychological feasibility of incentive-based compliance protocols for the enhanced-human profile

## Status
Phase 1: Feasibility study (cellular modelling only). Six months. No written records outside the room. No program code. Elizabeth Cross is not read in.
