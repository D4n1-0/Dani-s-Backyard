# The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr

## Overview

The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr is a wound on the face of [[Gaethon]] — a shallow sea of vitrified craters and radioactive islands where a proud peninsula once stood. Fifty years ago (Year 262 PC), Myrr attempted to build a submarine fleet in secret. The [[Silent Chorus]] identified the heat bloom. Thirty-seven missiles from the [[The Silent Spires|Silent Spires]] turned the kingdom into a bay. It is now a lawless frontier — and the front line of defiance against the [[Kaelen Mandate]].

## Features

- **The Crater Field:** A chain of overlapping impact craters, the largest over two kilometres wide. The glass at the crater floors still holds embedded heat three decades later — warm to the touch, faintly luminescent in total darkness.
- **The Survivor Isles:** A handful of islands that were once mountain peaks, now surrounded by vitrified shallows. Populated by scavengers, exiles, and fugitives fleeing [[The Census|census-takers]].
- **The Deep Fjords:** Submerged valleys where Myrr's submarine pens were hidden. Still structurally intact in places. The [[The Unbound|Unbound]] use these for concealed operations.
- **The Memorial Shoal:** A reef of fused debris — ships, buildings, human remains — calcified into a single glass formation. Pilgrims from the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame|Last Flame]] visit to witness the cost of defiance. The Unbound visit to remember the cost of failure.

## Inhabitants

No government. A shifting population of scavengers, mad prophets, Unbound cells, and those who have nowhere else to go. The Mandate's census-takers do not operate here — Myrr is the only place on Gaethon where no one counts the living.

## Current Events

An Unbound cell in the Deep Fjords claims to have made a breakthrough in decoding the [[The Kaelic Melody|Kaelic Melody]]. If true, Myrr — ground zero of the Mandate's wrath — could become the site of humanity's first attempt to speak back.
