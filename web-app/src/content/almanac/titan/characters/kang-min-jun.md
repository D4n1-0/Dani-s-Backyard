# Kang Min-jun

## Description
Senior Petty Officer and watch officer aboard [[KCG 506]]. Competent and steady. The first person to spot [[The Gate]] on radar — noticing an impossible return that looked like sea clutter but was too regular to be natural. His report to [[Park Do-hyun|Lieutenant Park]] sets the entire chapter in motion.

## Background
Career enlisted man in the [[Korea Coast Guard]]. Has served under [[Park Do-hyun|Lieutenant Park]] for two years. Trusted watch officer.

## Relationships
- [[Park Do-hyun]] — commanding officer; professional respect

## Current Status
On station at [[The Gate Threshold]].
