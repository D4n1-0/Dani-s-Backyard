# France

## Overview
The French Republic in the modern timeline — a secular republic with [[UNCLOS|laïcité]] as a constitutional principle, a founding NATO member, and a permanent member of the UN Security Council. France detected the Anomaly via its own satellite assets and asserts, through [[Étienne Morel|Foreign Minister Morel]], that no single nation holds jurisdiction over a phenomenon in international waters.

## Position on the Anomaly
France's response operates on two levels:

**Legal:** The Anomaly is approximately one hundred and fifty nautical miles from the nearest landmass — beyond any state's territorial waters under [[UNCLOS]]. The UK's Exclusive Economic Zone does not confer sovereignty over the water column itself. France insists on equal representation in any international framework and proposes a working group on jurisdiction before any command structure is established.

**Philosophical:** Morel's deeper objection is to the [[The Ark|ark theology]] itself. France's *laïcité* — the most absolute form of secularism in the modern world — is not negotiable. The concept that modern humanity is "cargo" in a divine vessel is, in Morel's words, "an assault on human dignity." France will not permit a foreign church's eschatology to become the basis for international relations. This positions France as the most doctrinally opposed to Faithful theological claims — even more than the UK's [[Cautious Secular Engagement|secular engagement]] policy.
