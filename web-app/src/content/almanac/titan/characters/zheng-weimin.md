# Zheng Weimin

## Description
Fifty-two years old. Deputy Director, Office of the Central Foreign Affairs Commission of the Chinese Communist Party. Political, not military — a Party apparatchik who rose through the International Liaison Department before moving to the CFAC. Broad-faced, deliberate, rarely speaks without first establishing consensus. His power lies not in his rank but in his access: he briefs the Politburo Standing Committee directly. Every word he speaks carries the implied weight of higher authority.

## Background
Graduate of Peking University and the Party School. Served in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs before being tapped for the International Liaison Department. Moved to the CFAC during the Xi-era consolidation of foreign policy under the Party apparatus. Has managed crises before — South China Sea standoffs, trade-war backchannels, the Taiwan strait tensions of the 2020s. This is different. This is not a crisis he can manage; this is a crisis that will manage him if he lets it.

## Current Status
Managing Chinese Gate policy from the CFAC compound in Beijing. Has received the confirmed [[Inverted Earth]] finding from Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]]. Tension with Vice Admiral [[Zhang Haifeng]] is escalating as the [[USS Milius (DDG-69)]] arrives on station — the American presence validates Zhang's argument that the framework is insufficient. Zheng continues the backchannel with [[Lee Jun-ho]], but the cracks in the handshake are widening.
