# Saito Hiroshi

## Overview
Captain, Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force. Commanding officer of the [[JS Suzutsuki (DD-117)]], the first CATF warship to cross [[The Gate]] into the AOT world. Briefed seventy-two hours before transit. Asked three questions about navigational hazards, boarded his ship without further comment, and steamed through a hole in the universe as though it were a routine strait transit.
