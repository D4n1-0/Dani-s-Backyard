# Daniel Brennan

## Description
Captain of *[[USS Harry S. Truman (CVN-75)|USS Harry S. Truman]]*, commanding officer of Carrier Strike Group Two's flagship. A career naval aviator — precise, contained, with the quiet intensity of a man who has spent decades training for contingencies that never arrived. Until now.

## Background
Brennan has commanded the Truman for eighteen months. He was midway through a NATO exercise in the Atlantic when the order came from Fleet Forces Command: break from exercise, proceed east at best speed, coordinates for the portal. He does not yet know that the order will define the rest of his career.

## Relationships
- [[Robert Cole]] — his Commander-in-Chief
- [[Michael Torres]] — the logistics officer who has to make Brennan's transit possible
- [[Alistair Pembroke]] — opposite number on the other side, whom he has not yet met

## Current Status
En route to the portal cordon at best speed.
