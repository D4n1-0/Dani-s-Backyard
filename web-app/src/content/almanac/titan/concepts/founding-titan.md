# Founding Titan

## Summary
The supreme Titan — possessing godlike control over the biology and memories of all Subjects of Ymir, and the ability to command millions of Colossal Titans embedded within the three Walls of Paradis Island.

## Details
Currently held by [[Eren Yeager]], though he cannot access its full power alone — he lacks royal blood. The Founder is bound by the Vow Renouncing War when wielded by a royal-blooded host. The loophole: physical contact between a non-royal Founder and a Titan of royal blood unlocks the Coordinate's full power. The Founder's capabilities include memory erasure/rewriting, mass anatomical modification of all Eldians, and — most critically — the ability to unleash the Rumbling by awakening the Wall Titans.

## Context
For the modern world, the Founding Titan is simultaneously a WMD of civilisation-ending scale, a biological superweapon embedded in a traumatised teenager, and the sole negotiating leverage of a besieged island nation. Every modern policy toward Paradis must account for the Rumbling.
