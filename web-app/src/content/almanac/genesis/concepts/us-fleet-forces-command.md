# US Fleet Forces Command (FFC)

## Summary
The United States Navy's fleet command responsible for manning, training, and equipping naval forces for assignment to combatant commanders. Based at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia, FFC is the operational headquarters that ordered CSG-2 to break from its exercise and proceed to the portal.

## Details
FFC exercises administrative and operational control over Atlantic-based naval forces. The order to the Truman was authenticated with a dual-level protocol — a two-man authentication that indicated flag-level generation and second-party verification — signalling that the portal crisis had reached the highest levels of the Navy chain of command.
