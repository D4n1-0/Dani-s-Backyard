# Mañana

## Description

*A dockworker — or was. The distinction no longer matters to him, if it ever did. Sits in the corner of the Whirling-in-Rags with an empty glass and an unopened fishing magazine, staring at the far wall with the serene blankness of a man who has made peace with the void. A human pause button. Time has no purchase on him.*

## Background

*Union member. The strike, the Tribunal, the reclamation — none of it appears to have disturbed his fundamental equilibrium. He has not said more than three words in a row since the shootings. He did not say more than three words before them either.*

## Relationships

- [[Garte]] — *Tolerates him. Or is tolerated by him. The dynamic is unclear.*
- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — *Member in good standing. Probably.*

## Current Status

*At his corner table. Unchanged. Unchangeable.*
