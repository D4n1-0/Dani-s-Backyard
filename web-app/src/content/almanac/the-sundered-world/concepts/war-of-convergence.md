# War of Convergence

## Summary

The War of Convergence was the apocalyptic conflict three hundred years ago in which [[Kaelen]] defeated the combined armed forces of the continental powers of [[Gaethon]] using technologies now forbidden — radar-directed guns, monoplane bomber fleets, rocketry, and the rumoured flash of atomic fission — followed by a systematic scouring of the world's scientific and industrial base.

## Details

### The Conflict

The war was brief by the standards of Gaethon's history — fought and won within a single campaign season. Kaelen's technological edge was so overwhelming that no conventional defence was possible. Radar gave Kaelen's forces total battlefield awareness; monoplane bombers struck with impunity beyond the ceiling of any defending biplane; rockets bypassed front lines entirely.

The rumour of atomic fission remains unconfirmed but persistent. Crater-glass formations at several pre-Convergence battlefields exhibit isotopic ratios inconsistent with conventional explosives — though no living scientist on Gaethon has the theoretical framework to verify this.

### The Scouring

After victory, Kaelen's forces did not occupy. They systematically stripped the world:

- Libraries and universities were emptied of scientific treatises, mathematical texts, and engineering blueprints.
- Museums were looted of industrial-age artifacts.
- Factories and foundries were dismantled or destroyed.
- Forests were put to the torch to deny timber for reconstruction.
- Farmlands were salted to induce famine and collapse.

The intent was not conquest but *reset* — reducing Gaethon to a pre-industrial baseline from which no rival could re-emerge.

### Aftermath

Kaelen withdrew behind its sea-wall and broadcast the [[Kaelen Mandate]]. The surviving population collapsed by an estimated 40–60% in the famine years that followed. The nations that exist today are the descendants of survivor states that coalesced around remaining arable land.

## Context

The War of Convergence is the foundational trauma of the Sundered World — taught to every child, commemorated in every religion. Its precise details are lost, having occurred before the Mandate's historical recording caps, but its shadow defines all politics. No living person has seen a photograph of pre-Convergence Kaelen or met someone who fought in its armies.
