# Kim Kitsuragi

## Description

*RCM Lieutenant, Precinct 57. Stoic, precise, morally anchored in a way that makes him an anomaly within the RCM. Wears a bomber jacket and wire-rimmed glasses — "the binoclard," as Garte called him. Partnered with Harry Du Bois during the Martinaise investigation. The competent one. The one who remembers.*

## Background

*Assigned as Harry's partner during the Martinaise murder case. Maintained professional composure through chaos, amnesia, the Tribunal, and everything else the week threw at them. Returned to Jamrock with Harry after the case closed. His report is on file. His opinion of Wild Pines is not recorded and probably damning.*
