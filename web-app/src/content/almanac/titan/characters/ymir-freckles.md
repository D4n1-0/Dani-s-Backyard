# Ymir (Freckles)

## Description
Freckled, sharp-tongued, selfless beneath the sarcasm. Holder of the Jaw Titan. Voluntarily surrendered herself to Reiner and Bertolt to save Historia and the Survey Corps. Consumed by Porco Galliard in Marley.

## Background
An orphan in Marley's internment zone who was adopted as a cult figure for a fringe Eldian sect. Turned into a Pure Titan as punishment. Wandered for 60 years outside Wall Maria. Regained humanity by consuming Marcel Galliard, one of the original Warriors sent to Paradis. Her second chance at life was lived entirely on her own terms — culminating in her decision to give the Jaw Titan back to Marley in exchange for Historia's safety.

## Relationships
- [[Historia Reiss]] — the person Ymir loved most; her final letter urged Historia to live with pride
- [[Reiner Braun]] — complicated respect

## Current Status
Dead. Consumed. Her Jaw Titan now belongs to Porco Galliard. Historia carries her memory.
