# Richter

## Overview
General of the Marleyan Titan Security Division. Member of the military faction within the General Staff. Oversees the empire's Titan programme — the Warrior Unit, the deployment of Shifters, the management of Eldian internment zones. His interest in the CATF contact is defensive: the technology transfer agreement bypasses Titan Security Division authority, and he has been pushing for a seat at the negotiation table ever since the MOU was signed. Sent [[Anton Schreiber|Colonel Schreiber]] as the military faction's observer at the [[South Bank (Odiha)]] compound.

## Background
Career Titan Security Division officer. Built his career on the doctrine that Eldians are weapons to be deployed, not people to be negotiated with. The CATF contact threatens everything he has built: if Marley can buy conventional weapons, it no longer depends on his Shifters. The Paradis annex — CATF's independent engagement with Eldia — is his worst nightmare made real.

## Relationships
- [[Anton Schreiber|Colonel Schreiber]] — his deputy; sent to Odiha as observer; loyal to Richter
- Admiral Strobl — military faction ally; Home Fleet command
- [[Hoffmann]] — General Staff chairman; Richter lobbies him constantly for Titan Security Division inclusion in the MOU
