# King Robert's Letter

## Description
A formal letter from [[King Robert the Longsword]], sovereign of the [[Crown of England]], addressed to "the Honourable Stephen Davenport, Prime Minister of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland." Written in a clear, elegant hand on heavy vellum, sealed with the Crown's own wax — the cross of the Faithful Church and the lion of the [[Crown of England]] pressed into crimson. Carried from Trench Crusade London to the modern world by [[Rebecca Osei|Lieutenant Commander Osei]], who had spent a week in the Faithful Crown's capital.

## Content
The letter is at once a diplomatic communiqué, a theological treatise, and a plea. Its central claim: the modern world is "an instrument of Providence, preserved beyond the reach of the Adversary, delivered at the hour of the Faithful's greatest need." [[King Robert the Longsword|King Robert]] does not threaten, does not demand, does not condition his offer on theological agreement. He offers alliance — military, diplomatic, economic — and frames it as Providence made manifest. The letter is politically astute (claiming the modern world before the [[Principality of New Antioch|New Antioch]] Patriarch or the Synod can frame it differently) and theologically devastating (a secular PM cannot accept the framing without endorsing the theology, and cannot refuse the offer without appearing to reject the Crown's hand in good faith).

## Significance
The letter forces [[Stephen Davenport|Davenport]] to confront the [[Cautious Secular Engagement]] doctrine's central contradiction: the Faithful Crown will never accept neutral engagement as a valid framework. A king who believes the modern world is the answer to eight centuries of prayer cannot be treated as merely another foreign power. [[Stephen Davenport|Davenport]]'s reply — whether he writes it, delays it, or delegates it — will define the diplomatic framework going forward.

## History
Written in the Palace of Whitehall, Trench Crusade London, after [[King Robert the Longsword|King Robert]] had observed the modern world for over a week through the reports of his priests and the testimony of [[Rebecca Osei|Osei]] herself. Carried through the portal by [[Rebecca Osei|Osei]] on her return to the modern world, and delivered directly to [[Stephen Davenport|Davenport]] aboard the *[[USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)|USS Gerald R. Ford]]*.
