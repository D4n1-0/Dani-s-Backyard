# Beijing

## Overview
Capital of the People's Republic of China. Population ~22 million. Political and administrative centre of China, housing the [[Central Foreign Affairs Commission (PRC)]], the State Council, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Central Military Commission, and all top-level organs of the CCP and government. During the Gate crisis, the military-civilian incident group operates from the CFAC compound in Xicheng District.

## Features
- Zhongnanhai (leadership compound, seat of the CCP Central Committee)
- Xicheng District (CFAC compound, MFA building)
- Ministry of National Defense (Babao Hill)
- Beijing Capital International Airport

## Current Events
[[Zheng Weimin]] chairs the CFAC incident group at dawn, 06:45 CST, as reports from the Yellow Sea arrive.
