# Security Apparatus (Wild Pines)

## Summary

*The comprehensive surveillance and access-control system designed by Lukas Delacroix for the Martinaise Dock Terminal. Cameras, automated door locks, and a centralised control room — designed to prevent another strike by making occupation permanent without looking like occupation.*

## Details

*Designed at the Ecole d'Ingénieurs de Koenigstein by Lukas Delacroix, under the supervision and patronage of Joyce Messier. The system integrates:*

- *Surveillance cameras — full perimeter and interior coverage*
- *Automated door controls — any section sealable in under eight seconds*
- *Centralised control room — old supervisor's office, repurposed*

*The system is elegant in design and sinister in implication. It transforms the terminal from a workplace into a panopticon — every corridor watched, every door controlled from a single room. The workers will know they are being watched. That is the point.*

## Context

*Lukas designed this system as his professional breakthrough. It represents everything he was trained to do and everything he is now being forced to confront. The apparatus is both his achievement and his mirror — a cage he is building for others that reflects the cage Joyce has built around him.*
