# Cosmology

In the Trench Crusade timeline, Hell is real and actively wages war on Earth. The Gate of Hell was opened in Jerusalem in 1099 by the Knights Templar, creating a permanent rift through which demonic forces pour into the world.

Heaven is believed to empower the Faithful through saints, miracles, and divine blessings, but does not intervene directly. The war is fought by humanity — some serving God, others serving Hell.

Orichalcum, a blessed metal discovered in 1503, is one of the few materials effective against infernal weaponry. Goetic Magic, the sorcery of Hell, corrupts reality itself.

The Church Militant governs the Faithful, maintaining a permanent state of holy war.
