# Portable Reliquary

## Description
A gilded reliquary hung around the neck, carried by Chaplain-Lieutenant Morgan Pell during the first contact mission. Contains blessed relics for protection against heretical forces.

## Function
Provides spiritual protection and blessing. Used by Faithful chaplains when venturing into uncertain or potentially hostile territory.

## History
Standard equipment for chaplains of the Sacred Order of the Dragon on dangerous missions.
