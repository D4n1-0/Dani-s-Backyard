# State Oceanic Administration (PRC)

## Overview
China's primary oceanographic research and maritime resource management agency. Responsible for marine scientific research, environmental monitoring, seabed surveying, and polar expeditions. Operates a fleet of research vessels and maintains ocean observation networks across the Western Pacific. The SOA's data on the Gate will be critical to any international scientific effort.

## Structure
- Oceanographic Survey Bureau (led by Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]])
- Marine Environmental Monitoring Centre
- Polar Research Institute
- Research fleet: MV Xue Long, MV Xiang Yang Hong series

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]] is the SOA representative in the CFAC incident group.

## Relationships
- [[Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology]] — research counterpart; will soon co-chair a joint scientific committee
- [[Central Foreign Affairs Commission (PRC)]] — providing scientific counsel

## Current Status
Preparing to contribute to international scientific study of the Gate. Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]] is advocating for China to co-lead the scientific response alongside KIOST, framing it as a matter of both national prestige and genuine scientific necessity.
