# Disco Cosmology

## The World

*Elysium is a planet of continents separated not by oceans but by the Pale — a grey, separative tissue that dissolves matter, memory, and meaning. The inhabitable landmasses are called Isolas. Travel between Isolas requires crossing the Pale, which is dangerous, disorienting, and closely regulated by the Moralintern.*

## The Pale

*The Pale is not empty space; it is anti-reality. It unmakes. Prolonged exposure erodes memory, identity, and eventually physical form. It is expanding — slowly, inexorably. Some believe it is caused by human consciousness. Most try not to think about it.*

## Innocences

*Innocences are world-historical individuals who appear at moments of crisis and reshape civilisation. Dolores Dei was the most recent and most celebrated — the Dolorian Century bears her name. Innocences are somewhere between saints, dictators, and natural disasters. Their existence is a matter of faith as much as history.*

## The Moralintern

*The Moralist International is the dominant political ideology of the Reál Belt — centrist, technocratic, imperial under a veneer of reason. It governs through institutions like the Coalition of Nations. Its project is order. The cost of that order is the question haunting every Isola.*
