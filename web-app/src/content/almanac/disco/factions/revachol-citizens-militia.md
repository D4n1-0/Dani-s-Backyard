# Revachol Citizens Militia

## Overview

*The volunteer police force of Revachol. A citizen militia in name, a compromised institution in practice — underfunded, understaffed, and overseen by the ICP as part of the Coalition's occupation framework. The RCM investigates crimes. The Coalition decides which investigations matter. The gap between those two functions is where men like Harry Du Bois disappear into.*

## Structure

- Precinct 57 (Jamrock) — Home of Detectives Du Bois and Kitsuragi
- Precinct 41 — The setting of the original case
- ICP oversight — Coalition supervision of all RCM operations

## Assets

- Jurisdiction over criminal investigations in Revachol
- Detective corps — small, under-resourced, occasionally brilliant

## Relationships

- [[Coalition of Nations]] — *Overseen by. The RCM is a local instrument of occupation.*
- [[Dockworkers' Union]] — *Jurisdictional rivals. The Union runs Martinaise; the RCM visits.*
- [[Wild Pines Group]] — *Corporate partner. The mercenary murder was an RCM case.*
