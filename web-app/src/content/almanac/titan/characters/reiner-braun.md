# Reiner Braun

## Description
Seventeen years old. Tall, heavily muscled, blond. Vice-Captain of the Warrior Unit and holder of the Armored Titan. Suffers from severe PTSD and dissociative identity fragmentation caused by his years of undercover infiltration on Paradis — the "soldier" Reiner who genuinely loved his comrades versus the "warrior" Reiner who broke the wall and killed 250,000 people. Has attempted suicide.

## Background
Born in the Liberio Internment Zone to a Marleyan father and an Eldian mother. Became a Warrior candidate to earn "Honorary Marleyan" status for his family — to prove Eldians could be "good." His infiltration of Paradis shattered him: he formed genuine bonds with Eren, Armin, and the 104th, then betrayed them all. At Shiganshina, Eren told him "I'll make you die in the most painful way possible." Reiner barely survived, escaping with Zeke.

## Current Status
In Marley, barely functional. Preparing the next generation of Warrior candidates. One bad day from complete collapse — or redemptive action.
