# Edmund Lassiter

## Description
Doctor-Abbot of the [[Holy Science Academy]], a hunched man of seventy whose hands are stained with orichalcum filings and chemical reagents. He has spent his career reverse-engineering captured Heretic machinery — his specialty is finding the devil in the gears. Carries a leather satchel of sanctified instruments: aetheric resonance gauges, blessed galvanometers, a portable spectroscope approved by the Patriarch of New Antioch.

## Background
[[Edmund Lassiter|Lassiter]] leads the Academy's mobile research contingent. He is the foremost authority on the physical properties of infernal materials and their interaction with the divine. Summoned to [[Caius Blackwood|Blackwood]]'s war council to lead the scientific investigation of [[The Anomaly]].

## Relationships
- [[Caius Blackwood]] — his commanding officer for this investigation
- [[Father Beauchamp]] — theological counterpart in the investigation

## Current Status
En route to [[The Portal Threshold]] aboard HMS *Herald* with the first Faithful scientific delegation.
