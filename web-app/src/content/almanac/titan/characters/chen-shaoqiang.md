# Chen Shaoqiang

## Description
Forty-three years old. *Shangxiao* (Senior Captain) in the [[China Coast Guard]], People's Armed Police. Nearly two decades of Yellow Sea patrols. Stocky, broad-shouldered, thinning hair, perpetual weariness etched into his face. Speaks passable Maritime English. Commands [[Haijing 2306]], a Shucha II-class cutter (1,500 tons, 42 crew) operating from the North Sea Bureau out of Qingdao.

## Background
Career PAP officer who transferred to the CCG during the 2013 reorganisation. Has spent years in the Yellow Sea's grey-zone theatre — shadowing South Korean patrols, intercepting fishing vessels, asserting Beijing's territorial claims without ever pulling a trigger. He is a man who understands brinkmanship as a language. The Gate gives him nothing to translate.

## Relationships
- [[Park Do-hyun]] — South Korean counterpart; the only other human who understands what they are both looking at

## Current Status
On station at [[The Gate Threshold]] for seven days. Designated co-enforcer of the exclusion zone alongside [[Park Do-hyun]]. Primary operator of the first Chinese UAV and ROV deployments through the Gate. Has not slept a full night since the Gate opened. His English has improved through necessity; his partnership with Park has become something his government did not plan for.
