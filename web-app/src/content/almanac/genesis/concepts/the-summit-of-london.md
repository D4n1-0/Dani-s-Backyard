# The Summit of London

## Summary
A planned trilateral summit in modern London between the Earth coalition, the [[Crown of England]], and the [[Iron Sultanate]] — the first time the Crown and Sultanate would meet in peace, and the first time either would formally enter the modern world. Proposed by the Earth coalition as part of the [[The Ark Strategy|Ark Strategy]] and issued by [[King Robert the Longsword|King Robert]] as a divine summons, the summit represents the manipulation's operational centrepiece.

## Origin
During the post-collision council, [[Marcus Webb]] proposed that a grand conference — framed in theological terms the Faithful could not refuse — would accomplish all three objectives of the [[The Ark Strategy|Ark Strategy]] simultaneously: stabilising the TC world by reinforcing the Providence narrative, securing Earth's interests by establishing formal diplomatic channels, and neutralising the [[Iron Sultanate]]'s uncertainty by bringing it inside the tent rather than leaving it as a wildcard observer.

## Participants
- **Modern Earth** — represented by [[Stephen Davenport|Prime Minister Davenport]] and the international coalition
- **[[Crown of England]]** — [[King Robert the Longsword|King Robert]] and his counsellors ([[Father Beauchamp|Beauchamp]], [[Alistair Pembroke|Pembroke]], [[Bishop Aldric|Aldric]], [[Inquisitor Paulus|Paulus]], and the military command)
- **[[Iron Sultanate]]** — invited by courier vessel; its response and delegation composition are yet to be determined

## Theological Framing
To the Faithful Crown, the summit is the third act of a divine sequence: the portal opened, the moderns arrived, the collision revealed their power, and now the King summons the Faithful to a great council in the moderns' own city. [[King Robert the Longsword|King Robert]] reads the invitation as Providence's next command — a call he was born to answer.

To the Earth coalition, the summit is a practical mechanism dressed in theological clothing. The framing is a fiction the coalition does not believe but will not correct.

## Unresolved Tensions
- The [[Iron Sultanate]] has not yet responded. Its relationship with the Crown — whether ally, rival, or neutral broker — is unknown to modern Earth.
- [[France]], through [[Étienne Morel]] and [[Étienne Roussel]], objects to the summit's theological framing and the broader [[The Ark Strategy|Ark Strategy]] it serves.
- The [[Heretic Legion]] has been listening. The [[The Heretic Submarine|submarine]] destroyed by *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|USS Stout]]* was relaying all transmissions. Whatever the Legion knows, it has not yet acted upon.

## Future Chapters
The summit itself will be the subject of a subsequent chapter. Its outcome — whether it succeeds as a diplomatic breakthrough or fractures under the weight of its own manipulation — is the central dramatic question of the Genesis scenario's next act.
