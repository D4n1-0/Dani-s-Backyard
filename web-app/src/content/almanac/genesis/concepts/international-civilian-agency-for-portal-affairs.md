# International Civilian Agency for Portal Affairs

## Summary
A proposed non-military international body — first floated by [[Anna Strauss|Chancellor Strauss]] — to manage contact, research, and protocol across the portal independently of any single nation's command structure.

## Details
Strauss proposes the Agency as an alternative to the military-led frameworks advocated by the US and UK. It would be:

- **Civilian-led**: staffed by scientists, diplomats, legal experts, engineers — not military commanders
- **Multinational**: representation from every nation that wants a seat, with no single power holding veto authority
- **Transparency-obligated**: open scientific research, shared data, no classification based on national interest
- **Mandate-limited**: focused on understanding the portal and establishing contact protocols, not on military strategy or threat response

## Context
The proposal is received coolly by the US (which sees it as a delaying tactic), with cautious interest by France (which sees the legal framework as a useful complement), and with resignation by the UK (which recognises that Germany cannot offer military support and is offering the best alternative it has). The Agency never materialises in the form Strauss envisions, but the idea — that civilian expertise should lead, not follow, military command — surfaces repeatedly in the debates that follow.
