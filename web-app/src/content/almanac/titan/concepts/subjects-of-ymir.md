# Subjects of Ymir

## Summary
The ethnic bloodline descending from Ymir Fritz, connected through the Paths and capable of being transformed into Titans via spinal fluid injection.

## Details
All Subjects of Ymir are linked through the Paths to the Founding Titan. Their biology is latent with the potential for Titan transformation — injection of Titan spinal fluid triggers the change. The Founder can also manipulate their memories and anatomy across great distances. This dual nature — human and potential weapon — makes every Eldian a geopolitical liability in the AOT world, where Marley confines them in internment zones and Paradis's population are considered "devils" by the global consensus.

## Context
For the modern world, the existence of a race of people who can literally be turned into man-eating giants by a biological trigger is a military, ethical, and legal crisis without precedent. Refugee status, human rights, biological weapons conventions — all must be re-examined.
