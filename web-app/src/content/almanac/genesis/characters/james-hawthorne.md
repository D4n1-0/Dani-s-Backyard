# James Hawthorne

## Description
Captain of *[[HMS Dragon|HMS Dragon]]* (D35), a Type 45 destroyer. A modern naval officer trained in crisis management, pragmatic and measured. Leads the initial naval standoff against the Faithful dreadnought.

## Background
Commanding officer of the nearest major surface asset when [[The Anomaly]] was reported. He exchanges the first signals with [[Alistair Pembroke|Captain Pembroke]] aboard *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*, navigating the theological language of the Faithful with care. He authorises the first contact boarding party and sends "God save the King" as a trust-building signal.

## Relationships
- [[Sarah Vance]] — his operational commander
- [[Alistair Pembroke]] — opposite number across the anomaly
- [[Rebecca Osei]] — dispatched her as the first contact officer
- [[Daniel Foster]] — his ship's chaplain, first modern theologian to engage Beauchamp

## Current Status
On station aboard *[[HMS Dragon|HMS Dragon]]* at [[The Portal Threshold]]. Authorised the first larger Faithful delegation — twelve unarmed scientists and priests — to cross into modern Earth. Coordinating the growing multi-ship presence around the anomaly with [[Sarah Vance|Commander Vance]] at [[Northwood Headquarters|Northwood]].
