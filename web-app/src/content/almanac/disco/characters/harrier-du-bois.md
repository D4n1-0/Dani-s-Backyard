# Harrier Du Bois

## Description
Lieutenant Double-Yefreitor of the [[Revachol Citizens Militia|RCM]], Precinct 41, C-Wing. Born '07 in the Commune of Revachol, on the ground floor of the Old Military Hospital. A legendary detective and a legendary disaster. Alcoholism, amnesia, and a spectacular capacity for both self-destruction and genuine insight.

## Background
Joined the RCM in '33. Rose to become one of the force's most effective investigators — the "human can-opener" who could crack any suspect. His breakup with [[Dora Ingerlund]] around '45 shattered him. He descended into a years-long bender that culminated in March '51, when he arrived in [[Martinaise]] with no memory of his own name and a week to solve a hanging.

He solved it. With [[Kim Kitsuragi]]. And photographed the [[Insulindian Phasmid]].

## Relationships
- [[Kim Kitsuragi]] — partner; the man who stayed
- [[Jean Vicquemare]] — long-suffering partner
- [[Judit Minot]] — colleague; sees the good in him
- [[Dora Ingerlund]] — ex-something; the wound that won't close

## Current Status
Post-case, recovering. Back at Precinct 41. Haunted. Possibly rebuilding. The Pale gave him a fragment of the future — Revachol destroyed in '73.
