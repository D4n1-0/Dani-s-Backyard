# Trost

The primary military and administrative hub of the [[Survey Corps]] within Wall Rose. Site of the Battle of Trost (Year 850). Houses the main Survey Corps barracks, training yard, headquarters building, and the first permanent CATF liaison office on Paradis — a converted shipping container with a blue four-star flag. The Grey Gull restaurant operates near the market square. Trost has become the de facto centre of CATF–Paradis cooperation.
