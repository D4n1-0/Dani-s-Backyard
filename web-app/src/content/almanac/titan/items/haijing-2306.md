# Haijing 2306

## Description
Shucha II-class cutter, approximately 1,500 tons displacement, crew of 42. Armed with a twin 37mm forward mount and secondary 14.5mm machine guns. Equipped with modern maritime radar, satellite communications, and a helicopter deck (no embarked aircraft). Home port: Qingdao, North Sea Bureau.

## Function
Maritime sovereignty patrol, fisheries enforcement, grey-zone operations in the Yellow Sea. Larger and better-armed than the [[KCG 506]], designed to project presence and withstand lower-intensity confrontations.

## History
Assigned to [[Chen Shaoqiang|Senior Captain Chen Shaoqiang]]'s command. Responded to [[The Gate]] after detecting the anomaly on sensors while conducting routine patrol. Second vessel on scene. Currently holding station at [[The Gate Threshold]] in standoff with [[KCG 506]].
