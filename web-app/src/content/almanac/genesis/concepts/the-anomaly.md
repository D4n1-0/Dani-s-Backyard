# The Anomaly

## Summary
An interdimensional portal connecting modern Earth to the Trench Crusade timeline, appearing in the North Atlantic as a shimmering silver curtain.

## Details
The Anomaly is a vertical plane of silvered light approximately one kilometre wide and two hundred metres tall, centred at sea level. It is transparent enough to see through but distorts radar, thermal imaging, and light. The sea passes through it without reflection.

## Context
First observed by RAF Flight Lieutenant [[Alice Hartley]] on a routine maritime patrol. Both modern Earth and Trench Crusade forces approach it with extreme caution.
