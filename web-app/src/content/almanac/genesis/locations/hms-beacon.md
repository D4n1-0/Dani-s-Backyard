# HMS Beacon

## Overview
[[King Robert the Longsword|King Robert]]'s personal vessel — a fast yacht adapted for wartime service, stationed at the portal cordon as the King's mobile command post. Smaller than a dreadnought, faster than a cruiser, the *[[HMS Beacon|Beacon]]* carries the royal standard and serves as the Crown's floating seat at the threshold between worlds. It was from the *[[HMS Beacon|Beacon]]* that the King crossed to *[[HMS Herald|HMS Herald]]* for the summit council.

## Significance
The *[[HMS Beacon|Beacon]]* is not a warship — it is a symbol. Its presence at the portal cordon signals that the Crown's attention is fixed on the modern world, and that the King himself is willing to remain at the threshold rather than retreat to the safety of London. It will carry the King across the portal when the summit convenes.
