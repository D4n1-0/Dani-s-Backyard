# Joyce's Apartment (Grand Couron)

## Overview

*The top floor of a converted Suzerainty townhouse in the old city of Grand Couron. Dark wood floors, tall narrow windows with old glass, streetlamp light filtering in amber through the panes. Expensive, sparse — a leather chair, a low table, a bookshelf of memoranda. A stage, not a home. The bathroom at the end of the hall: white tile, clawfoot tub, pedestal sink, a wall-wide mirror. This is where Joyce holds her non-boat sessions when both she and Lukas are in Grand Couron. The bathroom floor has borne witness to most of them.*

## Features

- Living room — leather chair, low table, the architecture of a woman who does not live in her own apartment so much as occupy it
- Bathroom — white tile, clawfoot tub, pedestal sink, wall mirror, narrow window overlooking the Moralintern spire
- Bedroom — through a door off the hall, not often visited by the person Lukas is when he enters this apartment

## Inhabitants

- [[Joyce Messier]] — Resident, owner, stage-manager

## Current Events

*Used for punishment sessions, private conversations, and the occasional dinner when the boat is not available. Joyce's domain on land.*
