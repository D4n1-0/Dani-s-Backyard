# Precinct 41

## Overview
The [[Revachol Citizens Militia|RCM]] precinct that serves the Jamrock district of Revachol West. Home to C-Wing and the Major Crimes Unit. A broken, bureaucratic, underfunded, perpetually undermanned police station staffed by alcoholics, eccentrics, and the occasional genuine professional.

## Features
- C-Wing — Major Crimes
- Radio dispatch — [[Jules Pidieu]]
- Holding cells
- Offices with "copotypes" on the walls
- Décomptage duty rotation board
