# Kaelen Ballistic Missile

## Description

The Kaelen ballistic missile is the enforcement mechanism of the [[Kaelen Mandate]] — a conventionally armed, precision-guided weapon launched from the [[The Silent Spires|Silent Spires]] that can reach any point on [[Gaethon]] within an hour. Its exact specifications are unknown — no intact missile has ever been recovered, and the physics required to analyse debris are forbidden — but centuries of observation have yielded educated estimates.

The missile is believed to be a two-stage solid-fuel design, approximately 12–15 metres in length, with a payload in the 500–1,000 kilogram range. Warhead type varies: observed strikes have used unitary high-explosive (the Keth incident), submunitions (the Myrr strikes), and an unknown warhead type that produces vitrification effects inconsistent with conventional explosives — possibly thermobaric, possibly something beyond Scattered Realms physics entirely.

## Function

Missiles are launched from one or more of the eight Silent Spires, following ballistic trajectories with a mid-course correction capability (inferred from the precision of strikes against moving naval targets during the [[War of Convergence]]). Terminal guidance is assumed to be provided by [[Silent Chorus]] drones or [[Eyes of Heaven|satellite]] downlink — the Myrr strikes achieved target accuracy within 50 metres against submerged submarine pens, a feat impossible with pure inertial guidance.

## History

Five confirmed strike events in 312 years:

1. **Year 17 PC** — Single launch, [[Oriens]] industrial site.
2. **Year 89 PC** — Twin launch, experimental monoplane airship facility.
3. **Year 201 PC** — Single launch, disputed target.
4. **Year 262 PC** — Thirty-seven launches, [[The Sunken Kingdom of Myrr|Myrr]]. Peninsula reduced to vitrified bay.
5. **Year 287 PC** — Single launch, [[Keth]]. 15-kilometre glass crater.

No launch in 25 years — the longest quiet period since the War. The missiles remain in their silos. The Spires stand ready. The silence is not reassurance.
