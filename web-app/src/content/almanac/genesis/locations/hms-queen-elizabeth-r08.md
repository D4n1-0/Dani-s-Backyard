# HMS Queen Elizabeth (R08)

## Overview
The lead ship of the Queen Elizabeth-class aircraft carriers, the largest warship ever built for the Royal Navy. Activated from Portsmouth as the centrepiece of the UK's response to the portal crisis, she sails with her destroyer screen as a full [[UK Carrier Strike Group]].

## Features
- STOVL configuration with F-35B Lightning II
- Twin-island design
- 65,000-tonne displacement
- Embarked air group of F-35Bs and Merlin helicopters

## Inhabitants
- Ship's company of the Royal Navy
- Embarked air wing

## Current Events
Sailing to the portal cordon from Portsmouth to join the multinational fleet.
