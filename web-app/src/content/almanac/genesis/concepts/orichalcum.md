# Orichalcum

## Summary
A blessed metal discovered in 1503, effective against infernal weaponry and used in high-value Faithful equipment.

## Details
Discovered by War Prophet Angelos, guided by St. Elegius. Orichalcum steel is extremely costly to produce but effective against all missile weapons. It remains in use into the 20th century for armour, reliquaries, and blessed equipment.

## Context
[[Father Beauchamp]] carries a processional crucifix of Orichalcum steel aboard *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]*, a mark of his rank in the [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]].
