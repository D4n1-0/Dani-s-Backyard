# AOT Side — Week 1 Post-Shiganshina

While Earth's survey drones map the empty southern ocean through [[The Gate]], the inhabitants of the Attack on Titan world are processing their own catastrophe, thousands of kilometres to the north, unaware the Gate exists.

## Events (Approximately Year 850, Days 1–9 Post-Battle)

- **Survey Corps** — The survivors of the Battle of Shiganshina reach Grisha Yeager's basement. The truth of the world is revealed: humanity survived beyond the Walls. Eldians are a despised race confined to internment zones. Marley controls seven of the Nine Titans and intends to conquer [[Paradis Island]]. The revelation shatters the Survey Corps' worldview — the enemy is not mindless Titans but the entire world.
- **Command transition** — [[Erwin Smith]] is dead. [[Hange Zoë]] assumes command of the Survey Corps. [[Armin Arlert]], having inherited the Colossal Titan from [[Bertolt Hoover]], begins his shortened 13-year lifespan. [[Levi Ackerman]] remains the Corps' deadliest soldier but is grappling with having chosen Armin over Erwin.
- **Marley** — The empire absorbs the catastrophic loss of the Beast Titan ([[Zeke Yeager]]), the Colossal Titan (Armin), the Armored Titan ([[Reiner Braun]], defeated), and the Female Titan ([[Annie Leonhart]], captured). The Warrior Unit is in disarray. Reiner returns to Marley bearing the psychological weight of his failed mission and the knowledge that Paradis now holds the Founding Titan and the Coordinate.
- **Mid-East** — The Mid-East Allied Forces have not yet declared war. The Mid-East Marleyan War (Year 850–854) has not begun — but Marley's vulnerability is already apparent to its rivals.

The Gate opens in the AOT world's southern ocean, remote and unwitnessed. No ship passes near it. No inhabitant of the AOT world is aware of its existence.
