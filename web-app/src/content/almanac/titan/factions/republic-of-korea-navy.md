# Republic of Korea Navy

## Overview
Naval warfare branch of the Republic of Korea Armed Forces. Operates a blue-water fleet of destroyers, frigates, submarines, and patrol vessels focused primarily on deterrence against North Korea and maritime sovereignty in the Yellow Sea and East China Sea. Close operational cooperation with the United States Seventh Fleet.

## Structure
- 3 Fleet Commands
- Destroyer flotillas (Sejong the Great-class, Gwanggaeto the Great-class)
- Submarine Command (Type 214, Dosan Ahn Changho-class)
- Naval Aviation
- ROK Marine Corps

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- A destroyer has been dispatched and is en route to the Gate site.

## Relationships
- [[United States Seventh Fleet]] — primary ally; joint exercises and combined command in wartime
- [[People's Liberation Army Navy]] — rival; overlapping claims in the Yellow Sea
- [[Korea Coast Guard]] — separate chain of command; the KCG are first responders, the Navy are follow-on force

## Current Status
Mobilising a destroyer for immediate dispatch. Admiral [[Yoon Seok-jin]] is urging caution — three converging navies on one patch of water is how accidents become wars.
