# Han Ji-won

## Overview
Petty Officer, Republic of Korea Navy — hospital corpsman. Assigned to the second CATF–Survey Corps rendezvous to deliver a training session on modern medical supplies: morphine auto-injectors, broad-spectrum antibiotics, antiseptic wound care, and sterile suture technique. Eldian-language competence is functional but limited; relies on [[Frieda]]'s translated printed instructions and a translator's tablet.
