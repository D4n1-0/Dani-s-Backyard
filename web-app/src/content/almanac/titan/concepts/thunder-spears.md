# Thunder Spears

## Summary
Rocket-propelled explosive lances developed by the Survey Corps — the first Paradis weapon capable of piercing hardened Titan armour.

## Details
Fired from ODM gear mounts, Thunder Spears deliver shaped-charge warheads designed to penetrate Armored Titan plating. They require a timed fuse pull before launch — suicidal-close engagement range. Effective but crude, representing Paradis's first step toward anti-Titan weapons beyond swords. Used decisively against the Armored Titan at Shiganshina.

## Context
For the modern world, Thunder Spears are rudimentary — roughly equivalent to early WWII-era bazookas. But their existence demonstrates Paradis's capacity for rapid military innovation when under existential pressure. The technology gap between Paradis and the modern world is vast — but Paradis learns fast.
