# Kedros Garrison

## Overview

Kedros Garrison is a coastal border town on the Valdran-Concordat frontier — nominally under the [[Federated Crowns of Valdris|Valdran]] diarchy, culturally mixed, and perpetually tense. It exists because the harbour needed a naval station, the army needed a garrison, and the [[The Census|census bureau]] needed somewhere to send surplus population when the precinct-cities tipped too close to a million souls.

The town curves around a natural harbour where the Tethyan shallows meet the eastern coast. On clear days, looking seaward past the breakwater, you can see the [[The Celestial Tether|Celestial Tether's]] three silver ribbons catching the light on the horizon — a permanent reminder of what sits at the centre of the ocean, and what it can do. The air smells of salt and coal smoke. The gulls have learned to ignore the soldiers, and the soldiers have learned to ignore the gulls.

Every five years, like weather, the border dispute between Valdris and the [[Iron Concordat]] flares up — a ritual of diplomatic letters, troop rotations, and requisitioned warehouses. The locals call it the **Five-Year Tension**. The last one was six years ago, which means the next one is overdue.

## Features

- **The Harbour:** A natural deep-water inlet protected by a stone breakwater laid down in Year 140 PC. Home to a Valdran naval squadron — two pre-dreadnoughts and a screen of torpedo sloops — and a fishing fleet that has learned to navigate around warships. During the Five-Year Tensions, civilian traffic is restricted to the inner basin.
- **The Rail Depot:** The eastern line's terminus. Trains carry troops and supplies from the Valdran interior. During the Tensions, the depot runs twenty-four hours, its cranes swinging day and night.
- **The Transmigrant District:** Identical two-storey tenements on a low rise above the harbour. Home to families relocated from Valdran precinct-cities. The locals call it "New Town" with a particular inflection.
- **The Supermarket:** A single-storey concrete building near the harbour road, with barred windows and a flickering neon sign that reads *KEDROS GENERAL*. The store is run by an elderly local couple — their three children are grown, two were taken by the transmigration lottery years ago, and the third was spared through the Beneficial Exception to care for them. The wife has a chronic lung condition that worsens in winter; the husband manages the store on good days and rests on bad ones. Their surviving child works a state job at the garrison telegraph office and helps with the shop in the evenings. The supermarket stays open through a combination of the wife's determination and the husband's refusal to let the building stand empty. [[Asahi]] works the register. The windows rattle when the troop trains pass.
- **The Airfield:** A grass strip and three iron hangars on the headland east of town, overlooking the sea. The mechanics can see the Tether from the hangar doors on clear days.
- **The Garrison:** A stone fort dating from Year 180 PC, now expanded with concrete barracks and a harbour-facing signal tower. During the Tensions, the garrison doubles its complement through rotating deployments.
- **The Provision Office:** A single-storey building beside the census chapel, where transmigrant families queue for housing allocation, ration cards, and the bureaucratic stamps that let them exist in a new town. Staffed by three clerks who process approximately forty applications per week. The queue is longest on Mondays.

## Inhabitants

Half transmigrant families, half local families. A garrison of 2,000 — swelling to 4,000 during the Tensions. A census clerk who keeps a population board outside the chapel. Fishermen who sell their catch to the supermarket before dawn. Two distinct populations that share a town and a harbour but not a life.

## Current Events

The Five-Year Tension is overdue — the last one was six years ago, and every month without a diplomatic incident is a month the garrison commander spends staring at the telegraph. Concordat patrols have been sighted closer to the border than usual. Valdran naval deployments to the harbour have increased. The census clerk has begun drafting secondary relocation orders, because if the Tension tips into something real, the transmigrant district is the first place the Concordat will shell — and the first place Valdris will evacuate.
