# Frieda

## Overview
Eldian mechanical engineer, age 24. Born in the [[Liberio Internment Zone]]. Assigned to the *Pelikan* expedition because her aptitude test scores exceeded every Marleyan candidate in her year group — a tool too valuable to waste on internment-zone labour.

Spent forty-seven days shovelling coal and repairing steam couplings aboard the *Pelikan*. After capture, [[DeSilva|Ensign DeSilva]] informed her that CATF does not recognise racial distinction — she is not property, not a devil, not a tool. She is a person. The Eldian engineers (all eight) accepted sanctuary and CATF employment.

Frieda does not believe in freedom — Liberio trained that out of her by age ten. But she believes in survival, and CATF offers survival on terms she has never experienced. When asked what she wanted, she replied: *"Show me your engines."* Currently studying gas turbine mechanics aboard the [[JS Suzutsuki (DD-117)]] under Chief Petty Officer Sato.
