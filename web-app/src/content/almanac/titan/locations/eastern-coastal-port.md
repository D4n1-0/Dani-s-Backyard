# Eastern Coastal Port

Paradis's primary external harbour, constructed at wartime pace following the Battle of Shiganshina. Built to service the new railway connecting the eastern coastline to Wall Rose and the interior. Features stone piers, Garrison artillery emplacements on the surrounding cliffs, and a railway terminal. Site of the first [[CATF–Survey Corps Rendezvous|CATF–Survey Corps]] naval patrol engagements (Survey Corps captured two Marleyan cruisers here in Chapter 5). Hosted the second CATF–Survey Corps rendezvous when the [[USS Tripoli (LHA-7)]] arrived bearing the first gifts from Earth.
