# Yoon Seok-jin

## Description
Fifty-seven years old. Admiral, Chief of Staff of the Republic of Korea Navy. Career surface warfare officer — commanded a Gwanggaeto the Great-class destroyer before rising through fleet command. Lean, grey-haired, speaks in measured sentences as though every word has been weighed. His instinct is to be clinical where others are emotional. Understands, perhaps better than anyone in the room, that three converging navies on one patch of water is how accidents turn into wars.

## Background
Naval Academy graduate. Destroyer command in the 2000s, Maritime Task Flotilla command in the 2010s. Rose through the Joint Chiefs system with a reputation for operational precision. Has commanded in naval exercises alongside the US Seventh Fleet and Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force.

## Relationships
- [[Lee Jun-ho]] — professional ally; appreciates Lee's willingness to listen to operational counsel
- Minister [[Kim Dong-wook]] — respectful but often at odds on tempo

## Current Status
In the crisis meeting, urging caution on naval deployments and pushing for joint communication protocols before the US and Chinese navies arrive in proximity.
