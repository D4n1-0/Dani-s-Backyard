# Joint Scientific Committee

## Summary
A scientific oversight body currently under construction, co-chaired by [[Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology|KIOST]] and the [[State Oceanic Administration (PRC)|SOA]], charged with coordinating all scientific investigation of [[The Gate]] — planned to hold its first virtual session within 72 hours of the Seoul-Beijing hotline agreement.

## Details
The committee was proposed during the [[Seoul-Beijing Hotline]] call between [[Lee Jun-ho]] and [[Zheng Weimin]]. Its founding principles:
- **Co-chaired**: KIOST (Dr. [[Ahn Jae-hyun]]) and SOA (Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]]) serve as joint leads
- **Observers only**: Japan, the United States, and relevant international bodies may send observers, but decision-making authority rests with Seoul and Beijing
- **Joint output**: All data, publications, and press briefings are jointly produced
- **First session**: Virtual meeting within 72 hours; in-person session at a neutral location to follow

## Context
The committee serves dual purposes. Scientifically, it pools data from the on-scene vessels to build a comprehensive model of the Gate's physical properties and the mixing zone between the Yellow Sea and the AOT-side ocean. Politically, it locks Seoul and Beijing into a cooperative framework before Washington or Brussels can impose their own — both governments understand that whoever leads the science controls the narrative.
