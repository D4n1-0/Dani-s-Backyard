# JS Suzutsuki (DD-117)

## Overview
An Akizuki-class guided-missile destroyer of the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force. Displacement 6,800 tons. Commanded by Captain [[Saito Hiroshi]].

First CATF warship to cross [[The Gate]] into the AOT world — transited at 05:00 on Day 29 with running lights dark and crew at general quarters, establishing the precedent that the Gate would be treated as a strait rather than a barrier.

## Features
- COGAG propulsion (gas turbines) — no funnel smoke, low acoustic signature at cruise
- AEGIS-derived combat system
- Helicopter deck and hangar
- Crew: approximately 200

## Current Events
Stationed at the [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]] as part of Task Group CATF-Alpha. [[Frieda]] is currently studying the ship's gas turbine engines under Chief Petty Officer Sato.
