# The Sifting

## Summary
A deeper Inquisition-level protocol employed by the [[Office of the Propagation of Virtue]], reserved for cases where the standard [[Rite of Discernment]] is deemed insufficient — where the subject may be warded, the taint hidden, or the examiner faces conditions of maximum uncertainty. A Sifting is not a test of faith. It is a test of nature, conducted under controlled conditions and involving sensory, physical, and verbal examination. It is rarely performed outside the [[Principality of New Antioch]].

## Details
The Sifting involves four stages:

1. **Consecration of the space.** The subject is isolated in a chamber stripped of external influence — no light but candle or chrism-flame, no sound but the examiner's voice and the subject's own body. The examiner anoints the space with blessed chrism and consecrated incense.
2. **Anointment of the subject.** The examiner marks the subject's forehead, sternum, and both palms with chrism in the sign of the Trinity, invoking the protection of St. Michael the Archangel and the intercession of the Seventy-Seven Martyrs.
3. **The Orichalcum Test.** The examiner places an Orichalcum-tipped staff against the subject's sternum. The metal is said to respond to infernal presence — growing warm in the presence of corruption, cold in the presence of the divine, and remaining inert against blankness. The subject is held in silence while the examiner reads pulse, respiration, and micro-expression for signs of hidden taint.
4. **Verbal Interrogation.** The examiner asks not about doctrine but about the subject's inner life — fears, loves, dreams, the condition of the soul as the subject understands it. The purpose is not to judge the answers but to observe the manner in which they are given. A soul touched by the Adversary will deflect, dissemble, or crack under sustained inquiry. A soul that is what it claims to be will answer cleanly, however uncomfortable the truth.

## Context
[[Inquisitor Paulus]] insisted on a full Sifting rather than a third [[Rite of Discernment]] — arguing that two Rites had already found blankness and that a blank page could be written on. The Sifting he performed on [[Rebecca Osei|Lieutenant Commander Osei]] in the bowels of *[[USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)|USS Gerald R. Ford]]* found no infernal presence — but found something the standard Rite had missed. [[Rebecca Osei|Osei]]'s soul was no longer blank. It had been touched — not by the Adversary, but by the weight of what she had witnessed in the [[Crown of England|Faithful Crown]]'s London. This finding disturbed [[Inquisitor Paulus|Paulus]] more than blankness would have, because it meant the modern world could be moved by the Faithful Crown's reality without being claimed by it.
