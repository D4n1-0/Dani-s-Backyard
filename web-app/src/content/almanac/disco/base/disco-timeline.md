# Disco Timeline

*Full chronology of Elysium, the Isolas, Revachol, and the events leading to the Disco scenario. See the Dashboard for a visual overview (Mermaid diagram).*

## Key Dates

- **~8,000 yrs ago** — Perikarnassian period
- **~500 yrs ago** — Franconigerian Centuries, Innocence Franconegro
- **~380 yrs ago** — Dolorian Century, Dolores Dei, Interisolary Age begins
- **Discovery of Insulinde** — Revachol founded
- **Suzerainty of Revachol** — The Capital of the World
- **Filippian Decline** — Regnum Cocainum
- **'02** — Antecentennial Revolution
- **'02–'08** — Commune of Revachol
- **13 May '08** — The Landing. Coalition crushes the Commune.
- **'08–'51** — Zone of Control. Moralintern occupation.
- **The Twenties** — Urban war, measles, nuclear pile meltdown
- **The Thirties** — The New: disco and ultraliberal boom
- **The Forties** — Economic collapse, austerity
- **March '51** — *Disco Elysium* (game events). Murder investigation, Tribunal.
- **Late March '51** — Disco scenario start. Terminal reclaimed.
- **May '51** — Le Retour. Coalition mandate expires.
