# Chevalier Paul (D621)

## Overview
A Horizon-class air defence destroyer of the Marine Nationale, flagship of Capitaine de vaisseau [[Étienne Roussel]]'s destroyer division. The Chevalier Paul was ordered to Brest to proceed independently to the portal cordon alongside her division-mate *Bretagne*, completing the transit in a single thirty-hour sprint at twenty-eight knots.

## Features
- Principal Anti-Air Missile System (PAAMS) with Aster 15/30 missiles
- HERAKLion multi-function radar
- Anti-surface and anti-submarine warfare capabilities
- Gas turbine propulsion

## Current Events
Arrived at the portal cordon at eighteen hundred hours on Day 10, taking station at the centre of the French sector. France's primary naval asset at the cordon, asserting independent European presence.
