# Holy See

## Overview

The modern Catholic Church — the Church of Rome, the See of Peter — in the modern Earth timeline. Two thousand years old, shaped by the Reformation, the Enlightenment, the Second Vatican Council, and the long peace of a world that has never known the Adversary. A Church that has learned to be gentle, convinced that forced faith is no faith at all. Ecumenical, diplomatic, and utterly alien to the [[Faithful]] Church of the Trench Crusade timeline — despite sharing the same apostolic succession, the same liturgy, the same Latin prayers.

## Structure

- **Secretariat of State** — the Vatican's diplomatic corps; dispatched [[Monsignor Franco Ricci]] to the portal zone to assess the theological implications of interdimensional contact
- **Holy Father** — the Pope; receives direct reports on the portal crisis

## Assets

- Diplomatic presence in nearly every nation on modern Earth
- The moral authority of a two-thousand-year-old institution
- A theology that has evolved through centuries the Faithful Church never experienced

## Relationships

- [[Faithful]] — same apostolic succession, same Latin liturgy, entirely different religion
- [[United Kingdom (Modern)]] — engaged through the Vatican's diplomatic channels

## Current Events

The Vatican has dispatched a representative to the portal zone. The Catholic Church is watching the theological implications of interdimensional contact — and the presence of a pre-Schism Catholic Church on the other side — with intense interest.
