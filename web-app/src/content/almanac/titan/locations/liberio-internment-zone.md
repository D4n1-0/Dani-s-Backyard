# Liberio Internment Zone

## Overview
The primary confinement zone for mainland Eldians within the [[Marleyan Empire]]. A walled ghetto where Subjects of Ymir are legally required to reside, wear identifying armbands, and submit to Marleyan authority. Access to the outside world is restricted; leaving without authorisation is a capital crime. The zone produces both the empire's Titan Shifters ([[Warrior Unit]]) and its expendable Eldian conscript soldiers.

## Features
- High walls and guard checkpoints
- Warrior candidate training grounds
- Residential quarters — overcrowded, impoverished
- The harbour — where Eldians are shipped to battlefields
- International festival venue — built for Willy Tybur's planned declaration (Year 854)

## Inhabitants
- Eldian families living under occupation — including the Braun, Grice, and Yeager (elder) households
- [[Warrior Unit]] members and candidates: [[Reiner Braun]], [[Gabi Braun]], [[Falco Grice]], Udo, Zofia
- Marleyan guards and overseers

## Current Events
Tense. The loss of the Colossal and Female Titans on Paradis has increased scrutiny on the Warrior families. The Mid-East War is consuming Eldian conscripts. The propaganda machine is accelerating — preparing the population for a war of extermination against Paradis.
