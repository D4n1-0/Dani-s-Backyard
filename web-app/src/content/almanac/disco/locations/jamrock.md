# Jamrock

## Overview

*A district of Revachol. Home to Precinct 57 of the RCM. Gritty, industrial, perpetually underfunded. The place Harry Du Bois and Kim Kitsuragi returned to after the Martinaise case. Far enough from Martinaise to feel like another world, close enough that the shadow of the bay still reaches it.*

## Inhabitants

- [[Harry Du Bois]] — RCM Lieutenant, Precinct 57
- [[Kim Kitsuragi]] — RCM Lieutenant, Precinct 57

## Current Events

*The Martinaise murder case is closed. Precinct 57 has moved on to other investigations. The RCM's official interest in Martinaise has concluded.*
