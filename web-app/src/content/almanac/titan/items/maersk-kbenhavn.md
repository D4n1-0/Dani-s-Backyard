# Maersk København

## Description
A Maersk Line heavy-lift container vessel, approximately 300 metres in length, chartered under CATF contract for AOT-side logistics operations. Carried prefabricated building modules, construction materials, a mobile crane, generators, vehicles, and the initial outfitting for the [[South Bank (Odiha)]] compound. Maersk-blue hull, five-high container stacks, civilian-crewed under Danish registry.

## Function
Heavy logistics transport for the CATF south bank construction. The *Maersk* was the first Earth-registered cargo vessel to enter Marleyan territorial waters and the largest vessel ever to enter the Odiha river mouth. Her cargo unloaded over three days, accelerating construction from a dredging operation to a functional compound.

## History
Chartered by CATF after the MOU was signed. Loaded at Singapore, transited the Gate, staged from [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub|Grande Terre]], and steamed north to Odiha under escort of the USS *Gerald R. Ford* CSG. Delivered her cargo over three days at the temporary south bank landing, then returned south upon charter completion.
