# United Kingdom (Modern)

## Overview
The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland in the modern timeline — a secular parliamentary democracy, constitutional monarchy, and NATO member. The first nation to make contact with the [[Trench Crusade Earth]] when [[The Anomaly]] opened in the Celtic Sea, and the initial lead state in managing the diplomatic, military, and intelligence response to the portal.

## Government
The UK is governed by a [[National Unity Government]] — a fragile cross-party coalition led by Prime Minister [[Stephen Davenport]], formed in the aftermath of an unresolved constitutional crisis. The NUG's fragility constrains every decision: Davenport cannot afford the political capital a full, transparent engagement with the Faithful would require, and his policy of [[Cautious Secular Engagement]] is as much a product of domestic necessity as philosophical conviction.

## Relationships
- [[United States]] — closest ally; tensions over the US push for joint military command
- [[France]] — NATO ally; jurisdictional dispute over the Anomaly's location in international waters
- [[Germany]] — NATO ally; constitutional constraints prevent military commitment
- [[Crown of England]] — the Faithful counterpart on the other side of the Anomaly; the relationship is the core of the story

## Policy
The UK's course is [[Cautious Secular Engagement]]: treat the Faithful as a foreign power, refuse theological entanglement, maintain secular neutrality — while preparing for the possibility that the Faithful will not accept secularism as a valid framework.
