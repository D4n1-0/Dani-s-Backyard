# Yelena

## Overview
Tall, severe, with short blonde hair. Defected from the Marleyan military where she served as a conscripted soldier. Organised a mutiny aboard a Marleyan patrol vessel and led the Volunteers to Paradis Island, offering military technology and insider knowledge in exchange for Marley's destruction.

Secretly devoted to [[Zeke Yeager]] and his Euthanasia Plan — the sterilisation of all Subjects of Ymir. Her fanaticism is total, her loyalty transferable only as far as Zeke's agenda permits. Paradis sees a useful ally. Zeke sees a tool. Yelena sees a prophet.

## Observed by CATF
First identified via drone surveillance on Day 48 when the Volunteer vessel arrived at Paradis's eastern port. CATF analysts recorded her disembarking alongside [[Onyankopon]] and being greeted by [[Hange Zoë]] and a formation of [[Survey Corps]] soldiers. Operational significance: Paradis is now in contact with an outside power and receiving intelligence on Marleyan military dispositions.
