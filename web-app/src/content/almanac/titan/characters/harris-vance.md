# Harris Vance

## Overview
Director of the CIA. Age 52. Senate-confirmed, former inspector general on intelligence oversight committees. A political appointee who knows exactly where the legal boundaries are — and therefore knows precisely how close to step to them without crossing. Speaks in budget-authority and feasibility-study language. Does not say "yes." Says "prove the concept first, then we'll call it something else."

## Role in CATF
Not a member of the [[Quadripartite Executive Committee]] — that role belongs to [[Elizabeth Cross]] at State. Vance operates inside the parallel intelligence framework, not the diplomatic one. He received the Ackerman biological data via ONI backchannels, not through formal CATF reporting channels.
