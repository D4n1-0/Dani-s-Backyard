# Zhang Haifeng

## Description
Fifty-nine years old. Vice Admiral, People's Liberation Army Navy, Deputy Commander of the Northern Theater Command Navy. Career surface warfare officer — commanded a Type 052D destroyer, later a destroyer flotilla, now responsible for all PLAN operations in the Yellow Sea and Bohai Gulf. Hard-faced, unapologetic, speaks with the certainty of a man who has never served a government that lost a war. Sees the Gate as a national security asset within China's maritime claims — and a threat to be contained.

## Background
Graduate of the Dalian Naval Academy. Rose through the destroyer force during the PLAN's modernisation era. Served in South China Sea operations during the 2010s island-building campaign. Transferred to Northern Theater Command as Deputy Commander. His instinct is always to assert, never to concede. The Gate threatens that calculus.

## Relationships
- [[Zheng Weimin]] — views him as a political operator who doesn't understand military imperatives
- [[Chen Shaoqiang]] — the on-scene CCG captain; Zhang sees him as a useful forward element but ultimately a PAP officer, not PLAN

## Current Status
In Beijing, arguing for immediate assertion of Chinese control over the Gate site. Has already ordered a Type 055 cruiser to make best speed toward the Yellow Sea.
