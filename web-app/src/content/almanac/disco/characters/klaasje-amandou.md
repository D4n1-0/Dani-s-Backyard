# Klaasje Amandou

## Summary
Born in [[Oranje]], ~'23. A corporate spy who fled her employers ([[Ostentatious Orchestrations]]) and ended up in [[Martinaise]]. The key witness — and key liar — in the mercenary hanging case. Arrested by the ICP, escaped from custody, fled Revachol before the investigation concluded.

## Background
Worked for a corporate espionage division. Conducted operations that violated numerous international agreements. The ICP (International Collaboration Police) was hunting her. Her presence in Martinaise is the reason the mercenary was sent there — he was investigating her, not the strike.

## Current Status
Fled. Whereabouts unknown. The ICP is still looking for her. She owes [[Harrier Du Bois]] her life and left him a letter.
