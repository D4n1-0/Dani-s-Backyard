# Xiang Yang Hong 06

## Description
Chinese polar-capable oceanographic research ship operated by the [[State Oceanic Administration (PRC)]]. Modern design, built for extended operations in high-latitude conditions. Equipped with a suite of long-range UAVs, an autonomous surface vehicle (ASV) — a solar-powered robotic kayak — and a deep-water remotely operated vehicle (ROV) rated for 6,000 metres. Carries wet and dry laboratories, a CTD winch, multi-beam sonar, and satellite communications array. Scientific complement of approximately 20 researchers, crew of 35.

## Function
Polar research, deep-sea survey, marine environmental monitoring. Rerouted from Qingdao to the Gate site under SOA emergency tasking following the hotline agreement.

## History
Arrives at the exclusion zone perimeter on Day 3 alongside [[R-V Isabu]], carrying Dr. [[Wang Lijuan]] as the lead Chinese scientific representative. Serves as the forward base for Chinese ASV and ROV operations through the Gate, with its long-range UAVs extending the survey to a 100-km radius of the AOT-side ocean.
