# CATF–Survey Corps Rendezvous

## Summary
The first face-to-face meeting between the [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)]] and the [[Survey Corps]], held on the night of Day 104 at the [[Rendezvous Grassland]] — an open field outside the Walls of Paradis. Two Airbus H125 helicopters landed under cover of darkness. The CATF delegation ([[Elias Vogler]], [[Tanaka Kenji]], and [[Ahn Jae-hyun]]) met a Survey Corps force of ten led by [[Hange Zoë]]. An agreement in principle was reached for cooperation against the Rumbling, research exchange, and eventual resource extraction. As a trust-building measure, a mutual liaison exchange was arranged: [[Connie Springer]] to CATF, Captain [[Park Sung-ho]] to Paradis.

## Details
- **Date:** Night of Day 104 (~Year 850 TC / 2026-09-29 Modern)
- **Location:** [[Rendezvous Grassland]], ~30 km from the eastern railway terminus, outside Wall Maria territory
- **CATF assets:** Two Airbus H125 helicopters, launched from a CATF vessel positioned beyond visual horizon off Paradis's eastern coast
- **Survey Corps force:** 10 soldiers including [[Hange Zoë]], [[Armin Arlert]], [[Levi Ackerman]], [[Mikasa Ackerman]], [[Jean Kirstein]], [[Connie Springer]], two Survey Corps officers, two Garrison representatives
- **Notable exclusions:** [[Eren Yeager]] (Founding Titan — too valuable to expose), [[Historia Reiss]] (Queen — head of state)
- **Lighting:** Helicopter nav lights, landing lights, and spotlights; no natural moonlight (overcast)
- **Language:** Eldian/Marleyan; [[Elias Vogler]] served as primary speaker for CATF; [[Hange Zoë]] served as primary speaker for the Survey Corps
- **CATF disclosed:** origin (parallel Earth), Gate existence, three months of surveillance, knowledge of Wall Titans and Rumbling, intentions (stability, security, research, resource extraction)
- **CATF withheld:** captured Pure Titan, Ex Nihilo mass-creation finding, Eldian biology implications, [[Klaus Dreher]]'s notebook
- **Liaison exchange:** [[Connie Springer]] to Grande Terre, Captain [[Park Sung-ho]] to Paradis (exchange at next rendezvous)
- **Follow-up:** Second rendezvous scheduled one week later at same coordinates

## Context
The rendezvous fulfilled the [[First Contact Protocol]]'s original framework — intercept, isolate, inform, integrate — though in reverse: the Survey Corps chose to attend rather than being intercepted. The success of the meeting validated CATF's strategic calculus that the Survey Corps, despite its military capability, was fundamentally a defensive organisation driven by survival and curiosity — not ideology or conquest. The meeting also represented the first time in either world's history that a pre-industrial civilisation made peaceful contact with a technologically superior power on terms approaching equality.
