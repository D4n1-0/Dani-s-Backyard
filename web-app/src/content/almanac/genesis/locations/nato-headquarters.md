# NATO Headquarters

## Overview
The political and administrative headquarters of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization in Brussels, Belgium. The site of the emergency North Atlantic Council session where the US, UK, France, and Germany's positions on the Anomaly collide.

## Features
- North Atlantic Council chamber — seats thirty-two delegations
- Secure communication links to all member-state capitals
