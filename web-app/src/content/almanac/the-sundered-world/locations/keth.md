# Keth

## Overview

Keth was once a city of moderate size in the contested borderlands of [[Oriens]]. In Year 287 Post-Convergence, its authorities attempted to obstruct and harm an [[The Observers|Observer]]. The response was the most devastating single strike since the [[War of Convergence]]: a 15-kilometre radius of fused glass that still glows faintly on moonless nights.

## Features

- **The Glass Plain:** The crater centre is a perfectly circular disc of dark, vitrified material — soil, stone, organic matter, and steel fused into a single substance. It is warm to the touch, even in winter, and emits a barely perceptible blue-green luminescence in total darkness.
- **The Penumbra:** The outer ring of the blast zone, where structures were not melted but flattened. Partial walls, twisted rail lines, and the occasional intact safe or vault draw scavengers despite the risks.
- **The Observer's Last Stand:** A marked location — maintained by the [[Theocratic Union of the Last Flame|Last Flame]] as a shrine — where the Observer was allegedly obstructed. No one knows if it was destroyed, departed, or is still there beneath the glass.

## Inhabitants

None. The crater is uninhabited and officially sealed. Scavengers enter at their peril — residual contamination (exact nature unknown, as radiation physics is a forbidden discipline) causes wasting sickness in those who spend more than a few days inside the Penumbra.

## Current Events

The [[Iron Concordat|Concordat]] has proposed "surveying" the crater for recoverable materials — a move the Theocracy condemns as desecration. Kaelen's drones patrol the site on a regular circuit, more frequently than any other location in Oriens, as if confirming that the lesson remains legible.
