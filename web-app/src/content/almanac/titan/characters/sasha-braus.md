# Sasha Braus

## Overview
Scout, [[Survey Corps]]. Former hunter from Dauper village. Known throughout the Corps for her extraordinary marksmanship, her bottomless appetite, and an instinct for reading terrain that rivals any officer's tactical assessment. Survived the Battle of Shiganshina. In the CATF timeline, she is alive and active at Year 850.
