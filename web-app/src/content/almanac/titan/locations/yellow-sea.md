# Yellow Sea

## Overview
The body of water between the Korean Peninsula and mainland China where [[The Gate]] opened on a Saturday evening. A strategically contested maritime zone with overlapping territorial claims, active shipping lanes, and regular military patrols from multiple nations. The Gate's precise coordinates (35°29'57.7"N 123°42'16.8"E) place it in international waters — but proximity to both Korean and Chinese coastlines ensures immediate attention from both sides.

## Features
- Average depth ~44m
- Heavy commercial shipping traffic
- Military patrol zones: ROK Navy, PRC PLAN, US 7th Fleet (regional)
- Fishing grounds — civilian vessels in the area

## Inhabitants
- Modern military assets: ROK Coast Guard, PRC maritime patrol
- Commercial shipping
- No permanent population
