# Tom Ksaver

## Description
An Eldian researcher and former inheritor of the Beast Titan. A mentor figure to Zeke Yeager during his time as a Warrior candidate.

## Background
A Titan researcher who used his position as a Warrior to study Titan biology. He inherited the Beast Titan prior to Zeke and was one of the few people Zeke trusted. He passed the Beast Titan to Zeke before the end of his 13-year term.
