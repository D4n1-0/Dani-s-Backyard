# Seoul-Beijing Hotline

## Summary
A dedicated secure communication channel between the [[National Security Office (ROK)]] in Seoul and the [[Central Foreign Affairs Commission (PRC)]] in Beijing — traditionally presidential-level, elevated to operational use during the Gate crisis by [[Lee Jun-ho]].

## Details
A Tandberg encrypted voice terminal with a single-button connection. By protocol, the hotline is reserved for the Director of National Security or the President on the Korean side, and the Director of the CFAC or the General Secretary on the Chinese side. [[Lee Jun-ho]] used it at the Senior Secretary level — a breach of protocol that he justified by the urgency of the crisis.

The call between Lee and [[Zheng Weimin]] represents the hotline's first use for an issue that is neither a security crisis nor a diplomatic incident — but something that is both and neither. The conversation produced the [[Exclusion Zone]] agreement and the [[Joint Scientific Committee]] framework, demonstrating that the hotline is capable of functioning as a negotiation channel, not merely an emergency notification system.

## Context
The hotline's elevation from presidential to operational suggests a broader shift in how Seoul and Beijing manage the Gate crisis. Future chapters may see it become a routine backchannel for coordination that bypasses slower diplomatic channels.
