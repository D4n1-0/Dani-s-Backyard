# Four-Power Gate Protocol

## Summary
The treaty signed in Singapore on Day 13 of the Gate era, expanding the Seoul-Beijing framework into a four-nation coalition. Signed by [[Lee Jun-ho]], [[Zheng Weimin]], [[Elizabeth Cross]], and [[Nakamura Kenji]].

## Structure
The treaty is two-tier:
- **Public face:** Joint Gate Research Initiative — a multilateral scientific cooperation agreement extending the exclusion zone to include American and Japanese research vessels under a coordinated protocol. Vague language, names no threats, claims no territory.
- **Classified annex:** Establishes the [[Combined Anomaly Task Force (CATF)]] — a standing combined military command with rotational authority. Authorises infrastructure on the AOT side, drone reconnaissance, undersea sensors, and a standing AOT-side naval force.

## Key Provisions
- Each nation retains operational control of its own forces
- Strategic decisions require consensus of all four parties via the [[Quadripartite Executive Committee]]
- No unilateral action within the AOT exclusion zone or Earth-side perimeter
- Full intelligence sharing
- Joint scientific oversight through the [[Joint Scientific Committee]]
- First-contact protocol: intercept, isolate, inform, integrate
