# Park Sung-ho

## Overview
Captain, Republic of Korea Marine Corps. Age 38. Two tours in Special Reconnaissance. Assigned to CATF and handed command of Operation REEVE's ground element six hours before the [[USS Tripoli (LHA-7)]] sailed. Designed the capture sequence for the live Titan — seven-step capture protocol, eleven identified points of failure, contingency plan for containment breach on the flight deck.

Led the twelve-Marine capture team during the Paradis insertion. Managed the Corporal [[Lee Dae-won]] casualty situation without mission abort. Recommended Lee for commendation.
