# Gabi Braun

## Description
A young Eldian Warrior candidate and cousin to Reiner Braun. Exceptionally skilled with a rifle and fiercely indoctrinated with Marleyan propaganda. Eventually inherits the Armored Titan.

## Background
Raised in the Liberio Internment Zone, Gabi was selected as a Warrior candidate to inherit the Armored Titan from Reiner. Her combat skills and conviction make her one of Marley's most promising young soldiers.
