# HMS Resolute

## Overview
A dreadnought of the Faithful [[Crown of England]], bearing a thirty-foot crucifix welded to its bridge. Twin twelve-inch turrets fore and aft, coal-fired, with masts festooned with signal halyards and black banners. The name is painted in gold leaf across its stern in an archaic script.

## Features
- Bridge crucifix coated in fresh human blood
- Armoured chasuble-and-relic storage in the chaplain's quarters
- Twelve-inch main battery, trained off-axis as a gesture of strained neutrality

## Inhabitants
- [[Alistair Pembroke]] — captain
- [[Father Beauchamp]] — chaplain

## Current Events
Severely damaged in a collision with the *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|USS Stout]]* during an ASW chase against a [[The Heretic Submarine|Heretic submarine]] that had been conducting intelligence surveillance on the portal cordon. The *[[USS Stout (DDG-55)|Stout]]*'s bow tore into the *[[HMS Resolute|Resolute]]*'s port side below the waterline. Listing and mission-incapable — flooding contained but the ship cannot fight. The flagship of the Faithful cordon is crippled. [[Father Beauchamp|Beauchamp]] and [[Alistair Pembroke|Pembroke]] are aboard, coordinating the Faithful response to [[Davenport's Reply]] and [[The Theology of Waiting|Aldric's theology]] while damage-control parties work to save the ship.
