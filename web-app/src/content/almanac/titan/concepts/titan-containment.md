# Titan Containment

## Summary
The protocol and facility for holding a live Pure Titan specimen at the [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]]. Developed as an extension of the [[First Contact Protocol]] and the biological containment standards established by the CATF joint scientific committee. The containment pen is a converted 40-foot shipping container with reinforced steel anchoring points, a remote light-control system, and 24-hour camera surveillance.

## Design
- **Structure:** Converted shipping container with roof removed and reinforced lid; hardened steel industrial anchors rated for static load of 50 tonnes
- **Light control:** Remote-operated light-blocking shroud system; Titan is dormant in darkness, active in sunlight
- **Anchoring:** Four-point chain anchoring (shoulders, pelvis) connected to floor-mounted restraints
- **Access:** Remote sampling ports for blood, tissue, and spinal fluid extraction while dormant; no direct human entry into the pen while Titan is active
- **Contingency:** If containment fails, concentrated M2 .50 calibre fire to the nape is the authorised contingency — specimen destruction is acceptable if human life is at risk

## Operational Status
One Class A (6 m) Pure Titan in containment. Dormant cycle maintained via light control. Daily sampling during dormancy periods. Active monitoring during sunlight hours. Full biological analysis ongoing.
