# Jürgen Haas

## Description
Late thirties. Senior able seaman of the Imperial Marleyan Navy, nineteen years of service. Assigned to the *Pelikan* polar expedition as a deck crewman. Intercepted by CATF on Day 36 and spent the following two months at [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub|Grande Terre]] as a detainee-turned-consultant. Broad-shouldered, quiet, a man who joined the navy to escape a farming village and stayed because the sea made more sense than the land.

## Background
Career enlisted sailor. No political connections. No Titan Security Division involvement. Chosen as one of the fourteen returning crew specifically because his record was clean and his testimony would carry the weight of an ordinary man rather than a political operative. During his two months at Grande Terre, he developed a genuine working relationship with CATF personnel — including the Eldian engineers he had previously regarded through the lens of Marleyan indoctrination. His return to Odiha was voluntary: he wanted to go home. He wanted to be the one who told the empire what was coming.

## Relationships
- [[Elias Vogler]] — expedition leader; Haas respects Vogler as a scientist who saw further than his uniform
- [[Frieda]] — Eldian engineer whose humanity Haas learned to recognise during their shared time at Grande Terre
- [[Satomi Ishihara]] — taught her Marleyan slang and engine-room terminology during the voyage
- [[Konrad Verner]] — delivered the first account of CATF directly to the admiral at the Odiha pier

## Current Status
Returned to Odiha. Debriefed by Marleyan naval intelligence for eleven hours. Reunited with family. Processing what home means after two months in a world that rewrote every assumption he had about his nation's place in reality.
