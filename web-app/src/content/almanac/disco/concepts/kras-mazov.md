# Kras Mazov

## Summary
The founding theorist of communism in [[Elysium]] and leader of the [[Antecentennial Revolution]] in [[Graad]]. Died in '08 when his government was overthrown.

## Details
Mazov's philosophy — infra-materialism — holds that the material conditions of society determine its consciousness, and that the Pale is manifested past/information rather than anti-matter. His work is foundational to dialectical materialism in Elysium. The star-and-antlers symbol, developed in the '60s of the Last Century, was adopted by Mazov and the Communards.

## Context
Though dead for 43 years, Mazov haunts Revachol. The communist vision quest in *Disco Elysium* is literally a conversation with his ideas. His failure and the subsequent mass executions are the reason communism is a tragic faith in Revachol, not a live political movement with institutional power.
