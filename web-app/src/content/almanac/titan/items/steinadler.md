# Steinadler

## Description
A Marleyan steam frigate, approximately 80 metres in length, twin stacks, eight-inch guns, crew complement of approximately two hundred. The *Steinadler* was the pride of the Southern Command's surface fleet when she was commissioned two decades ago — now a mid-generation warship, obsolescent by Marleyan standards, obsolete by CATF standards. She has been stationed at [[Odiha]] for three years under the command of Captain [[Langsdorff]], serving as the district's primary naval asset.

## History
Assigned to the Southern Command as a presence platform during the Mid-East War. Her principal mission was coastal patrol — a task she performed without incident for three years until the summer of Year 850, when the [[Pelikan]] returned to port with two foreign vessels in escort. On Day ~170, Admiral [[Strobl]] of the Home Fleet ordered the *Steinadler* to conduct a "routine coastal patrol" of the Odiha river approaches — an order that placed her anchor in the construction channel of the [[South Bank (Odiha)]] compound, directly obstructing CATF construction logistics. Captain Langsdorff held position for fourteen hours before weighing anchor on receiving Commander [[Theo Magath|Magath]]'s runner and intelligence of the approaching [[USS Gerald R. Ford (CVN-78)|USS Gerald R. Ford]] Carrier Strike Group. The *Steinadler* returned to her normal anchorage. No shots were fired. The message was received.
