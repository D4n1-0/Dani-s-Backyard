# Levi Ackerman

## Description
Early thirties. Short, sharp-featured, perpetually unsmiling. The most lethal single combatant in the Survey Corps — an awakened Ackerman whose ODM combat is so fast it appears supernatural. Recently lost most of the Survey Corps veterans, including Erwin Smith, whose life he chose not to save, giving the Titan serum to Armin instead. Carries a deep, quiet grief wrapped in absolute discipline.

## Background
Born in the Underground City beneath Mitras. Orphaned early, raised by the serial killer Kenny Ackerman (his uncle). Joined the Survey Corps after Erwin recruited him. Has killed more Titans and more humans than anyone in the Corps. His Ackerman awakening makes him physically untouchable — but Erwin's death broke something in him that combat cannot repair.

## Relationships
- [[Hange Zoë]] — commander; old comrades, mutual trust
- [[Eren Yeager]] — ward; Levi has kicked Eren's face in more than once to teach him discipline
- [[Mikasa Ackerman]] — distant cousin; mutual respect
- [[Erwin Smith]] — former commander (deceased); Levi carries his final order
- [[Zeke Yeager]] — enemy; Levi swore to kill him

## Current Status
On Paradis Island. Recovering from the Battle of Shiganshina. Missing fingers. Missing Erwin. Still the most dangerous man in two worlds.
