# Bretagne (D655)

## Overview
An Aquitaine-class multi-mission frigate (FREMM) of the Marine Nationale, the second vessel in Capitaine de vaisseau [[Étienne Roussel]]'s destroyer division. Roussel served aboard the Bretagne as a lieutenant earlier in his career before the portal crisis. The Bretagne was ordered to detach from the Atlantic squadron and proceed independently to the portal cordon alongside the *Chevalier Paul*.

## Features
- Multi-mission capability: anti-air, anti-surface, anti-submarine
- Hull-mounted sonar
- Cruise missiles and anti-ship missiles
- Reduced crew complement through automation (Aquitaine-class design)

## Current Events
Holding position six hundred metres off the *Chevalier Paul*'s port quarter in the French sector of the portal cordon, her hull-mounted sonar active and sensors contributing to the multinational picture.
