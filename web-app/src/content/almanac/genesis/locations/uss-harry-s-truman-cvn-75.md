# USS Harry S. Truman (CVN-75)

## Overview
A Nimitz-class nuclear-powered aircraft carrier of the United States Navy, flagship of Carrier Strike Group Two. The Truman was midway through a NATO exercise in the Atlantic when she was ordered to break from the exercise schedule and proceed east at best speed to the portal cordon.

## Features
- Nuclear propulsion — unlimited endurance, but her escorts are not so lucky
- Air wing of F/A-18E/F Super Hornets, EA-18G Growlers, and E-2D Hawkeyes
- Full combat information centre with modern sensor fusion
- Capacity for 5,000+ crew

## Inhabitants
- [[Daniel Brennan]] — commanding officer
- Crew of approximately 5,000

## Current Events
En route to the portal cordon to join the multinational fleet.
