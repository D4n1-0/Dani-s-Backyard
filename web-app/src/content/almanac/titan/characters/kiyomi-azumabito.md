# Kiyomi Azumabito

## Description
Ambassador of the Azumabito clan, representing the Nation of Hizuru. Skilled diplomat and merchant-royalty. Primary agent for negotiating an exclusive trade monopoly on Paradis Island's Iceburst Stone.

## Background
Head of the Azumabito clan's foreign operations. Seeks to restore Hizuru's economic standing by leveraging the clan's historical alliance with the Eldian Empire. Has not yet made contact with Paradis at the time of the Gate's opening (arrival scheduled for Year 852 in canon).

## Current Status
Unaware of the Gate's existence. Her planned Paradis mission may be irrevocably altered by the arrival of a second world.
