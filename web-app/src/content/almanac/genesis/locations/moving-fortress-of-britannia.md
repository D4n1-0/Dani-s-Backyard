# Moving Fortress of Britannia

## Overview
A massive mobile citadel of the Faithful Crown of England, completed in 1907. The Fortress houses forges capable of working [[Orichalcum]], and chaplains of the [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]] are stationed there. It is one of the Crown's key strategic assets, alongside the [[Fortress of the White Cliffs]] and the dreadnought fleet.

## Features
- Orichalcum forges — where [[Father Beauchamp|Beauchamp]]'s crucifix was forged and blessed
- Order chaplain garrison — diviners and warrior-priests of the [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]]

## Context
The Moving Fortress is mobile — its exact position changes with the Crown's strategic requirements. At the time of the Anomaly's appearance, it serves as a rear-echelon support base and manufacturing centre, not a front-line position.
