# First Contact Protocol

## Summary
The CATF standing procedure for encountering AOT-native personnel within the exclusion perimeter. Drafted on Day 19 under the classified annex of the [[Four-Power Gate Protocol]], first executed on Day 36 against the Marleyan polar expedition ship *Pelikan*.

## Four Phases
- **Intercept** — patrol vessel or CATF warship intercepts the contact; armed boarding party secures the vessel; no lethal force unless fired upon
- **Isolate** — crew separated into groups; each group transported to separate facilities; no inter-group communication; information compartmentalised
- **Inform** — full disclosure to cooperative personnel; tailored disclosure to potentially hostile personnel; all information controlled by CATF intelligence section
- **Integrate** — cooperative personnel offered CATF civilian employment; sanctuary offered to individuals facing persecution by their home nations (e.g., Eldians); hostile personnel held pending committee review

## First Execution
The *Pelikan* crew (31 souls): Dr. [[Elias Vogler]] employed as scientific consultant; Oberleutnant [[Klaus Dreher]] held in pragmatic submission; [[Frieda]] and seven other Eldian engineers granted sanctuary and CATF employment.
