# Floch Forster

## Description
Late teens. Reddish-brown hair, gaunt features. The sole survivor of Erwin Smith's suicide charge against the Beast Titan at Shiganshina. Before the charge, he was an ordinary Garrison soldier who joined the Survey Corps out of idealism. After the charge — having watched every comrade around him die while he alone lived — something in him broke.

## Background
Floch's survival was random. The Beast Titan's barrage killed everyone else in his formation. He carried the wounded Erwin toward Levi, desperate for the commander to receive the Titan serum. When Levi chose Armin instead, Floch's worldview crystallised: only a "devil" willing to sacrifice everything can save Paradis. He has not yet become the radical Yeagerist recruiter he will be — but the seeds are planted.

## Relationships
- [[Eren Yeager]] — Floch will come to see Eren as the "devil" Paradis needs
- [[Levi Ackerman]] — resentment; Floch believes Levi chose wrong
- [[Hange Zoë]] — distrust; Floch views the old Survey Corps as too soft

## Current Status
On Paradis. A traumatised survivor whose ideology has not yet fully radicalised — but is primed for it. The Gate will offer him a new vector for his desperation.
