# Morgan Pell

## Description
Chaplain-Lieutenant of the [[Sacred Order of the Dragon]], stationed at the [[Fortress of the White Cliffs]]. A diviner whose face bears the premature ageing of one who has cast true discernment many times. Quiet, precise, with a thin but steady voice. His theological assessments carry weight with the Faithful command.

## Background
[[Morgan Pell|Pell]] serves as the Order's liaison to [[Caius Blackwood|Inquisitor-Admiral Blackwood]], providing theological counsel on matters that extend beyond standard doctrine. He was the first to voice the doctrinal implications of [[The Anomaly]] — that if the other world's souls are neither saved nor damned, the nature of salvation itself may be broader than the Synods teach.

## Relationships
- [[Caius Blackwood]] — his commanding officer at the Fortress
- [[Father Beauchamp]] — fellow chaplain of the Order, now at the threshold

## Current Status
At the [[Fortress of the White Cliffs]], participating in [[Caius Blackwood|Blackwood]]'s theological analysis of the anomaly.
