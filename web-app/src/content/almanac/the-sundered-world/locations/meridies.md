# Meridies

## Overview

Meridies is the scorched south — the equatorial continent of [[Gaethon]], characterised by tropical coastlines, mangrove swamps, and the mineral-rich mountain knot of the Vorran Massif. It is the world's economic artery and its most technologically audacious region, home to the [[Free Cities of the Amber Coast]] and the [[Technate of Vorr]].

## Features

- **[[The Amber Coast]]:** A string of prosperous port-cities stretching along the northern Meridian shoreline. The world's great trade hub — airship yards, telegraph cables, and merchant banking.
- **[[Hanavel]]:** The largest airship yard on Gaethon, where the magnificent *Cloud-King*-class passenger zeppelins are constructed.
- **[[The Vorran Massif]]:** A mountain knot riddled with coal and iron, where the Technate's factories and catacomb-cities press against every edge of the [[Kaelen Mandate|Mandate]].
- **The Southern Approaches:** Unexplored equatorial waters. Rumoured to hold pre-[[War of Convergence|Convergence]] wreckage, but Mandate restrictions on deep-sea exploration make verification impossible.

## Inhabitants

The Free Cities are the world's neutral ground — merchants, bankers, and spies from every faction mingle in their coffee houses and telegraph exchanges. The Vorran Massif is the opposite: insular, secretive, and dangerous, a closed society that views outsiders as potential Inquisition agents.

## Current Events

The Technate's stratospheric airship test has alarmed every power. The Amber League's High Syndic is attempting to mediate a Concordat-Vorran understanding before the troops on the hinterland become an invasion.
