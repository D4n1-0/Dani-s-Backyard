# Heretic Legion

## Overview
The military forces of Hell on Earth, composed of humans who have made the unholy pilgrimage to Hell's Maw and sworn eternal fealty to an Arch-Devil. One third of Earth's population lives under Hell's servitude.

## Structure
Organised into numbered Legions (e.g., XXI Heretic Legion 'Widowmakers'), each serving a different Arch-Devil patron. Includes Heretic Priests, Choristers, Anointed, Heretic Troopers, Death Commandos, and the Heretic Fleet.

## Assets
- Heretic Fleet (naval branch, commanded by High Captain Ranga)
- Goetic Magic (reality-warping sorcery)
- Infernal artillery (Goetic rune shells)
- Sentient barbed wire
- Submarines (constructed 1866 onward)
- War altars and Golden Calf idols
