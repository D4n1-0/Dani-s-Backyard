# Nakamura Kenji

## Overview
Vice Foreign Minister of Japan. Career diplomat with postings in Beijing, Washington, and Seoul. Speaks all three languages with quiet precision. Carried SIGINT intercepts to the Singapore negotiation that confirmed AOT-native radio transmissions — Japan had pieced together the Gate's existence within thirty-six hours of its opening.

Represents Japan on the CATF [[Quadripartite Executive Committee]]. Quiet, sharp, strategic — the man who knows more than he reveals and reveals only what serves Japanese interests. His intelligence apparatus gives Japan disproportionate influence within the coalition.
