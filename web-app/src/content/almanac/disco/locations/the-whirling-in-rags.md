# The Whirling-in-Rags

## Overview

*Three storeys of peeling plaster and salt-eaten brick, its sign hanging at an angle that suggests the building itself is drunk. Ground-floor windows boarded up — Tribunal damage. Bullet holes above the doorframe. A dark smear on the step, scrubbed but not gone. Inside: air thick with old cigarette smoke and industrial solvent. Cafeteria. Pawnshop kiosk (shuttered). Room 1 (empty). The place where the case was solved and the blood was spilled.*

## Features

- Cafeteria — open, technically. Garte behind the counter.
- Room 1 — Where Harry Du Bois stayed. The amnesiac's base of operations.
- Pawnshop — closed since the Tribunal
- The yard — where the Tribunal confrontation occurred

## Inhabitants

- [[Garte]] — Manager, permanent resident, permanent grievance
- [[Mañana]] — Corner table, permanent presence

## Current Events

*Damaged, open, haunted by the week that changed everything.*
