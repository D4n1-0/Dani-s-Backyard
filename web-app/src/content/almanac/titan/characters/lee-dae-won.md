# Lee Dae-won

## Overview
Corporal, Republic of Korea Marine Corps. Wounded during the Titan capture on Paradis Island (Operation REEVE) when the target Titan's right arm struck him with a reflexive swing during the anchoring phase. Thrown six metres into a pine tree. Sustained two fractures in the left arm and two fractured ribs. Evacuated to the [[USS Tripoli (LHA-7)]]'s medical bay. First human casualty of AOT-ground contact.
