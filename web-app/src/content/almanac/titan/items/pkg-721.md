# PKG 721

## Overview
A Gumdoksuri-class fast patrol boat (PKG) of the Republic of Korea Navy, assigned to Task Group CATF-Alpha on the AOT side. Displacement 570 tons, crew of 27, armed with a single 76mm main gun.

Executed the first [[First Contact Protocol]] intercept — boarded the Marleyan polar expedition vessel *Pelikan* at 06:48 on Day 36, commanded by Lieutenant Cho.

## Features
- 76mm main gun in low-profile turret
- Surface search radar and EO/IR sensors
- 40-knot maximum speed (typically operated at reduced speed for acoustic minimisation)
- RHIB-capable for boarding operations
- Crew: 27

## Current Events
Operating from the [[Grande Terre Logistics Hub]] as a perimeter patrol vessel on regular rotation.
