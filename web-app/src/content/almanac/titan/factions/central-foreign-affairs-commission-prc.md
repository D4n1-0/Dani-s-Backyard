# Central Foreign Affairs Commission (PRC)

## Overview
The Party body that directs all foreign policy and national security decisions of the People's Republic of China. Supersedes the State Council's Foreign Ministry on strategic matters. Staffed by Party cadres, military officers, and intelligence personnel. The CFAC is the true locus of Chinese foreign policy power — the Ministry of Foreign Affairs implements decisions; the CFAC makes them.

## Structure
- Chairman (typically the General Secretary)
- Deputy Directors and Directors-General
- Office of the Central Foreign Affairs Commission (day-to-day operations)
- Direct coordination with PLA, MSS, and MFA

## Assets at [[The Gate]]
- [[Zheng Weimin]], Deputy Director, is chairing the military-civilian incident group.

## Relationships
- [[National Security Office (ROK)]] — counterpart; direct hotline connects the two offices
- [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs (PRC)]] — implements CFAC directives
- [[People's Liberation Army Navy]] — military component coordination

## Current Status
[[Zheng Weimin]] is managing the crisis from Beijing. The CFAC must balance the Party's instinct for control against the diplomatic reality that unilateral action would trigger international backlash — especially with the US Seventh Fleet en route.
