# The Gate

## Summary
An interdimensional portal connecting modern Earth to the Attack on Titan world, appearing on the [[Yellow Sea]] as a silent hard visual seam — 500m wide, 200m tall, bisecting the ocean at 100m below and 100m above sea level. Both sides are night — but the stars are wrong. The boundary is fully bidirectional: water, air, and matter pass freely in both directions.

## Details
The Gate opened on a Saturday evening before the Battle of Shiganshina, detected first by the [[Korea Coast Guard]] vessel [[KCG 506]]. The rupture is silent, without radiation or shockwave — a clean knife-cut between realities, like a hard video-game texture seam. No transition zone. No shimmer. Both sides are night — the same time of day — but the constellations on the far side belong to the southern celestial hemisphere. Different water, different air.

The Gate is fully bidirectional. Warm Yellow Sea water bleeds into the cold subantarctic ocean; antarctic air and seabirds cross freely to the Earth side. A mist forms along the seam where the two water bodies meet.

On the far side, the AOT world is a geographic inversion of Earth: the same landmasses and features, but north and south reversed. The AOT-side gate opens at approximately 46°25'S 51°45'E — roughly 20km from Île de la Possession in the Crozet Archipelago (which, in AOT-world inverted geography, sits in the northern hemisphere). This is a remote stretch of ocean thousands of kilometres from any inhabited landmass or trade route.

## Mechanism
Unknown. The Gate does not correspond to any known physics on either side. On the AOT side, it is distinct from Paths-based phenomena — Eldians do not sense it through their connection to the Coordinate. It is not Titan-made.

## Context
First secured by the [[Korea Coast Guard]] ([[KCG 506]], Lieutenant [[Park Do-hyun]]). [[China Coast Guard]] cutter [[Haijing 2306]] (Senior Captain [[Chen Shaoqiang]]) intercepted shortly after, producing a tense standoff before either side has crossed the threshold. Both vessels are holding position under orders from their respective commands. The AOT world remains unaware of the Gate's existence.
