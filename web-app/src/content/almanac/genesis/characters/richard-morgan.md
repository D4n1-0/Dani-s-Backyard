# Richard Morgan

## Overview
Captain of *[[HMS Herald|HMS Herald]]*, a fast cruiser of the [[Crown of England]]. Lean, grey-haired, with a face permanently set in the expression of a man who has seen too much and trusted too little. A career officer who rose through the Faithful fleet over three decades of war — cautious, sceptical, slow to accept what he cannot verify.

## Personality
[[Richard Morgan|Morgan]] is the sceptic in the Faithful command. He does not dismiss faith — he is a faithful man, a communicant of the Church, a soldier who has blessed his sword before every battle — but he does not extend faith to strangers. The modern world, in his assessment, is a power whose intentions are unproven and whose capabilities are dangerous. The collision with *[[HMS Resolute|HMS Resolute]]* confirmed his caution: the moderns are not enemies, but they are not yet allies either, and the speed with which the King has embraced them is faster than prudence permits.

## Role in the Council
During the post-collision councils, [[Richard Morgan|Morgan]] argued consistently against uncritical trust. He sees the moderns' power — the submarine detected and destroyed in thirty-one seconds — as a warning, not a miracle. He is overruled by the King, but his objections are noted, and his silence afterward is heavier than his words.

## Relationships
- [[Alistair Pembroke]] — respects Pembroke's judgment, even when it differs from his own
- [[King Robert the Longsword]] — loyal to the Crown, but troubled by the speed of the King's embrace of the moderns
- [[Father Beauchamp]] — respects Beauchamp's theology but finds it too optimistic
