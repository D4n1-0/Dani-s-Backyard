# Tone & Themes

## Core Tone

**Geo-political paranoia with dieselpunk aesthetics.** The Sundered World is not post-apocalyptic — civilisation rebuilt, cities thrive, nations wage war. But every factory, every army, every census is conducted under an invisible ceiling. The tone is one of *managed tension*: a world that has adapted to perpetual surveillance and calibrated its ambitions accordingly.

## Primary Themes

### The Cage of Knowledge
The central horror of the setting is not tyranny but *enforced ignorance*. Kaelen does not rule the Scattered Realms; it merely polices their ceiling. The Mandate bans not just weapons but *understanding*. Science is a haunted pursuit. Mathematics is whispered. This creates a society where intelligence is dangerous and the curious are watched.

### Asymmetric Power
Kaelen's dominance is absolute but *passive*. It does not extract tribute, demand worship, or install governors. It simply watches and punishes violations. This creates a unique political dynamic: the Scattered Realms are free to govern themselves, wage war, build empires — as long as they stay within the lines. The question of *why* Kaelen chose this arrangement is the setting's central mystery.

### Adaptation Under Constraint
The major factions are not good or evil but *different answers to the same question*: how do you build a civilisation when someone else has set a hard ceiling on your ambition? Valdris chose diplomacy and precise compliance. The Concordat chose martial excellence and grey-zone exploitation. The Last Flame chose theological surrender. The Technate chose hidden defiance. Each is a valid strategy; none is comfortable.

### The Cost of Obedience
The Mandate's caps are brutal in their specificity. Cities of 999,000 souls live with the knowledge that one more birth means erasure. The [[The Census|census]] is a ritual of terror. Infanticide is a grim policy tool. The setting asks: what does a society sacrifice to stay within the lines?

### Secrets as Currency
In a world where open research is forbidden, knowledge becomes contraband. The [[The Thought Cabinet|Thought Cabinet]]'s hand-copied circuit diagrams, the [[The Signal Seekers|Signal Seekers]]' recorded melodies, the [[The Unbound|Unbound]]'s smuggled magnetron schematic — these are the setting's true treasures. A single page of pre-Convergence physics could shift the balance of power.

## Aesthetic

- **Technology:** Diesel-age iron and steam, brass telegraphs, vacuum-tube radios, riveted armour, biplane wings, airship envelopes. Everything feels heavy, mechanical, tactile.
- **Surveillance:** The Kaelen drones are matte-grey and silent — alien yet recognisable. Satellites streak across the night sky in geometric constellations. Observers appear like polished ghosts.
- **Geography:** Vast oceans, continental interiors, mountain catacomb-cities, vitrified craters. The world is immense and Kaelen, at its centre, is the permanent unseen audience.
- **Sound:** Crackling radio, airship engines, howitzer thunder, telegraph keys, jazz broadcasts, the seven-note [[The Kaelic Melody|Kaelic Melody]].

## Narrative Approach

Stories in the Sundered World should feel like Cold War thrillers set in 1935. Diplomats negotiate under drone shadows. Spies trade in forbidden physics. Admirals dream of dreadnoughts they cannot build. Scientists work in catacombs, passing knowledge like a holy succession. And somewhere in the centre of the ocean, the Island watches.
